# SREP 0 — Add homogeneous and height media, UsdVol volumes and null-scattering transport

## Abstract

[SREP 12](srep-0012.md) names, as its A2 building block, "homogeneous, height and grid media from
OpenVDB/NanoVDB assets and UsdVol volumes; blackbody emission; null-scattering transport for the path tracer
(Miller et al. 2019); froxels and ray marching in raster", and records what landed: grid volumes from SRVOL
and OpenVDB assets with blackbody emission and multiple scattering ([SREP 40](srep-0040.md),
[SREP 79](srep-0079.md)); "height media, UsdVol and null-scattering transport are open" (line 272). This SREP
is the open part. A `medium` gains a `source`: the volume asset (today), or a homogeneous box or sphere, or a
height medium falling off above a base, with no asset. A volume asset gains the `usdvol` format: a UsdVol
`Volume` prim whose field relationships name the OpenVDB grids, read by the existing decoder. The path tracer
gains `transport="tracking"`: a majorant per domain, delta tracking for collisions and ratio tracking for
transmittance, unbiased and driven by the seeded streams of [SREP 26](srep-0026.md) so a frame alone equals
the frame played. The raster renderer gains froxels: exponential depth slices integrated front to back, which
converge to the path tracer as the slices rise. The bars of the plan are measured by
`conformance/tools/volumes_reference.py`: Beer–Lambert exact on the height medium's closed form, ratio
tracking unbiased against it, the white furnace keeping all of its energy at albedo 1, and froxels converging
to the exact transmittance.

## Motivation

- **Shot 31** (SREP 12 line 619, a capsule's plasma sheath and bow shock) is a glowing medium shaped by hand,
  not baked from a simulation: a sphere or box of homogeneous density with emission, placed and animated like
  any object. Today a medium needs a volume asset or a pyro simulation (VOL1, `schema/scene-render.sch:513`).
- **Height fog** is the one medium every scene uses: a fog that thins with height, over the whole scene, with
  an exact optical depth along any ray. SREP 40's grids cannot express an unbounded medium.
- **UsdVol** is how volumes travel between tools: a `Volume` prim whose `field:density` names an
  `OpenVDBAsset` with a file and a grid. The engine reads OpenVDB (`crates/sr-volume/src/openvdb`); the prim
  that points at it is a text layer away.
- **Null scattering.** SREP 40's path tracer marches media with a fixed step (`crates/sr-volume/src/medium.rs`,
  `integrate`; `crates/sr-gpu/src/volume.wgsl`, `volume_transport`): a biased estimator whose bias falls with
  `stepSize` and whose cost rises with it. Delta and ratio tracking (Novák et al. 2018; Miller et al. 2019)
  are unbiased for any step, with a cost set by the majorant; they are the estimators a path tracer is
  measured against, and they make the white-furnace test exact.
- **Raster.** A raster camera with media today renders through the shared transport pipeline with path-traced
  settings (SREP 40, "Passes containing volumes use the shared transport pipeline"). Froxels (Hillaire 2015)
  are the raster method: one lit, integrated volume per frame, read by every surface, converging to the path
  tracer as the slice count rises, which is the plan's bar.

### Semantics

The reference implementation is `conformance/tools/volumes_reference.py`; the names in parentheses are its
functions. Points are in the object's local space for sources and in scene space for rays; a ray is an origin,
a unit direction and the parameter range `[t0, t1)` of the segment inside the domain.

1. **Homogeneous media** (`homogeneous_density`). `source="box"` has density 1 where `|x| ≤ width/2`,
   `|y| ≤ height/2`, `|z| ≤ depth/2` and 0 elsewhere; `source="sphere"` where `|p| ≤ radius`. The domain of
   SREP 40 (its interval along a ray, its bounds for light grids) is the box or the sphere's bounding box.
2. **Height media** (`height_density`, `height_field`). `source="height"` has density
   `exp(−max(0, y − base)/falloff)` inside the object's box, `base` defaulting to the box's bottom. Its
   optical depth along a segment is exact (`height_optical_depth`): the length below the base plus
   `falloff/dy · (e^{−(y₀−base)/falloff} − e^{−(y₁−base)/falloff})` above it (`dy` the direction's y per unit
   length; the horizontal case is the density times the length), times `extinction`. A renderer MAY use the
   closed form for transmittance; the midpoint march (`march_optical_depth`) converges to it with order 2.
3. **Beer–Lambert** (`transmittance`). Transmittance is `exp(−∫σ_t)`, the march of SREP 40 for the march,
   the closed form or an unbiased estimate (rule 7) otherwise. `densityScale`, `extinction`, `albedo`,
   `anisotropy`, emission, `scatterBounces` and the rest of SREP 40 and SREP 79 apply to every source.
4. **UsdVol** (`usdvol_fields`). A `usdvol` asset is a usda text layer (`#usda 1.0`); `prim` names its
   `Volume` prim, else the layer's `defaultPrim` when that is a `Volume`, else the first `Volume`. Each
   `rel field:<channel> = <path>` of the prim names an `OpenVDBAsset` prim whose `asset filePath` and
   `token fieldName` (and `int fieldIndex`, 0 when absent) give the OpenVDB file and grid of that channel;
   `densityGrid`, `temperatureGrid` and `velocityGridX/Y/Z` name channels of the prim, not grids of the
   file. A missing prim or field, a `Field3DAsset` or another field type, or a field without `filePath` and
   `fieldName` is a validator error (`E2`); the file is then read as `format="openvdb"` (SREP 40), its
   relative path resolved against the layer. Binary layers (usdc) and time samples are open issues.
5. **The draws** (`draws`). A ray's draws of purpose `p` at sample `s` of pixel `x` are
   `S(e, p, s, x·2¹⁶ + i)`, `i = 0, 1, …`, with `e = E(project, id, seed, "transport")` the medium's
   element seed (D24 section 4, the random-streams draft section 5), purposes `P_DISTANCE` 0 and `P_PHASE`
   1; a ray draws at most 2¹⁶ times per purpose (`E3`). So a sample of a pixel at a frame is a function of
   the document and the frame.
6. **The majorant** (`majorant`). A domain's majorant `σ̄` is its largest density (the maximum over its
   bricks or 1 for a box, sphere or height source) times `densityScale` and `extinction`; a renderer MAY
   use a tighter local majorant per brick. A density whose extinction exceeds the majorant is an error
   (`E4`), never clamped silently.
7. **Tracking** (`delta_tracking`, `ratio_tracking`). With `transport="tracking"`, tentative collisions
   along a segment are `−ln(1 − u)/σ̄` apart. *Delta tracking* accepts a tentative collision as real with
   probability `σ_t(x)/σ̄` (a second draw) and returns its distance, or none when the segment ends first; the
   real collision scatters, emits and continues the walk of SREP 79. *Ratio tracking* multiplies a weight by
   `1 − σ_t(x)/σ̄` at every tentative collision and returns it at the segment's end; its expectation is the
   transmittance exactly (the numeric table: means 0.2525 and 0.231133 against `e^{−1.4}` = 0.246597 over
   400 seeded rays at majorants 1× and 2×, and 0.219881 against 0.205217 on the height medium). More than
   `maxSteps` tentative collisions on one ray is an error (`E5`), as a march over budget is today.
8. **The phase function** (`phase_hg`, `sample_hg`, `scatter_direction`). Henyey–Greenstein with
   `anisotropy` g, normalised over the sphere; sampled by `cos θ = 1 − 2u₁` for g = 0 and
   `(1 + g² − ((1 − g²)/(1 − g + 2gu₁))²)/(2g)` otherwise, `φ = 2πu₂`, about the incoming direction.
9. **The walk and the white furnace** (`furnace_walk`, `furnace_mean`). A path in a medium collides by rule
   7, multiplies its throughput by `albedo`, turns by rule 8, and leaves after at most `scatterBounces`
   collisions (cut to zero beyond, as SREP 79). In a furnace of radiance 1 with albedo 1 every escaping path
   carries exactly 1 (the table's 50 walks), and with albedo `a` exactly `a^bounces`; the 300-walk mean on a
   unit sphere of unit extinction is 1.0. This is the energy test of the plan.
10. **Froxels** (`froxel_depths`, `froxel_index`, `froxel_integrate`, `froxel_transmittance`). With
    `rasterTransport="froxels"`, the raster renderer cuts the view frustum into `froxelSlices` depth slices at
    `near·(far/near)^{k/n}`, samples every medium's extinction and source (emission plus single scattering lit
    as SREP 40) at each slice's midpoint along the pixel's ray, and composes the slices front to back:
    radiance `+= T·(1 − e^{−σΔ})/σ · source` (`Δ` for σ = 0) and `T *= e^{−σΔ}` (the weights of SREP 79 rule
    4). A surface at depth `z` reads the radiance and transmittance of the slices before it. The result
    converges to the exact transport as `n` rises (table: errors 0.002335, 0.000146, 9·10⁻⁶ at 8, 32, 128
    slices on the height medium) and so to the path tracer's (the raster-against-tracking row, within 0.03 of
    400 tracked rays at 256 slices).
11. **Determinism and bounds.** Sources and froxels are functions of the document and the frame; tracking
    is a function of the frame, the pixel, the sample and the element seed through rule 5, so the seek check
    (SREP 14 rule 5) holds and two engines draw the same tentative collisions. At most 64 domains per pass
    (SREP 40), 32 bounces (SREP 79), 1024 slices, 2¹⁶ draws and `maxSteps` ≤ 65536 collisions per ray.

## Rationale

- **Sources on `medium`, shapes from the object.** The object already has `width`, `height`, `depth` and
  `radius` for its primitives; a medium that reads them needs no new element, and VOL1 keeps "exactly one
  source".
- **A height medium with a closed-form depth** gives the plan's "Beer–Lambert exact" test a non-trivial
  oracle and lets a renderer skip the march for fog.
- **UsdVol as a pointer, not a decoder.** The subset read is the relationship and the two attributes every
  exporter writes; the voxels come through the OpenVDB reader that exists.
- **Tracking as an opt-in.** `transport="march"` stays the default so every SREP 40 frame renders the same;
  `tracking` is the unbiased estimator for the scenes that need the furnace to close.
- **Draws by (purpose, sample, pixel, index)** make the estimator deterministic across seeks and engines, as
  SREP 26 does for every other random effect; the pixel index block of 2¹⁶ bounds the draws and the
  collisions together.
- **Froxels** are the raster method that reads once per surface and converges; the plan's bar is convergence
  to the path tracer, which rule 10 measures.

## Rejected alternatives

- **NanoVDB as a second file format**: the plan allows it; the engine's OpenVDB reader already builds the
  sparse bricks it needs, and NanoVDB's layout is a cache of the same tree. An open issue.
- **Spectral or chromatic majorants** (per-channel tracking): coloured `extinction` is not in SREP 40
  (scalar extinction, RGB albedo); the hero-wavelength extension is a later SREP.
- **Residual ratio tracking** (a control variate): lower variance, more rules; the plain estimators are
  stated so engines can agree, and a renderer MAY use a better unbiased one.
- **Half-resolution raster marching without froxels**: cheaper, but every surface marches again and nothing
  converges by one knob.

## Backwards compatibility

New attributes with the existing behaviour as their defaults and one revised rule (VOL1, which accepts what
it accepted), gated by V17; the `usdvol` format is a new enumeration value. Every 1.7 document renders the
same.

### Kit cases

The cases are described here and added to `conformance/srep_cases/` once the SREP has a number; they are not
measurable on an engine before SR-78 lands.

| Case | Checks | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `srep-<n>-medium-box.xml` | a unit box medium of `extinction="2"` in front of a white backdrop, camera looking through its full depth: the pixel is `exp(−2)` within 10⁻³ in the path tracer with `transport` march and tracking, and in the raster renderer with froxels at 64 slices | to be measured |
| `srep-<n>-medium-height.xml` | a height medium (`falloff="1"`, base 0) seen along a ray from (0, 0, 0) to (4, 3, 0): transmittance equals the closed form within 10⁻³ (march, `stepSize` 0.01) and within 0.02 (tracking, 256 samples) | to be measured |
| `srep-<n>-usdvol.xml` | a `usdvol` asset pointing at SREP 40's smoke VDB beside the same file as `format="openvdb"`: the two frames are identical | to be measured |
| `srep-<n>-tracking-plume.xml` | SREP 40's plume with `transport="tracking"` at 256 samples against the march at `stepSize` 0.05: PSNR ≥ 40 dB over the plume | to be measured |
| `srep-<n>-white-furnace.xml` | a unit sphere medium of albedo 1, no absorption, `scatterBounces="32"`, inside a uniform white dome of radiance 1 with `transport="tracking"`: every pixel within 0.02 of 1 at 1024 samples | to be measured |
| `srep-<n>-froxels.xml` | the plume with a raster camera and `rasterTransport="froxels"` at 32 and 256 slices against the path tracer: the mean absolute difference falls with the slices and is below 0.01 at 256 | to be measured |
