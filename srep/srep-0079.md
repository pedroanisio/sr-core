# SREP 79 — Add multiple scattering in media and foam albedo

## Abstract

`medium` gains `scatterBounces`, a whole number from 0 to 32 with default 1. It is the most collisions a light path
may have in the medium. 1 is today's single scattering. 0 turns scattering off. Values above 1 add the light scattered
more than once, which a renderer estimates by a random walk. `whitewater` gains `foamMode` (`particles`, the default
and today's behaviour, or `albedo`), `foamRadius`, `foamAlbedo` (0.9) and `foamRoughness` (0.8). In `albedo` mode the
foam tracers are not drawn as triangles. They become a per-vertex coverage of the ocean surface, and a path-traced
sample on covered water sees a white diffuse surface with the probability of that coverage. One Schematron rule
(OCN14) and four validator warnings (W06, W07, W08, W10) come with them. A raster renderer refuses `foamMode="albedo"`
with an error.

## Motivation

This is one of six SREPs (76 to 81) that take upstream what the reference engine's vendored schema has and sr-core 1.5.0 lacks, so that the engine can vendor sr-core's XSD and Schematron byte for byte again. `conformance/tools/engine_gap.py` measures the gap between sr-core at tag schema-1.5.0 and the engine at commit 4bf7a9e (whose `schema/UPSTREAM` cites schema-1.5.0 at 3f14a20): every element, attribute, enumeration value, documentation block and Schematron pattern, rule, let and assertion the engine has and sr-core lacks or words differently, 222 items, with the engine commit that introduced each one, in `conformance/tools/engine-gap-1.5.0.json`. None of them belongs to SREPs 66 to 75 (the tool checks their declarations and rule ids), the engine has nothing that sr-core has and it lacks, and no new rule id collides with an existing one. This SREP takes 13 of them: 12 XSD items added and 0 changed, 1 Schematron items added and 0 changed.

**Multiple scattering.** SREP 40 defines media as "a cinematic single-scattering model; it does not claim
multiple-scattering convergence" (SREP 40, line 375; the same text is in the engine's draft, srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:384-385).
Single scattering loses most of the light of a bright, optically thick medium. The engine measured this on a
homogeneous slab of optical thickness 2, albedo 1, sun cosine 0.8 and view cosine 0.7, against an independent solution
of the transport integral equation (`tools/volume_slab_reference.py`, commit a7cf5bd):

| Collisions | Albedo | Reflected BRF (reference) | Test tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 0 | 0.0001 |
| 1 (single scattering, today) | 1 | 0.16588 | 0.001 |
| 32 | 1 | 0.56361 | 0.035 |
| 32 | 0.8 | 0.29005 | 0.025 |

Source: `crates/sr-gpu/tests/volume_grids/volume.rs:1049-1125`. Single scattering gives 0.16588 / 0.56361 = 29 % of
the reflected light of the white slab. The commit message of a7cf5bd gives the tool's numbers: 0.5636 and 0.3656 for
the slab at albedo 1 (the estimator's model: 0.5635 and 0.3661), 0.1659 for the first order.

**Foam in the albedo.** SREP 40 draws each foam tracer as an 8-triangle horizontal disc (SREP 40, line 1370;
srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1611-1613). The engine's history does not name a scene that asked for a continuous foam cover (see
Unverified). What it does record:

- A disc costs 9 × 256 + 24 × 8 bytes of the surface budget (`crates/sr-eval/src/ocean/whitewater.rs:12`). At the
  default `maxParticles` of 10,000 that is about 9.6 MB (commit 8e27af14).
- Foam in `albedo` mode is the water's own surface, so it takes the surface's light and shadow. Under clear water with
  foam on its left half, the floor is 0.051 next to the foam against 0.297 without it (commit 4dee96b1, NVIDIA,
  256 samples).

### Semantics

#### `medium/@scatterBounces`

1. **Meaning.** `scatterBounces` = L is the most collisions a path may have in media between its light source and the
   point the path tracer is shading. The collision that is lit directly (by next-event estimation) counts. L = 1 is
   single scattering. L = 0 is a medium that absorbs and emits but does not scatter. L > 1 adds the scattering orders
   2 to L. Sources: the XSD documentation in this SREP's diff; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:341.
2. **L = 0.** A renderer MUST add no in-scattered light for that medium. Its extinction and emission are unchanged.
   Reference: `crates/sr-gpu/src/pathtrace.rs:1258-1259` (the in-scattering term needs `scatterBounces > 0`) and the
   CPU integrator `crates/sr-volume/src/medium.rs:460`.
3. **L = 1.** A renderer MUST render what it renders without the attribute. The reference compiles the walk only when
   some medium of the scene has L ≠ 1 (`pathtrace.rs:1237-1246`); with every L = 1 the shader text is unchanged
   (test `pathtrace.rs:683-716`).
4. **The first order.** The first collision is the existing single-scattering march (SREP 40). Each march step k of
   length Δ at midpoint x_k, with transmittance T_k before the step, gives
   - σ_t(x) = Σ_m ρ_m(x) ε_m (density times `extinction`, summed over overlapping media),
   - w_k = (1 − e^(−σ_t Δ)) / σ_t,
   - S_k = Σ_m ρ_m E_m + Σ_{m: L_m > 0} ρ_m ε_m α_m L_d(x_k, ω_o),

   where α_m is the RGB `albedo`, E_m the emission and L_d the light arriving directly at x_k, weighted by the
   Henyey–Greenstein phase function of `anisotropy` g (`crates/sr-gpu/src/volume.wgsl:120-123, 183-210, 213-251`).
5. **Where the walk starts.** For L > 1 a renderer MUST add, in expectation, the light scattered 2 to L times. The
   reference does it with one walk per marched segment (`pathtrace.rs:1256-1270`; every path segment through media is
   marched, `pathtrace.wgsl:609-612`):
   - Each step k and each medium m with L_m > 1 is a candidate with RGB weight c = T_k w_k ρ_m ε_m α_m and scalar mass
     μ = max(c_r, c_g, c_b) (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:11-26`).
   - One candidate is kept by weighted reservoir sampling, with probability μ / Σμ (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:18-19`).
   - The walk's throughput starts at β = c · Σμ / μ_kept (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:96`). Dividing by the scalar
     probability of the largest channel keeps coloured media unbiased (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:2-3`).
6. **A step of the walk.** From collision j − 1 at x with outgoing direction ω_o:
   - Direction: the new incoming direction is sampled from Henyey–Greenstein with the medium's g, by inversion:
     u ~ U[0,1); cos θ = 1 − 2u if |g| < 10⁻³, else s = (1 − g²)/(1 − g + 2gu) and
     cos θ = clamp((1 + g² − s²)/(2g), −1, 1); azimuth 2π·u′ (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:28-39`). The phase function and
     its pdf cancel, so β does not change.
   - Flight: τ = −ln(1 − u), and the collision is where the optical depth along the ray reaches τ. The optical depth
     is that of the same piecewise-constant quadrature the march uses: steps no longer than the smallest `stepSize`,
     split at domain boundaries (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:41-92`). The flight ends at the first surface
     (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:44`).
   - Emission met along the flight is added, times β (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:68, 102`).
   - If the flight leaves the media or reaches a surface, the walk ends (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:103`).
   - Otherwise the collision is in medium m′, chosen with probability ρ_m′ ε_m′ / σ_t (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:69-83`).
   - The limit becomes L ← min(L, L_m′), and the walk ends if j > L (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:105-106`).
   - β ← β · α_m′ · e^(−σ_w s), where s is the flight length and σ_w the absorption of transmissive water the segment
     is inside, or 0 (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:107`; `pathtrace.rs:1269-1270`).
   - The walk ends if β is zero in every channel (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:108`).
   - Collision j is lit directly: C += β · L_d(x_j, ω_o′) with the same direct-light function as the first order
     (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:109`; `pathtrace.rs:1271-1282`). With `lighting="grid"` and x_j on the lattice, that
     function reads the light grids (`volume_grid.wgsl:125-126`).
7. **Bounds.** The walk has at most min(32, L) collisions, counting the first (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:98`). It has no
   Russian roulette. A renderer MUST NOT add more than L collisions. A renderer MAY end walks at surfaces, as the
   reference does: "its indirect walk terminates at surfaces rather than adding surface-volume interreflection"
   (`RELEASE_NOTES.md:18`).
8. **Extinction.** `scatterBounces` MUST NOT change the transmittance or the alpha of the medium (test
   `volume_grids/volume.rs:1122-1123`).
9. **Renderers.** Passes with volumes use the transport pipeline even when the camera asks for raster rendering
   (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:390-393), so the attribute applies to both camera kinds. The reference's CPU integrator evaluates the
   first order only (`crates/sr-volume/src/medium.rs:69-70`).
10. **Randomness.** The reference draws from the path tracer's per-pixel, per-sample PCG stream, seeded by
    `pcg(pixel × 9781 + pcg(sample × 6271 + 1))` (`pathtrace.wgsl:151-160, 776`). The SREP does not fix the sampler.
    The result is a Monte Carlo estimate, and conformance is judged against the reference within a tolerance.

#### `whitewater/@foamMode` and the foam coverage

11. **`particles`** (default). Foam tracers are drawn as triangle batches with `foamMaterial`, as in SREP 40.
    `foamRadius`, `foamAlbedo` and `foamRoughness` are ignored (XSD documentation in the diff).
12. **`albedo`.** No foam triangles are built, and `foamMaterial` is unused (`ocean.rs:459`; `whitewater.rs:4-15`).
    The tracers, their simulation and its budgets do not depend on the mode (commit 10263d60).
13. **Coverage kernel.** At the whitewater frame's time t, with radius r = `foamRadius`, or the ocean's `cellSize`
    when it is absent (`ocean.rs:222`), every surface vertex v gets a share

    c(v) = 1 − Π_p (1 − f_p · K(d_p(v) / r)),

    where:
    - K(x) = (1 − x²)² for x < 1, and 0 for x ≥ 1 (`foam.rs:154-158`). K is not normalised: its peak is 1.
    - d_p(v) is the horizontal distance between the tracer and the vertex, in the ocean's x and z. Height does not
      count (`foam.rs:154-156`; test `foam.rs:196-208`).
    - a_p = (t − birth) / lifetime; f_p = 1 for a_p ≤ 0.6, else (1 − a_p) / 0.4 (`foam.rs:9, 147-148`).
    - Only foam tracers with 0 ≤ a_p < 1 and finite x and z take part. Spray covers nothing (`foam.rs:126-131`;
      srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1624-1626).
    - The product is taken in tracer order, so the same tracers give the same bits (`foam.rs:95-99`).

    The reference computes c in f64 and stores it as f32 in the alpha of each vertex colour (`foam.rs:165`;
    `ocean.rs:454-456`). Within a triangle, c is interpolated barycentrically (`pathtrace_water.wgsl:38-39`).
14. **When the coverage is made.** The reference makes it only when the water takes foam (rule 17) and some camera of
    the composition has `renderer="pathtrace"`. Otherwise no coverage is made, and no foam triangles are drawn either
    (`ocean.rs:226-238, 448-459`; commit e9acb4f3).
15. **Shading.** c is the share of the surface's area that foam covers (`pathtrace.rs:1143-1147`). At a hit with
    share c, a sample MUST be on foam with probability c and on the water otherwise, so the expected picture is
    (1 − c) · water + c · foam (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1636-1642).
    - The foam surface is a non-metal diffuse grey of albedo `foamAlbedo` and roughness `foamRoughness`, with
      transmission 0. The reference uses α = max(foamRoughness², 10⁻³), F0 = ((n − 1)/(n + 1))² with the water's IOR
      n, and the water's specular weight (`pathtrace.rs:1179-1186`).
    - The water sample is the water's material, unchanged. Light through the uncovered part keeps the water's tint.
    - A share of 0 MUST give the unmixed water. The reference draws no random number there, and the picture is
      identical bit for bit (commit c92474e).
    - Metallic water is covered like any other water (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1668-1670).
16. **Shadow rays through water.** The transmittance of a refracted shadow ray through covered water is scaled by
    (1 − c), and the tint is left alone (`pathtrace_water.wgsl:36-41`; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1642-1646). This is the expected
    value of the per-sample choice, not a sample of it.
17. **Water that takes foam.** A renderer MUST refuse `albedo` foam on water whose material has `alphaMode` `mask` or
    `blend`, is `unlit`, or has emission (an `emissiveStrength` above 0 and an `emissive` colour that is not black)
    (`render_three.rs:1452-1471`; `crates/sr-model/src/foam.rs:7-22`).
18. **Informative (reference only):**
    - The lobe choice at a sample's first surface hit uses u = fract(h(pixel)/2³² + sample × 0.6180339887), where h
      is a PCG hash of the pixel. Later hits draw at random (`pathtrace.rs:1156-1162, 1176-1178`).
    - The denoiser's albedo guide is (1 − c) · base + c · `foamAlbedo` (`pathtrace.rs:1164-1169`).
    - Measured effect of the stratified choice (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1647-1657; commit 64939330): at 8 samples a pixel and
      c = 0.5, the standard deviation is 0.0155, against 0.0354 for a random draw.
19. **Budgets.** A renderer MUST bound the coverage's work and memory per frame. Over a bound, the frame MUST fail
    with an error that names the bound. It MUST NOT truncate the coverage. The reference's measures:
    - Work: the number of vertex distances. Vertices are binned in squares of side
      s = max(r, √(w_x w_z / n), max(w_x, w_z) / n), where n is the vertex count and w_x, w_z the surface's extent
      (`foam.rs:26-42`). Each living tracer costs the vertex count of the 3 × 3 squares around its own. The total is
      counted before any distance is taken, and over the whitewater's `maxWork` the frame fails (`foam.rs:132-145`).
      This budget is the coverage's own, not shared with the solver's (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1628-1629).
    - Memory: B = 4(squares + 1) + 16n bytes (`foam.rs:44-48`). B is charged against what remains of
      `surfaceMemoryMiB` after the surface (`ocean.rs:451-453`; `foam.rs:116-122`).
    - In `albedo` mode, only spray tracers are charged to the whitewater mesh budget (`whitewater.rs:10-15`; commit
      8e27af14).
20. **Spray.** Spray is drawn in both modes as before, with `sprayMaterial` or the default spray material
    (`whitewater.rs:10`; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1611-1616).

### Errors and warnings

- **OCN14** (Schematron, error): `foamRadius` ≤ 64 × `cellSize`, with `cellSize` defaulting to 1. The rule applies in
  both modes, so changing the mode cannot make a document invalid (diff; `rules.rs:1327-1333`; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1549).
- **W06** (warning): `foamMode="albedo"` with a `foamMaterial`, which is not used (`rules.rs:1336-1343`;
  `codes.rs:54-57`).
- **W07** (warning): `foamMode="albedo"` in a document where no `camera` has `renderer="pathtrace"`
  (`rules.rs:1344-1348, 1373-1379`; `codes.rs:63-67`).
- **W08** (warning): `foamMode="albedo"` on an ocean whose `material` names a material that fails rule 17. The
  material is read from the document's attributes at the start, so animated attributes are not seen
  (`rules.rs:1349-1372`; `codes.rs:58-62`; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1664-1665).
- **W10** (warning): a `medium` with `scatterBounces` > 1 and a black `albedo`. The reference detects black only in hex
  spelling: the first six digits after `#` are all 0 (`rules.rs:1802-1820`; `codes.rs:73-77`).
- **Runtime, raster:** a pass that is not path-traced and contains water with foam in `albedo` mode reports an error,
  and the 3D pass is not drawn. If the camera asked for the path tracer but it cannot render the pass, the error names
  that limit (`render_three.rs:2978-2982, 3156-3165`; commit 562d27d).
- **Runtime, water material:** a water that is not opaque reports an error, and the ocean is not drawn
  (`render_three.rs:1454-1461`). An unlit or emissive water does the same (`render_three.rs:1463-1470`; commit
  823a3d12).
- **Runtime, budgets:** "ocean foam coverage needs more than N distances (the whitewater's maxWork)" and "ocean foam
  coverage exceeds the surface memory budget (surfaceMemoryMiB)" (`foam.rs:118-121, 141-143`).

Schema errors (S06) cover a `scatterBounces` outside 0 to 32 or with a fraction, a `foamMode` outside the
enumeration, a `foamRadius` ≤ 0, and a `foamAlbedo` or `foamRoughness` outside [0, 1] (corpus documents
`vol-scatter-bounces-range`, `vol-scatter-bounces-fraction`, `whitewater-foam-mode`, `whitewater-foam-albedo-range`;
`tests/corpus/manifest.json`).

### Defaults and the neutral case

- `scatterBounces` absent or 1: single scattering as in SREP 40. The reference keeps the shader text and pipeline
  (`pathtrace.rs:1237-1246`, test at 683-716).
- `foamMode` absent or `particles`: the frame has the same vertices, foam batch and cache key as before
  (commit 10263d60; test `crates/sr-eval/tests/ocean_foam_albedo.rs:26`). The three other foam attributes are ignored.
  OCN14 still checks `foamRadius` when it is given.
- No new warning fires on a document that does not use a new attribute.

## Rationale

- **Coefficient and phase conventions** follow SREP 40, which follows PBRT's volume transport
  (https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Volume_Scattering/Transmittance, cited at srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:386). `anisotropy` is the
  Henyey–Greenstein parameter (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:330).
- **A count, not a switch.** One attribute covers no scattering (0), today's model (1) and a bounded walk (2 to 32).
  Its default is today's behaviour. The cap of 32 bounds the cost of a path. The white slab reaches its reference
  within 0.035 at 32 collisions (`volume_grids/volume.rs:1104`).
- **Per medium.** Scattering order is a property of the material being rendered, like `albedo`.
- **Foam as area coverage.** Choosing the foam or the water per sample keeps the picture linear in the share. It also
  keeps the water's tint in the light that passes through (commit c92474e; see Rejected alternatives).
- **A smooth kernel with a fade.** (1 − x²)² is 1 at the tracer and 0 at r. The fade over the last 40 % of life
  avoids pops at death (`foam.rs:8-9`). Combining by 1 − Π(1 − w) keeps c ≤ 1 and does not depend on how tracers
  overlap.
- No external precedent with a DOI or URL is cited by the engine for the random walk, the reservoir or the coverage
  kernel.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Foam as a continuous mix of albedo, transmission and roughness by the share** (first form, commit 4dee96b1).
  Rejected in c92474e:
  - Light through the water took the foam's colour. At a share of 0.5, a water of tint 0.1 gave 0.1245 against the
    mean 0.1173 of the bare and covered pictures.
  - The diffuse brightness went with the square of the share (0.0283, 0.0552, 0.1042 at 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, against
    0.0526, 0.0996, 0.1462).
  - The denoiser lost the edge of the foam.
- **A random lobe choice at the first hit.** At 8 samples and c = 0.5 it was 5.8 times as noisy as the continuous mix,
  and the denoised mean came out about 5 % low. Replaced by the stratified choice (commit 64939330).
- **Forcing the transmissive-water shader variant for foam on opaque water.** It cost 6.7 times the plain shader:
  21.25 s against 3.2 s for the GPU pass on the hero frame (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1677-1684).
- **Refusing metallic water.** The foam sample is a non-metal, so metallic water is accepted: a wholly covered
  metallic sea is 0.1926 against 0.1928 for the quadrature (commit 823a3d12).
- **Making and charging the coverage for every `albedo` ocean.** A large unlit sea then failed on the coverage budget
  before the renderer could report the real cause (commit e9acb4f3).
- **Charging foam discs in `albedo` mode** (commit 8e27af14).
- **OCN14 only in `albedo` mode.** It is checked in both modes, so that changing the mode cannot invalidate a document
  (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1549).
- **Sharing the coverage's work with the solver's `maxWork`** (the form of commit 9ccd258). The coverage now has a
  budget of its own, equal to `maxWork` per frame (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1628-1629).
- **`scatterBounces`:** none recorded in the engine's history. Multiple scattering inside the light grids was left
  out (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:518).

## Backwards compatibility

Every document valid under the previous version stays valid, because OCN14 only constrains the new `foamRadius`. Every
valid document renders as before, because both defaults are today's behaviour (Defaults and the neutral case). The
change is MINOR.

Version gate: as written, `medium` and `ocean` are SREP 40 elements whose rules require `scene/@version="1.3"` exactly
(V8 at `schema/scene-render-1.1.sch:509-510`, OCN1 at `:644`). The new attributes therefore inherit that gate. See
Open issues.

## Open issues

- **Version gate (all of SREPs 76 to 80).** The engine's text gates these features on `scene/@version="1.3"` exactly: BH1 and VOX1 directly, and the attributes on SREP 40 elements through SREP 40's own gates (V8 for volumes, media and pyro, CRT1, FRX1, P3D1, OCN1), which are already in sr-core 1.5.0 in that form. A document that uses them cannot declare 1.4, 1.5 or 1.6, so it cannot also use the features those versions gate (SREPs 57 to 75). SREPs 66 to 75 gate their features on 1.6 with an explicit list of the earlier versions. This draft keeps the engine's text verbatim, because the aim is a byte-equal vendoring; the review should decide whether the 1.3 gates become "1.3 or later" (a separate SREP that would also amend SREP 40). **Engine change:** yes, if the gates change: the engine mirrors every Schematron rule natively (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs`), so it must change those checks and its corpus, then re-vendor.

- **Version gate.** `medium` and `ocean` come from SREP 40 and are gated on `version="1.3"` exactly (V8, OCN1). This
  SREP targets schema 1.6, and SREPs 66 to 75 gate on 1.6. The XSD text is kept verbatim: `scatterBounces` says
  "Version 1.3." and has no gate of its own. The editor should decide whether the new attributes need a 1.6 gate, and
  how the exact-1.3 gates of SREP 40 relate to 1.6 documents.
- **Two SREPs.** The two features share no element, no rule and no code path, except that both are path-tracer
  shading on SREP 40 elements. They landed on different branches (52ab3c3, a590300) and could be accepted or rejected
  separately. Recommendation: split into "medium/@scatterBounces" (with W10) and "whitewater foam in the water's
  albedo" (with OCN14, W06, W07, W08). This draft keeps them together as asked.
- **The XSD documentation is narrower than the code.** The `foamMode` documentation names only the opaque alpha mode.
  The reference also refuses unlit and emissive water (`render_three.rs:1463-1470`; W08; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:1670-1673).
  Rule 17 follows the code. The documentation should say "an opaque, lit material without emission".
- **The engine's docs lag the code.**
  - srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:384-385 still says the media are "a cinematic single-scattering model".
  - The `mediumType` inventory at srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3561-3578 omits `scatterBounces`.
  - The engine draft has no text on the estimator. Rules 4 to 10 are derived from the code alone.
  - The module comment `crates/sr-eval/src/ocean/foam.rs:1-3` still describes the superseded continuous mix ("mixes
    the water's albedo, transmission and roughness toward the foam's").
- **Overlapping media.** The walk's limit is the minimum `scatterBounces` of the medium it starts in and of every
  medium it collides in (`pathtrace_scatter.wgsl:98, 105`). The XSD's "in the medium" does not say this. Decide
  whether it is normative.
- **Walks end at surfaces** (`RELEASE_NOTES.md:18`), and the dome reaches walk vertices only through next-event
  estimation. Rule 7 permits this. Decide whether a renderer that adds surface–volume interreflection conforms.
- **W10 spelling.** W10 recognises black only as hex. The `colorType` channel form ("0,0,0") and `var(--…)` tokens are
  not detected (`rules.rs:1804-1808`; XSD `colorType`).
- **W08 spelling.** The shared criterion treats any `emissive` spelling other than hex or `black` as shining
  (`crates/sr-model/src/foam.rs:9-10, 24-37`). W08 can therefore warn for a black channel-form emission that the
  renderer accepts (`render_three.rs:1452-1453` uses the parsed colour).
- **Budget measures are implementation-defined.** The work measure depends on the reference's binning. Another
  engine would fail at a different threshold. Decide whether the SREP fixes the measure or only requires a bound and
  a named error (rule 19 does the latter).

- What 0.3656 measures in commit a7cf5bd. Transmitted radiance is likely but not stated.
- Whether the raster refusal makes the whole render job fail or only records an error in the frame's statistics.
