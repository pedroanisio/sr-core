# SREP 0 — Add the terrain grid

## Abstract

SREP 12's F2, the foundation of milestone 8: one `terrain` element holding a regular grid of layers over a
rectangle. The grid has `cells` along the longer side and the other side by the aspect, each cell
`metersPerCell` wide; every layer, mask and process shares that one grid resolution (the SREP 12 open issue,
decided here: no nested grids, finer detail is a second element). Layers are named kinds with units: `bedrock`,
`loose`, `lava`, `ice` and `snow` by type in metres and solid; `water` and `sediment` in metres; `flow` in
m²/s with two channels; `temperature` in kelvin; `fuel` in kg/m²; `burn`, `hardness` and `wetness` in [0, 1].
The ground surface is the sum of the solid layers and the water surface adds `water`. A layer is a flat
`value`, a `generate` child (`fbm`, `ridged` or `billow` noise on the D24 lattice of the random-streams draft,
with `octaves`, `frequency`, `lacunarity`, `gain`, a domain `warp`, `terraces` with a `terraceSharpness`, and
`amplitude` and `offset` in the layer's unit), or an image or elevation asset (`from` with an `encoding` of
`gray`, `gray16`, `terrarium` or `mapbox`). `mask` children measure the ground surface (`slope` in degrees,
`altitude` in metres, `curvature` as the Laplacian, `flow` as the log10 of the D8 accumulation) and ramp over
`min` and `max` with a `feather`; `material` children splat over the altitude ramp through them. `display`
draws the rectangle as a `picture` (the baseline erosion's hill-shaded ramp, `colorLow` to `colorHigh`,
`sunAzimuth`, `sunElevation`, `relief`), or an `object3D primitive="terrain"` draws the grid as SREP 70's
`heightfield` over its physical extent, or `none` keeps the grid for processes only. The same document
generates byte-identical layers, pinned by their `sha256` digest (SREP 14). Rules TG1–TG6 and V17; the
schema files are unchanged, the diff is inside the draft.

## Motivation

SREP 12 row F2 asks for "the element, layers, units, generation (fBm, ridged, warp, terraces, from DEM or
image), masks, 3D and 2D display, material splatting by slope, altitude, curvature and flow", with the bar
"parity with Gaea 2, World Machine, World Creator and Houdini heightfields in primitives and masks; terrain as
a 3D object and a 2D picture from one element". At 67a661a terrain exists three times and never as data:
`<erosion>` builds a fractal value-noise height in [0, 1] and draws it shaded in 2D (`erosion.rs`), `object3D
primitive="map"` raises a map by elevation tiles (SREP 10), and `heightfield` is an expression surface
(SREP 70); craters (SREPs 40, 77) carve their own ground. None has masks, material, units or more than one
layer, and the processes of milestone 8 (erosion T1, surface water T2, snow and ice T3, lava T4, wildfire T5)
need a grid they can read and write together. This draft is that grid; it moves the noise onto the D24 lattice
so that SREP 14's rule 2 holds (every procedural value from the lattice hash) and the layers reproduce
anywhere.

## Specification

### Syntax

The diff against `scene-render.xsd` 1.7.1 and `scene-render.sch`; the schema files are unchanged in this
draft, the editor applies the diff on acceptance.

**`terrainType`**, a new node type offered wherever `erosion` is (`scene-render.xsd:5376`), with the
`nodeBehaviour` group and these attributes:

| Attribute | Type | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | `xs:ID` | required | referenced by `object3D/@terrain` and by the processes |
| `x`, `y` | `xs:double` | 0 | the rectangle's top-left corner in the 2D scene |
| `width`, `height` | `positiveDecimal` | required | the rectangle in scene units |
| `cells` | `xs:int` | 256 | cells along the longer side, 4 to 4096 (TG2); the other side by the aspect, at least 4 |
| `metersPerCell` | `positiveDecimal` | 1 | the physical cell size (TG2) |
| `seed` | `xs:unsignedLong` | — | part of the element seed (random-streams draft section 3) |
| `display` | `picture` \| `surface` \| `none` | `picture` | the 2D picture, the 3D surface through an `object3D`, or data only (TG6) |
| `sunAzimuth`, `sunElevation`, `relief`, `colorLow`, `colorHigh`, `blend`, `effects` | as on `erosionType` | 315, 45, 1, `#3B4A2FFF`, `#E8E2D0FF` | the picture's shading and ramp |

Children, in order: `layer` (one or more), `mask` (any number), `material` (any number).

**`layer`**: `kind` (required; one of `bedrock`, `loose`, `lava`, `ice`, `snow`, `water`, `sediment`, `flow`,
`temperature`, `fuel`, `burn`, `hardness`, `wetness`), `type` (`xs:string`, snow layers only, so that several
snow types coexist), and exactly one source (TG3): `value` (`xs:double`, a flat layer, the default 0),
a `generate` child, or `from` (`xs:IDREF` of an `image` or `map` asset) with `encoding` (`gray` | `gray16` |
`terrarium` | `mapbox`, default `gray`), `amplitude` (default 1) and `offset` (default 0).

| Kind | Unit | Solid | Channels | Range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bedrock`, `loose`, `lava`, `ice`, `snow` | m | yes | 1 | — |
| `water`, `sediment` | m | no | 1 | — |
| `flow` | m²/s | no | 2 (x, y) | — |
| `temperature` | K | no | 1 | — |
| `fuel` | kg/m² | no | 1 | — |
| `burn`, `hardness`, `wetness` | 1 | no | 1 | [0, 1] |

**`generate`**: `noise` (`fbm` | `ridged` | `billow`, default `fbm`), `octaves` (1 to 16, default 6),
`frequency` (features across the longer side, default 3), `lacunarity` (≥ 1, default 2), `gain` ((0, 1],
default 0.5), `warp` (cells, ≥ 0, default 0), `warpFrequency` (default 1), `terraces` (0 to 64, default 0),
`terraceSharpness` (≥ 1, default 1), `amplitude` and `offset` (the layer's unit, defaults 1 and 0). TG3.

**`mask`**: `id` (`xs:ID`), `kind` (`slope` | `altitude` | `curvature` | `flow`), `min`, `max` (`xs:double`,
either optional, `min` ≤ `max`), `feather` (≥ 0, in the quantity's unit, default 0), `invert`
(`xs:boolean`, default false). TG4.

**`material`**: `color` (`paintType`), `mask` (`xs:IDREF` of a mask of the same terrain). TG5.

**`object3DType`** (`scene-render.xsd:4112`): the `primitive` enumeration gains `terrain`, and the type gains
`terrain` (`xs:IDREF`), required with that primitive (TG6).

**Schematron** (`scene-render.sch`):

- **TG1** Every `layer/@kind` is one of the thirteen kinds; a kind appears once (a `snow` kind once per
  `type`); a `value` of `burn`, `hardness` or `wetness` lies in [0, 1].
- **TG2** `cells` is an integer from 4 to 4096 and `metersPerCell` is positive.
- **TG3** A `layer` has one source (`value`, `generate` or `from`); `generate`'s attributes lie in the ranges
  above; `from` names an existing asset and `encoding` is one of the four.
- **TG4** `mask/@kind` is one of the four, mask ids are unique, `min` ≤ `max`, `feather` ≥ 0.
- **TG5** `material/@mask` references a `mask` of the same `terrain`.
- **TG6** `display` is `picture`, `surface` or `none`; an `object3D` with `primitive="terrain"` has `terrain`
  referencing a `terrain` element.
- **V17** (extended) `terrain` and `primitive="terrain"` require `scene/@version` 1.8 or later.

### Semantics

The reference module is `conformance/tools/terrain_grid_reference.py`; the function named in each rule is
its definition. It loads the random-streams reference (F1) for the draws.

1. **The grid.** With s = max(`width`, `height`) / `cells`, nx = max(round(`width` / s), 4) and
   ny = max(round(`height` / s), 4) (the erosion rule); cell (i, j) covers the rectangle's i-th column and
   j-th row, i right and j down, and sits `metersPerCell` from its neighbours. `grid`. Every layer, mask and
   process of this element uses this one grid resolution.
2. **Layers and the surface.** A layer is nx × ny values in its unit (`flow` has two). The ground surface is
   the sum of the solid layers present (every `snow` type included), and the water surface adds `water`;
   a terrain without a solid layer is invalid. `surface`, `water_surface`.
3. **The lattice and the noise.** The element seed is e = E(project, `id`, `seed`, "terrain") (random-streams
   draft section 3). The lattice value at the 2D index (i, j) on channel c is 2 U(e, c, pack(i, j, 0)) − 1
   (section 2.3); the value noise at (x, y) blends the four surrounding lattice values with the quintic fade
   6t⁵ − 15t⁴ + 10t³ (`lattice_value`, `value2`). Layer l draws its octave k from channel
   l · 4096 · 1024 + k and its warp from channels (l · 4096 + 1) · 1024 and (l · 4096 + 2) · 1024, the
   octave convention of the draft's W. With u = x / max(nx, ny) in fractions of the longer side, octave k
   samples the value noise at `frequency` · `lacunarity`^k and weighs `gain`^k; the sum is divided by the sum
   of the weights. `fbm` adds the noise n, `ridged` adds 2 (1 − |n|)² − 1 and `billow` adds 2 |n| − 1, so all
   three lie in [−1, 1]. `noise`, `octave_channel`.
4. **Warp and terraces.** The cell (i, j) is sampled at (i, j) + `warp` · (value noise of the x channel, of
   the y channel) at `warpFrequency` (`warped`). The 0..1 height h = (1 + n) / 2 is terraced into `terraces`
   steps: with t = h · `terraces`, k = ⌊t⌋ and f = t − k, f becomes f^s / (f^s + (1 − f)^s) for s =
   `terraceSharpness`, the identity at s = 1 and a hard step as s grows (`terrace`). The layer value is
   `offset` + `amplitude` · h. `generate_layer`.
5. **Layers from assets.** The asset is resampled by nearest pixel (x = ⌊i · w / nx⌋, the erosion rule) and
   decoded: `gray` is the value in [0, 1], `gray16` divides by 65535, `terrarium` is (R · 256 + G + B / 256)
   − 32768 m, `mapbox` is −10000 + (R · 65536 + G · 256 + B) / 10 m; the layer is `offset` + `amplitude` ·
   decoded. `decode`, `from_image`.
6. **Derived quantities.** On the ground surface with cell size d: the gradient by central differences
   (one-sided at the edges) per metre; `slope` = atan |∇h| in degrees; `curvature` = ∂²h/∂x² + ∂²h/∂y² by
   second differences (an edge cell takes the nearest interior second difference), in 1/m; `flow` = log10 of
   the D8 accumulation, where every cell drains to its steepest strictly lower neighbour of eight (diagonals
   over √2) and the accumulation counts the cell and everything upstream (O'Callaghan and Mark 1984).
   `gradient`, `slope_degrees`, `curvature`, `flow_accumulation`, `quantity`.
7. **Masks and materials.** A mask is 1 where the quantity lies in [`min`, `max`], falls linearly to 0 over
   `feather` outside that band (hard without), and is inverted when `invert` is set (`mask_values`, `mask`).
   The picture starts from the altitude ramp, `colorLow` at the lowest cell to `colorHigh` at the highest,
   and each `material` in order replaces the colour by its own in the proportion its mask gives.
8. **The picture.** The sun vector is (sin az cos el, −cos az cos el, sin el) with az = `sunAzimuth`
   (0 up the image, clockwise) and el = `sunElevation`; the normal is (−`relief` · gx, −`relief` · gy, 1)
   normalised; the shade 0.25 + 0.75 max(0, n · sun) multiplies the colour (`erosion.rs` `image`, with the
   physical gradient in place of the normalised one). The picture fills the rectangle at (`x`, `y`) in the 2D
   scene, one cell per grid cell, bilinearly resampled to the frame. `sun_vector`, `hillshade`, `picture`.
9. **The surface.** `object3D primitive="terrain"` is SREP 70's heightfield mesh over the physical extent
   nx · `metersPerCell` by ny · `metersPerCell`, centred, vertices (x, −h, z) row by row with h the ground
   surface, two triangles per cell, in local units equal to metres; the object's own transform applies after.
   `mesh`, `mesh_counts`.
10. **Determinism.** Generation is a pure function of the document, so the layers are the same on every
    engine and every run; their digest is the SHA-256 of the layers as JSON with sorted keys and values
    rounded to three decimals (`digest_of`, `build`). The processes of T1–T5 state their own streams
    S(e, p, t, pack(i, j, 0)) and seek rules; this draft has no time-dependent state.
11. **Validation.** `validate_terrain` raises TG1–TG6 and V17 with the rule name in the message.

## Rationale

- **One grid, one resolution.** The processes exchange mass between layers cell by cell; nested or per-layer
  resolutions would need conservative resampling at every step and make the seek check (SREP 14) depend on
  it. A second `terrain` element covers a detail area; the SREP 12 open issue is closed this way.
- **Named kinds with units** rather than free layer names: processes need to know what a layer is (water
  flows, bedrock erodes into loose material, snow melts into water), and the units make `amplitude`,
  `metersPerCell` and the masks' thresholds physical, which the quality bar's masks (slope in degrees,
  altitude in metres) require.
- **D24 value noise, quintic fade.** SREP 14 rule 2 requires the lattice hash; the quintic fade keeps the
  second derivative continuous, which the curvature mask needs. `erosion.rs` uses a cubic fade and its own
  hash; the picture of a 1.7 `<erosion>` is unchanged because `erosion` keeps its own generator.
- **Ridged and billow as octave transforms** (Musgrave) rather than a separate multifractal with feedback:
  the three share one code path and one range, and the quality bar names fBm, ridged and warped noise, not a
  particular multifractal.
- **Nearest resampling of assets** matches `erosion.rs` and keeps a DEM's values exact at its own resolution;
  bilinear or cubic is a later option.
- **The hillshade** keeps the baseline's look (0.25 ambient, Lambert sun) so the picture of a flat `erosion`
  and a flat `terrain` agree.

## Rejected alternatives

- Extending `<erosion>` with layers: `erosion` is a process on a height; this element is the data, and T1
  becomes a process on it.
- Free-form layer names with a `unit` attribute: processes could not find the water or the bedrock.
- A 3D object that is itself the terrain (no 2D picture): the bar asks for both from one element, and the
  2D picture is what explainer shots use.
- Houdini's heightfield layer model wholesale (SREP 12 rejects adopting an engine's model).
- Bicubic or Perlin gradient noise in 2D: the random-streams draft defines the 2D lattice draw and the
  value noise; gradient noise in 2D would need its own normative gradients.

## Backwards compatibility

A 1.7 document is valid and unchanged in meaning: `terrain` and `primitive="terrain"` are new. Documents
using them require version 1.8 (V17). `erosion`, `heightfield` and `primitive="map"` are unchanged; T1 will
propose how `erosion` relates to a terrain.

## Open issues

1. **Flow routing.** D8 routes each cell to one neighbour; multiple-flow-direction routing (Freeman 1991,
   D∞ of Tarboton 1997) gives smoother flow masks on gentle slopes. The editor may admit `routing="d8|dinf"`.
2. **Flats and pits.** D8 leaves flats and pits as sinks (the pit row collects 25 cells); a priority-flood
   fill (Barnes et al. 2014) before routing makes rivers continue; T1 and T2 will need it.
3. **Resampling of assets.** Nearest keeps DEM values exact; a bilinear option matters when a DEM is coarser
   than the grid.
4. **The picture's resampling to the frame.** Bilinear per rule 8; the baseline's `erosion` draws one cell
   per pixel at its own resolution, and the two differ at non-integer scales.
5. **Mask smoothing.** Masks are per cell; Gaea blurs masks before splatting (a `blur` in cells).
6. **Snow types.** `type` is free text; T3 will name the types it moves between (powder, packed, firn, ice).
7. **Memory.** 4096 × 4096 cells × 13 layers × 4 bytes is 870 MB; the editor may cap `cells` × layers.
