# SREP 30 — Add lineWidth to the grid generator

## Abstract

A `generator` of kind `grid` draws lines whose width is fixed at about 4 % of its pitch (`scale`), at least one pixel.
The width cannot be chosen: a fine grid with one-pixel lines needs a small `scale`, which also shrinks the pitch.
`generator` gains an optional `lineWidth`, the line width in asset pixels, independent of `scale`. A Schematron rule (R47)
makes `lineWidth` valid only on a grid. Without it the lines are as before.

## Motivation

A style-library study (finding T21 F2 of the engine's task list) needed grids with hairline or heavy lines at a
pitch the layout chose. With the baseline's rule, a pitch of 100 px draws 4 px lines, and the only way to a 1 px line at
that pitch was to draw the grid twice or to scale a smaller grid up, which blurs it (the alternatives an author has are inferred, not recorded in the finding). The engine's test pins the baseline:
at `scale="100"` the lines are 4 px wide and at `scale="25"` they are 1 px wide (the 4 % rule with the 1 px floor).

## Specification

### Syntax

```xml
<!-- generatorType gains -->
<xs:attribute name="lineWidth" type="nonNegativeDecimal">
  <xs:annotation><xs:documentation>Grid only: the line width in asset pixels, whatever @scale says; the pitch stays
  @scale. Absent, the lines are about 4 % of @scale wide (at least 1 px). Animates like @scale.</xs:documentation></xs:annotation>
</xs:attribute>
```

```xml
<sch:pattern id="p65">
  <sch:rule context="generator[@lineWidth]">
    <sch:assert id="R47" test="@kind='grid'">@lineWidth is the line width of a grid generator.</sch:assert>
  </sch:rule>
</sch:pattern>
```

A new attribute with no default, accepted in every version. The rule rejects only documents that set an attribute that
did nothing.

### Semantics

For `kind="grid"`: the pitch is `@scale` in asset pixels, as before. With `@lineWidth` = w, each line is w pixels wide
(w = 0 draws no lines), in place of `max(1, 0.04 · @scale)`. The lines keep the positions the baseline gives them (the
pattern is centred on the asset, turned by `@angle` and scrolled by `@evolution` periods; a line starts at each multiple of
the pitch). `@lineWidth` animates like `@scale`.

For every other kind, `@lineWidth` is invalid (R47).

### Defaults and the neutral case

Absent `@lineWidth`, the lines are `max(1, 0.04 · @scale)` pixels, as in the baseline.

## Rationale

- **Pixels, not a fraction of the pitch.** A hairline is a physical width; the author's intent does not scale with the pitch.
- **A new attribute rather than a changed rule.** The 4 % rule is declared behaviour that existing scenes depend on.
- **Only on grids.** Checkerboards and stripes have no lines.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A fraction (`lineRatio`).** Makes a 1 px line at pitch 100 a value of 0.01, and at pitch 37 something else.
- **Stroking a path-based grid.** A different feature (vector grids); the generator is for procedural backgrounds.

## Backwards compatibility

Class: Added, MINOR in effect, accepted in every version (neutral default). R47 rejects a document only if it sets
`lineWidth` on a non-grid generator, where the attribute did not exist.
