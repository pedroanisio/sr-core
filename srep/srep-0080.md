# SREP 80 — Add voxel assets and breakable objects of cells

## Abstract

This SREP adds models made of cells. A `voxelAsset` reads cells from a MagicaVoxel `.vox` file, from the engine's SRVOL
cache, or from a closed mesh cut into cells. An `object3D` with `primitive="voxels"` draws the exposed faces of those
cells. A `rigidBody` can take the cells as its collider (`shape="voxels"`, mass from `density`). A crater cuts such a
body cell by cell, and its burst throws the cells that the cut removed. A fracture splits it by Voronoi seeds, planes
or palette labels, either at an impact or when the load on a joint between pieces passes a strength (`mode="stress"`).
Two attributes that were required become optional (`burst/@count`, `fracture/@interiorMaterial`). The Schematron still
requires both everywhere except on objects of cells. The SREP covers 105 schema items, rules VOX1 to VOX15, CRT13 to
CRT17, FRX8 to FRX15, changes to CRT5, FRX3 and FRX4, and the validator warning W09. It requires SREP 77.

## Motivation

This is one of six SREPs (76 to 81) that take upstream what the reference engine's vendored schema has and sr-core 1.5.0 lacks, so that the engine can vendor sr-core's XSD and Schematron byte for byte again. `conformance/tools/engine_gap.py` measures the gap between sr-core at tag schema-1.5.0 and the engine at commit 4bf7a9e (whose `schema/UPSTREAM` cites schema-1.5.0 at 3f14a20): every element, attribute, enumeration value, documentation block and Schematron pattern, rule, let and assertion the engine has and sr-core lacks or words differently, 222 items, with the engine commit that introduced each one, in `conformance/tools/engine-gap-1.5.0.json`. None of them belongs to SREPs 66 to 75 (the tool checks their declarations and rule ids), the engine has nothing that sr-core has and it lacks, and no new rule id collides with an existing one. This SREP takes 105 of them: 58 XSD items added and 3 changed, 41 Schematron items added and 3 changed.

sr-core 1.5 has no element for a model of cells, and `object3D/@primitive` has no value `voxels`. A crater deforms
only a static or kinematic surface (`trimesh` or `auto`: CRT5, sr-core `schema/scene-render.sch:637` at tag
`schema-1.5.0`). A fracture only breaks a closed mesh, and it needs an `interiorMaterial` for the faces the cut makes
(FRX3, same file, line 644). There is no way to make an object whose drawing, collider, crater and fracture all use the
same cells.

The reference engine has this. One sparse grid of cells (`sr_3d::occupancy`) is drawn, collides and is cut, "so what
an object looks like and what it collides as are the same cells" (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3147-3149). At commit `4bf7a9e` every
part is evaluated end to end: there are no pending SREP rows (`crates/sr-eval/src/pending.rs`, `PENDING = &[]`), and
all eight valid corpus documents with bodies of cells evaluate with no failure through their last frame
(`crates/sr-eval/tests/voxels/corpus.rs`, test
`the_valid_documents_of_the_corpus_that_make_bodies_of_cells_are_evaluated_as_what_they_say`). The engine reports
these measurements. None was re-run for this draft.

- A crater from the law, in soft rock, takes 6,460 cells out of a slab with a pillar (6,352 on flat ground, 98.34% of
  the law's volume). It throws 80% of them and heaps 20% on a rim (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3454).
- On ground sloping 10, 20 and 30 degrees, the volume cut is within 1.5% of the law's (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3457).
- A cantilever of five cells welded to a wall, with a weight on its tip, breaks nothing in two seconds at 0.995 of
  the load that brings its root joint to the strength. At 1.005 it breaks the root joint and no other
  (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3415; `crates/sr-eval/tests/voxels/scene_stress.rs:116`, `:131`).
- The CPU side of the surface: a 1,000,000-cell sphere gives 31,392 quads in 0.023 s of merge. The worst case, a
  checkerboard of 126, gives 6,001,128 quads (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3206-3216).

### Semantics

The key words MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD and MAY are used as in RFC 2119. "Scene units" are the units of the composition.
A metre is `physics/@pixelsPerMeter` scene units, default 100 (`schema/scene-render-1.1.xsd:3444`;
`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:628`). Axes are the scene's: x right, y down, z away from the camera.

#### 1. The lattice

1.1. A cell is an integer key `[i, j, k]` with a palette index from 1 to 255. Index 0 means empty. The cell fills
`[i, i+1) x [j, j+1) x [k, k+1)` in cells of the object's space (`crates/sr-3d/src/occupancy.rs:6-11`). Keys MUST lie
in `[-2^30, 2^30)`. A renderer MUST refuse, as an error, a cell outside that range
(`crates/sr-3d/src/occupancy.rs:12-13`, `:41`).

1.2. The scan order is z, then y, then x, with x running fastest (`crates/sr-3d/src/occupancy.rs:16`). Every order
below that is not stated otherwise is this order.

1.3. With a cell size `c` in object units, the centre of cell `key` is `(key + 1/2) * c`
(`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel.rs:56-58`). The cell that holds a point `p` is `floor(p / c)` on each axis, except that a
quotient within `4 * eps * max(|q|, 1)` of a whole number counts as on the face and goes to the cell above. Here `eps`
is the f64 machine epsilon (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel.rs:60-83`).

1.4. After loading, the cells MUST be moved so that the minimum corner of the box of occupied cells is key
`[0, 0, 0]`. That corner is the object's origin (XSD annotation of `object3D/@voxels`;
`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:263-289`). If the box spans more than 2^30 cells on an axis, that is an error that
names the span (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:270-276`).

#### 2. Voxel assets (`voxelAsset`)

2.1. **Source.** An asset has exactly one source (VOX2). Either a file `src` whose format is `format`, or the file
extension `.vox` or `.srvol` when `format` is absent. Or a mesh asset `fromMesh` with `cellSize` in scene units.

The reference matches the extension without regard to case (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:359-363`). VOX2
matches it with case (see Open issues). With no format and no recognised extension, the asset is an error that asks
for `format` (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:378`).

2.2. **Reading bounds.** The renderer MUST resolve the file as a mesh asset's file is resolved. A remote scheme is an
error that names it (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:254-261`).

The renderer MUST refuse, before parsing, a file longer than `maxMemoryMiB * 2^20` bytes
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:313-320`, `:173-206`). When `sha256` is declared, the renderer MUST check it
against the bytes read, before parsing (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:339-346`). For `fromMesh`, the mesh asset's
own `sha256` is the one checked (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:9-12`, `:303-309`).

`maxCells` bounds the cells and `maxMemoryMiB` bounds the bytes of the grid. Both are checked before allocation.
Exceeding either is an error that names the number. The renderer MUST NOT cut the model short (XSD annotation of
`voxelAssetType`; `crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:295-298`; `crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/vox.rs:538-543`).

When the attributes are absent, the engine uses `maxCells` 4,194,304 and `maxMemoryMiB` 128
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:51-53`).

2.3. **The `.vox` reading.** A renderer MUST read the file as follows. Each point cites
`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/vox.rs`.

- **Header and chunks.** The header is `VOX ` with version 150 or 200 (`:232-238`). The first chunk is `MAIN`.
  `PACK`, `SIZE`, `XYZI`, `RGBA`, `MATL`, `nTRN`, `nGRP` and `nSHP` are read. Every other chunk is skipped by its length
  (`:263-376`). All numbers are little endian (`:7-8`).
- **A model.** A model is a `SIZE` followed by an `XYZI`. Each side is from 1 to 256 (`:224`, `:267-269`). Each cell
  is inside the size, appears once, and has a colour index from 1 to 255. Index 0 is an error (`:285-293`). If a
  `PACK` chunk is present, its count MUST equal the number of models (`:381-385`).
- **Axes.** MagicaVoxel's cell `[x, y, z]` (z up) becomes the scene's cell `[x, -z-1, y]`. This is a half turn about x
  composed with a swap of y and z, with determinant 1, so no face is mirrored (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel.rs:4-7`,
  `:43-45`).
- **Palette.** The colour of index `c` is entry `c - 1` of the `RGBA` chunk. A file with no `RGBA` chunk uses entry
  `c` of the format's default palette (`:589-595`; `crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/default_palette.rs`). Sources: sections 7
  and 8 of `MagicaVoxel-file-format-vox.txt` in https://github.com/ephtracy/voxel-model (`:22-24`).
- **Materials.** The id of a `MATL` chunk is the palette index. The format description does not say this. The engine
  takes it from `ogt_vox.h` (https://github.com/jpaver/opengametools) and from two real files (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3252-3255).
- **`model` given.** That model (numbered from 0) is placed with no transform (`:545-552`).
- **`model` absent, no scene graph.** The file MUST have exactly one model. Otherwise it is an error that asks for
  `model` (`:555-561`).
- **`model` absent, with a scene graph.** Every `nSHP` model is placed by the transforms above it, using frame 0 of each
  `nTRN` only (`:326-347`). The translation `_t` is three integers. The rotation byte `_r` encodes a matrix `R`:
  - bits 0-1 give the column of the nonzero entry in row 0;
  - bits 2-3 give the column of the nonzero entry in row 1;
  - row 2 takes the remaining column;
  - bits 4, 5 and 6 give the signs of rows 0, 1 and 2.

  A byte that does not give a permutation is an error (`:86-94`).
- **Placing a cell.** A model's cells are placed about its pivot `floor(size / 2)`. With `q = cell - pivot`, the new
  cell on axis `k` is `R[k] q + t_k` when sign `k` is positive, and `-q[column k] - 1 + t_k` when it is negative
  (`:104-113`, `:570-582`). Where two models place a cell in the same spot, the later one in the walk wins (`:19`,
  `:581`). Layers, hidden nodes, animation after frame 0 and cameras are not read (`:19-20`).
- **Scene graph checks.** Before anything is placed, the graph MUST be checked whole (`:406-450`):
  - exactly one root;
  - no cycle;
  - no node that the root does not reach;
  - depth at most 64;
  - at most 2^20 nodes;
  - at most 2^20 placements, a model counted each time it is used;
  - the placed cells within `min(maxCells, 2^26)`;
  - a file of at most 65,536 models.

  Defaults are at `:55-66`. A translation or placed cell more than 2^24 from the origin is an error (`:220`,
  `:342-344`, `:578-580`).

2.4. **The SRVOL reading.** The model is one grid of the cache. It is the grid named by `voxelGrid`, or `voxels` when
absent (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/srvol.rs:13`; `crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:373`).

The value at index `[i, j, k]` is the palette index of the cell whose corner is that index. The background MUST be 0.
Every nonzero value MUST be a whole number from 1 to 255. Any other value is an error that names the cell and the value
(`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/srvol.rs:1-7`, `:54-59`, `:72-77`).

The cell size of the cache is the grid's scale, taken only when the transform is a uniform positive scale with no
shear and no offset (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/srvol.rs:85-89`). A cache has no palette (`:6`). The decoder's own ceilings
are those of SRVOL version 1 (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3459-3492).

2.5. **A mesh cut into cells (`fromMesh`).** The renderer MUST place the mesh vertices exactly as it draws them: the
import basis times each node's transform, in scene units (100 to the metre for an asset in metres) and scene axes
(`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:214-248`). This rule came from commit `61e854b`.

The lattice is aligned to multiples of `cellSize` in those coordinates. Cell `[i, j, k]` is filled, with palette index
1, if and only if its centre `((i, j, k) + 1/2) * cellSize` is inside the closed mesh. "Inside" is decided by the
smoke-collider test, where the nearest oriented surface decides. A mesh that is not closed is an error
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel.rs:1-8`, `:85-86`, `:100-114`).

Before any test, the box of the lattice MUST be checked against 2^28 cells and against `maxMemoryMiB`
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel.rs:28`, `:68-84`). The result MUST NOT depend on the number of threads
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel.rs:87-121`). The engine's test is a glb cube of one metre cut at 10: 1,000 cells, x 0 to 9,
y -10 to -1, z -10 to -1 (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3229-3230).

#### 3. Objects of cells and the `blocks` surface

3.1. **Cell size.** The cell size in object units is the object's `cellSize`. Absent, it is the asset's cellSize (the
`fromMesh` `cellSize`, or the SRVOL grid scale). Absent again, it is 1 (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:84-87`). The
object's transform applies on top, like any `object3D` (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:439`).

3.2. **Material of a palette index.** For each index in use, a renderer MUST resolve the material in this order
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:220-302`):

1. If `palette` is a list of material ids and the index is at most the list length, the i-th id.
2. Otherwise, the object's `material`.
3. Otherwise, if `palette` is `file`, or if `palette` is absent and the asset has colours, the file's colour and file
   material.
4. Otherwise, an error that names the index (`:295-298`).

A list id that names no material is an error (`:269`). See Open issues for two cases where the reference and the XSD
annotation differ.

3.3. **File materials.** The format has no photometric unit, so the mapping is the engine's choice. A renderer SHOULD
map file materials as follows (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:106-128`, `:154-161`; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3187-3191):

| File material | Mapping |
|---|---|
| diffuse | dielectric, roughness `_rough` (else 0.8) |
| metal | metallic `_metal` (else 1), roughness `_rough` (else 0.2) |
| glass | transmission `_trans` (else 1); index of refraction `_ri`, else `1 + _ior`, else 1.5; roughness `_rough` (else 0.05) |
| emit | emission of `_emit` (else 1) times the colour |
| blend | alpha-blended, opacity `_alpha` |
| unknown `_type` | drawn as diffuse, with a note in the frame |

For glass, `_ri` wins over `_ior` when both are present (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/material.rs:58-61`). The unknown-type
note is at `crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:283-291`.

3.4. **Classes.** Two indices are one class when they look the same: the same document material; the same colour and
file material; or the same emissive colour and strength. A class is see-through when its material transmits or is
blended (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:303-317`).

3.5. **Exposure.** The face of cell A toward its face-neighbour B is exposed when any of these holds
(`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface.rs:10-13`, `:69-76`):

- B is empty;
- A is opaque and B is see-through;
- both are see-through, of different classes, and A's class is the lesser.

Cells that touch only by an edge or a corner hide nothing of each other.

3.6. **Merge.** The renderer MUST merge exposed faces into quads plane by plane (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface/greedy.rs:26-31`, `:77-99`):

- Only faces of one class, on one plane, facing one way, merge.
- Scan by `v`, then by `u`. Here `(u, v)` are the two other axes in cyclic order: for axis x, (y, z); for y, (z, x);
  for z, (x, y) (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface.rs:7-9`).
- The first face not yet taken starts a quad. The quad takes the widest run of its class along `u`, then grows along
  `v` while the whole row is of its class and not taken.
- A quad side is cut at 65,535 cells (`greedy.rs:9-10`).

The result is a function of the cells alone. The surface is not conformed: T-junctions remain
(srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3194-3198).

3.7. **Surface budget.** The surface memory of an object MUST NOT exceed `surfaceMemoryMiB`. The engine default is
128 (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:191`). Planes are made in a fixed order and the budget is checked after each
plane. The first plane that takes the surface over the budget stops the work with an error. A renderer MUST NOT draw
part of a surface (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface/greedy.rs:53-75`; `crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface/cache.rs:144-180`).

The budget belongs to the object. The object and the pieces that come away from it share it, in the frame's order
(object first, then pieces). A piece that does not fit is not drawn and reports the error
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:324-340`, `:427-434`).

The cost of one quad differs between the XSD and the reference:

- The XSD annotation of `surfaceMemoryMiB` says 1,240 bytes a quad at the peak. That admits 108,240 quads at 128 MiB.
- The reference counts 888 bytes: `3 * 24 + 2 * (4 * 96 + 6 * 4)`. That is three copies of the 24-byte compact quad,
  plus host and device copies of four 96-byte vertices and six 4-byte indices
  (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface/greedy.rs:36-42`, asserted at
  `crates/sr-3d/tests/geometry/voxel_surface.rs:530`). That admits 151,146 quads (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3176).

This SREP keeps the XSD text verbatim and leaves the figure as an open issue. The path tracer's copy of the triangles
is outside the budget (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3176).

3.8. **Drawing.** Each group of quads (one document material, one file look, or one emissive colour and strength) is
one mesh. It is drawn by both renderers with no special shader (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:1-21`,
`:396-421`). An object that a simulation cuts is drawn from the frame's cells and pieces, not from the asset
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:8-10`, `:192-217`).

#### 4. Bodies of cells (`rigidBody` on an object of cells)

4.1. **Collider.** A `rigidBody` of an object of cells whose `shape` is absent, `auto` or `voxels` has the cells as
its collider. It is a "body of cells" (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:560-565`).

4.2. **Mass.** Let `s` be the object's world scale, `c` the cell size from 3.1, `rho` the `density` (kg/m^3), `ppm`
the pixels per metre, and `N` the number of cells. Each cell is a box of `h_a = c * s_a / ppm` metres on axis `a`, and

    m = N * rho * h_x * h_y * h_z

(`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:259-276`; `crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:21-43`). A renderer MUST refuse, by error, a body
of cells whose scale is not positive on every axis, which includes a mirrored scale
(`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:268-270`).

4.3. **Centre and inertia.** These come from the exact cells (`crates/sr-3d/src/occupancy.rs:562-590`). With
`u = 2 key + 1` per axis, the centre is `h_a * sum(u_a) / (2N)`. The inertia about the centre is

    I_aa = N * (m/N) * (h_b^2 + h_c^2) / 12 + m * (Var_b + Var_c)
    I_ab = -m * Cov_ab

`Var` and `Cov` are the variance and covariance of the cell centres in metres. They are computed from exact integer
sums. Equal cells MUST give equal bits whatever their order (`crates/sr-3d/src/occupancy.rs:30-31`). The engine's test:
a cube of two cells a side has mass `8 rho s^3` and inertia `m L^2 / 6` with `L = 2s`, to 1e-12
(`crates/sr-eval/tests/voxels/loader.rs:122`; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3298-3300).

#### 5. Craters on objects of cells (CRT5, CRT13 to CRT17)

5.1. **When.** A crater in an object of cells grows from a `source` (CRT15). Its owner is static or kinematic (CRT5).
It cuts the cells once, at the first impact that its watch reports: "one crater is one watch is one impact is one cut"
(`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1364`).

The impact uses the crater law of SREP 77, with one change: the normal is the axis of the cell surface (5.2), and the
speed given to the law is the closing speed along that axis (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:440-455`). The owner's
world scale MUST be uniform. Otherwise it is an error at run time (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1236-1240`). The cells
MUST be cubes (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:484-487`).

5.2. **Axis and plane.** The axis is the outward normal of the cell surface around the impact point: minus the
direction of the centroid of the filled cells within a radius `R` of the point (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:285-315`).
`R` is half the crest radius when that is at least 4 cells, else the whole crest radius
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:118-121`, `:258-268`).

A normal within 2 degrees of a lattice axis snaps to that axis (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:270-283`). The
contact normal is used only when no estimate exists, or when the estimate points the other way.

The kernel's plane is placed where the axis meets the cell surface. On ground that is not a lattice face, it is placed
at the mean plane of the staircase (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:80-110`, `:126-186`).

5.3. **Kernel.** The kernel is the law's conserving crater with bulking 1. The bowl holds the law's volume; the rim
holds the share that is not thrown (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:65-76`). This is why CRT13 refuses `mantle`,
`bulking` and `repose`. CRT14 refuses `curve` because the cut is instantaneous. CRT6 already refuses `start` and `end`
for a crater with a source (commit `4ea3b74`).

5.4. **Which cells are removed.** Let `(a, r)` be a cell centre's height above the crater plane along the axis and its
distance from the axis, in metres. Let `S(r) = rim_height_at(r) - bowl_depth_at(r)`. A filled cell is removed if and
only if

    r < crest + rimWidth  and  S(r) <= a <= crest

(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:11-14`, `:259-267`).

5.5. **Thrown cells and rim.** Let `N` be the number of removed cells, and let `T = round(share * N)`, where `share` is
the law's ejecta volume over its volume, 0.8 (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:52-53`;
`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:303`).

Removed cells are ordered by `r`, then by `-a`, then by scan. The first `T` are thrown (`voxel_crater.rs:305-313`).
Thrown cell `i` (from 0) moves at speed `v_i`. `v_i` is the speed above which the fraction `(i + 1/2) / T` of the law's
ejecta mass lies. That distribution is sampled with 4,000 particles of the engine's launch model
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:21-24`, `:47-71`; `crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:350`).

The cell's direction is

    e = 45 deg + 15 deg * (2 u - 1)
    v = v_i * (cos e * radial + sin e * axis)

`u` is a number in [0, 1) hashed from the seed and the cell. The seed is the FNV-1a-64 hash of the owner's id
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:121-128`, `:316-327`; `crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:346-355`).

The other `N - T` cells are heaped on the rim as new cells, keeping their palette indices. They fill the law's rim
level set, lowest level first. A cell is added only where the cell under it is ground or rim. Cells are neither made
nor lost (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:15-28`, `:328-333`).

5.6. **What stays the body.** The body after the cut is the cells left plus the rim. It splits into connected
components by faces (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:495-509`; `crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:91-104`). Which
parts stay depends on `anchor`:

- `base`: every component that has a cell on the base layer stays. The base layer is the greatest y key of the cells
  before the cut, which is the lowest layer, since y points down (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:59-61`;
  `crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:114`).
- `largest`: only the largest component stays. Among equal ones, the first in scan order stays
  (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:107-113`).

When `anchor` is absent, the engine uses `base` for a static or kinematic owner and `largest` for a dynamic one
(`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:704-708`). CRT5 never allows a dynamic owner here, so the effective default is `base`
(commit `dc9e034`).

5.7. **Fragments, dust and overflow.** Each loose component (one that does not stay) is handled as follows:

- **Dust.** A component with fewer than `fragmentMinCells` cells (engine default 1) is dust. Its cells leave the body
  (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:118-119`; `crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:715`).
- **Fragments.** The others become fragments. Each takes one of `maxFragments` slots (engine default 64). Slots are
  dynamic one-cell bodies created at build time and kept out of the world until a cut fills them
  (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:716`, `:946-990`).
- **Overflow.** If there are more fragments than free slots:
  - `fragmentOverflow="error"`: an error that names both numbers (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:122-128`;
    `crates/sr-sim/src/physics3d/voxel_split.rs:224-231`).
  - `fragmentOverflow="dust"`: the largest components keep the slots (ties to the first in scan order) and the rest
    become dust (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:130-136`).
- **Motion.** A fragment leaves with the motion that its own cells had in the body (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3453).

5.8. **The burst (CRT16, CRT17).** A `burst` whose `crater` names the crater of an object of cells emits one particle
per thrown cell. Each particle is born at the impact instant at its cell's centre, with the cell's velocity and the
mass of one cell, transformed by the owner's pose at the impact (`crates/sr-eval/src/particles3d.rs:540-567`,
`:713-720`). The dust of the cut is emitted too, at rest in the owner's frame (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1602-1619`).

The particle count is the cut's, so `count` is not given (CRT16). The direction comes from the cut, so `angle` and
`angleSpread` are not given (CRT17). Every other burst keeps `count` and its existing rules.

#### 6. Fracture partitions on objects of cells (FRX3, FRX8 to FRX12)

6.1. **Partition.** The `partition` assigns every cell a label. Each label's cells then split into face-connected
components, so every piece is connected. Pieces are numbered by their first cell in scan order
(`crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs:388-438`). The three partitions:

- **`voronoi`** (default; `crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1195`). There are `pieces` seeds (XSD default 8), drawn from
  `seed` (XSD default 0) in the doubled box `[2 min + 1, 2 max + 1]` of the cells. Axis `a` of seed `s` is

      lo_a + splitmix64(seed + (3 s + a + 1) * 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15) mod (hi_a - lo_a + 1)

  (`crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs:314-352`). Each cell goes to the seed with the least squared distance between the
  seed and the cell's doubled centre `u = 2 key + 1`, in exact integers. A tie goes to the lowest seed index
  (`crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs:362-373`).
- **`planes`**. Up to 63 planes `nx ny nz offset` in object units (FRX11). A cell's label is the bitmask of the planes
  whose positive side it is on, with `n . ((key + 1/2) * cellSize) >= offset` (XSD annotation of `fracture/@planes`).
  The reference evaluates this in integers:

      n_int = round(n * 2^20),  o_int = round(2 * offset / cellSize * 2^20),  test n_int . u >= o_int

  Numbers that are not within 1e15 after scaling are an error (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:528-551`;
  `crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs:396-411`). The XSD annotation says the normal is taken to 2^-32 of its largest component.
  The rounding differs; see Open issues.
- **`labels="material"`**. The label is the palette index (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1188`, `:1194`).

6.2. **No interior material (FRX8).** Pieces keep the material of their cells. A face that the cut exposes is drawn
from the palette like any other face (XSD annotation of `fracture/@partition`). FRX3 still requires `interiorMaterial`
on a fracture of any other owner.

6.3. **Impact mode.** With `mode` absent or `impact`, the fracture fires at `at`, or at the impact of `source` (SREP 77),
as before. Pieces with fewer than `fragmentMinCells` cells are dust. If more than `maxFragments` pieces remain, that is
an error or, with `fragmentOverflow="dust"`, the smallest become dust (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:187-226`). If no
piece is left as a body, that is an error (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:222-226`).

The pieces become dynamic bodies of cells. Each has the mass of its cells and its share of `impulseX/Y/Z`
(`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:787-866`). The dust's mass, centre and inertia go to the world, which records the
momentum and angular momentum that the dust takes away as lost. The dust emits no particles
(`crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:183-186`, `:236-247`; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3455).

A partition makes at most 4,096 pieces. More is an error that names the number (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxels.rs:251-252`;
`crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs:430-432`).

#### 7. Fracture by stress (`mode="stress"`, FRX13 to FRX15)

7.1. **The body.** The object of cells is one dynamic rigid body. The pieces of its partition (6.1) are not bodies,
and the joints between them are not solver constraints. A joint is the set of faces that two pieces share
(`crates/sr-sim/src/physics3d/stress.rs:1-12`).

Each joint's section has (`crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs:165-182`, `:196-292`):

- area `A`;
- area-weighted centre;
- exact second moments `J` of its faces about that centre;
- unit mean normal `n` from piece `a` to piece `b`;
- the box that holds its faces.

At most 1,024 pieces (`crates/sr-sim/src/physics3d/stress.rs:33`, `:277-281`) and at most one world joint holding the
body (`:369-371`). Exceeding either is an error.

7.2. **Loads.** After each step, the force `F` and moment `M` that the rest of the body puts on one side of a cut are

    F dt = dP - J_ext,    M dt = dL - K_ext

`dP` and `dL` are the change in that side's momentum and angular momentum over the step, worked out from the side's
mass sums and the body's rigid motion. `J_ext` and `K_ext` are the impulses of the external loads on that side: weight
and fields, the step's contact impulses on the pieces they touch, and the one world joint
(`crates/sr-sim/src/stress/balance.rs:1-14`).

The balance is taken about the body's centre of mass, in the body's motion relative to it. Loads and arms are read in
the body frame at the rotation halfway through the step (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3388-3393, `:3416`). Friction impulses come from
the solver's last sub-step and are scaled to the whole step (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3416). The limits of that reading are in Open
issues.

What is left with no joint is a rigid acceleration and makes no stress. This covers damping, a velocity that was set,
and a driver torque (`crates/sr-sim/src/stress/balance.rs:11-14`).

7.3. **The cut of a joint.** The cut depends on whether the joint is a bridge (`crates/sr-sim/src/stress/plan.rs:4-14`):

- **Bridge** (the only path between two parts): the cut is that joint alone, and the side is the part on piece `a`'s
  side. Its load is exact.
- **In a cycle**: the side is the pieces whose centres lie on piece `a`'s side of the plane through the joint's centre,
  normal to `n`. The cut is all intact joints between that side and the rest. They carry the load as one rigid section.
- **Tangled** (`a` and `b` not on opposite sides of the plane): the side is piece `a` alone.

7.4. **Stress and the break rule.** For a cut with total area `A`, centre `C`, combined second moments `J` (each
joint's own plus `A_j d d^T`) and section normal `n`, the stresses are as follows. Each line cites
`crates/sr-sim/src/stress.rs`.

    sigma_N = (F . n) / A                                       (:143, :206)
    tau_V   = |F - (F . n) n| / A                               (:144, :207)
    M_C     = M + (P - C) x F ;  T = M_C . n ;  M_b = M_C - T n     (:146-151)
    g       solves J g = n x M_b in the plane of the section    (:159-183)
    sigma_B = max over the 8 corners p of the joint's box of g . (p - C)   (:188-202)
    tau_T   = |T| * r_max / (J_uu + J_vv)                       (:184, :203-214)
    s = sigma_N + sigma_B ,  t = tau_V + tau_T
    sigma_1 = max(0, s/2 + sqrt(s^2/4 + t^2))                   (:215-217)

`P` is the point that `M` is given about. `r_max` is the farthest in-plane distance of the joint's box corners from
`C`. A joint breaks when `sigma_1 >= strength`. This is Rankine's criterion for a brittle joint: a pure compression
gives zero (`crates/sr-sim/src/stress.rs:19-21`).

7.5. **Order.** Every joint is read on the state at the end of a step. All joints at or over the strength break
together at the start of the next step (`crates/sr-sim/src/physics3d/stress.rs:7-9`). The broken body then splits:

- The part held by the world joint stays the body. With no world joint, the largest part stays (ties to the first).
- The other parts take slots of the `maxFragments` pool, in order of their lowest piece, with the motion of their own
  centres (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3404-3407).
- Parts with fewer than `fragmentMinCells` cells are dust. Dust leaves silently: no particles, momentum not recorded
  (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3415; `crates/sr-sim/src/physics3d/stress.rs:18-20`).
- More parts than free slots is an error, or the smallest become dust (`crates/sr-sim/src/physics3d/stress.rs:771-782`).
- A part made of several joined pieces can break again, from the same pool (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3408-3409).

7.6. **The beam reading (commit `c59d084`).** A body at rest has the stress that the solver's contact distribution
gives it. The solver holds a 2 m x 2 m x 1 m block at the four corners of its foot, a quarter of the weight at each.
The block therefore reads as a deep beam of span `L = 2 m` on two supports:

    M = W L / 8 = 23.5 kN m
    sigma_1 = 6 M / (b h^2) = 3.53e4 Pa      (b = 1 m, h = 2 m)

The engine reads this within 3% (`crates/sr-sim/tests/rigid/stress_world.rs:1038-1052`; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3417-3421).
The true tension of a block lying on its whole face is zero. The 3.53e4 Pa is an artefact of the contact model, and it
grows with `rho g L`. A `strength` under it breaks such a block where it lies.

#### 8. Small cells (W09)

A validator SHOULD warn W09 when a voxels object's cell in the scene is smaller than 0.5:

    c_scene = cellSize * min(|scaleX|, |scaleY|, |scaleZ|)

`cellSize` here is the object's own, else the asset's. Scales default to 1. The values are read as `xs:double`
(`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:1725-1747`; `crates/sr-model/src/codes.rs` entry W09). The warning exists because the
raster renderer's shadow offsets are fixed in scene units. Measured against cells of 1, 23 of 51 shadow pixels differ at
0.25, and all of them at 0.1 (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3200-3204). A parent group's scale and the grid scale of an SRVOL file are
not seen by the validator (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3204). The renderer does not report W09 (commit `6a67c71`).

Several of these have no XSD default on purpose. A default would make the attribute present on every element, and
then VOX2, VOX5 and VOX11 could not refuse it where it does not belong (XSD annotations of `maxFragments`, `surface`,
`surfaceMemoryMiB` and `voxelAssetType`).

**Neutral case.** A document with no `voxelAsset` and no `primitive="voxels"` is not affected by any new attribute.
VOX5, VOX8, VOX9 and FRX9 refuse every new attribute outside an object of cells. CRT13 to CRT17 and FRX8 to FRX15 apply
only to objects of cells, or to `mode="stress"`, which FRX14 limits to objects of cells.

The reference enters the voxel code only for `primitive="voxels"` (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:1438-1441`;
`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:562-565`). So a document that was valid before renders as before. This claim comes from
reading the dispatch code; no pixel comparison was run for this draft.

## Rationale

- **One set of cells for drawing and physics.** The drawn surface, the collider, the mass and every cut read the same
  occupancy. A crater or fracture changes what is drawn and what collides together (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3147-3149;
  `crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:8-10`).
- **Errors, not truncation.** Every limit (`maxCells`, `maxMemoryMiB`, `surfaceMemoryMiB`, `maxFragments`) is checked
  before work is done, and going over it is an error that names the number. This follows SREP 40, where
  `maxParticles` overflow "is an error" (srep-0040.md:1335), and the burst annotation: "More particles than
  `maxParticles` is an error, not a truncation".
- **Determinism.** The following are all exact integer arithmetic or fixed orders, so equal cells give equal bits on
  any thread count and in any input order:
  - moments (`crates/sr-3d/src/occupancy.rs:30-31`);
  - Voronoi distances and plane tests (`crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs:26-29`, `:405`);
  - the scan order;
  - the merge order (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface/greedy.rs:28-31`).

  The quad hash matches an independent reference mesher (`tools/voxel_reference_mesher.py`;
  `crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface/greedy.rs:101-104`).
- **A proper rotation from MagicaVoxel axes.** `[x, y, z] -> [x, -z-1, y]` has determinant 1, so no face is mirrored
  and cells stay on the lattice (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel.rs:4-7`).
- **A cut that conserves cells.** With bulking 1, a cell is a cell of the same mass, so the rim holds exactly the
  cells that were not thrown (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:5-7`).
- **No attribute without an effect.** CRT13, CRT14 and CRT17 refuse attributes that would mean nothing on a cut of
  cells. The commits give the reason: "an attribute with no effect is a falsehood in a document" (`29ab659`,
  `4ea3b74`).
- **A rigid model for stress.** The body stays one rigid body until a joint breaks. This costs about 0.05 ms per step
  for 640 joints of 256 pieces (first reading 1.5 ms). It also keeps momentum, angular momentum and energy to 1e-9 when
  a beam breaks in flight (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3407, `:3413-3414`). The cost is that there is no stress wave and no elastic
  energy (7.6 and Open issues).

External sources the engine cites:

- The MagicaVoxel format description, https://github.com/ephtracy/voxel-model (`MagicaVoxel-file-format-vox.txt`,
  sections 7 and 8, and section (c) of its extension file for the rotation byte).
- `ogt_vox.h` of https://github.com/jpaver/opengametools, for the pivot `floor(size / 2)` and `MATL` ids as palette
  indices (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/vox.rs:22-28`).
- The `dot_vox` crate's `axes.vox`, as evidence for the pivot (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3270). No URL is given.

The engine names Rankine's criterion, Maxwell's Z-model (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_crater.rs:19-20`) and Rapier 0.36
(srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3416) without a bibliographic reference. This draft adds none.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Welding pieces as separate solver bodies** for the stress model. At 240 steps per second, a 2 m block of 64 welded
  boxes hit at 100 m/s kept 2.1 times its kinetic energy. A block of 256 cost 6.1 ms per step
  (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3384-3387; `crates/sr-sim/src/physics3d/stress.rs:3-4`; test `tests/rigid/stress_spike.rs`).
- **The cut of least area for a joint in a cycle.** It isolates the weakest piece of a block, whose load is only its
  own weight, and says nothing about the load carried across (`crates/sr-sim/src/stress/plan.rs:10-12`;
  srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3398).
- **A balance about a point fixed in the world.** It left 16 N and 65 N m on a body in free fall at 4 m/s, and the
  error grows with speed (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3391-3392).
- **Loads and arms in the end-of-step frame.** It turned a spinning beam's force half a step against it: a pull of 2.5
  read as 3.25 (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3416).
- **The contact normal as the crater axis.** On a slope of cells it is off by 10 degrees or more, and gave a crater
  from a speed 3% low (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:250-252`, `:440-442`).
- **The crater plane at the contact point.** It cut 8% over the law's volume: 7,080 cells instead of 6,460. Fixed in
  commit `e5b43bc` (`tools/evidence/cinematic-impact.json`, milestone 62, `limits`).
- **An interior material for cells.** A cell has no cut surface to paint. A face that should look different is a
  palette index, or a later `surface` value (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3374-3375).
- **`anchor` default `largest` for a dynamic body.** It never applies, because only a crater owner has an anchor and
  CRT5 makes that owner static or kinematic (commit `dc9e034`).
- **CRT14 refusing `start`, `end` and `curve`.** CRT6 already refuses `start` and `end` for a crater with a source, so
  that clause never fired alone. CRT14 is `curve` only (commit `4ea3b74`).

## Backwards compatibility

The change is MINOR.

- Every document valid under sr-core 1.5 stays valid. The new enumeration values (`voxels` on `object3D/@primitive`
  and `rigidBody/@shape`) and all new attributes are additions.
- Two attributes lose `use="required"` in the XSD: `burst/@count` (`burst3DType`) and `fracture/@interiorMaterial`.
  Documents that omit them were invalid before. They are now valid under the XSD alone. The Schematron still refuses
  them everywhere except on objects of cells:
  - **CRT16** requires `@count` on every burst that is not from the crater of an object of cells. The diff's test reads
    `(... and not(@count)) or (not(...) and @count)`. The corpus document `crt16-no-count.scene.xml` expects CRT16.
  - **FRX3** requires a declared `interiorMaterial` unless the parent is `primitive="voxels"`:
    `parent::object3D[@primitive='voxels'] or @interiorMaterial=...`.

  So a document that omits either attribute on a mesh, a surface crater or a plain emitter is still invalid under the
  full schema. A validator that runs only the XSD would accept it.
- CRT5 now also accepts `shape="voxels"`. VOX8 limits that shape to objects of cells, so no existing crater owner can
  newly pass with it.
- FRX4 no longer reads `partition`, `planes`, `labels` and `mode` as numbers. These attributes did not exist before.
- The version gate is VOX1: `version="1.3"` exactly, as V8 of SREP 40. See Open issues.

## Open issues

- **Version gate (all of SREPs 76 to 80).** The engine's text gates these features on `scene/@version="1.3"` exactly: BH1 and VOX1 directly, and the attributes on SREP 40 elements through SREP 40's own gates (V8 for volumes, media and pyro, CRT1, FRX1, P3D1, OCN1), which are already in sr-core 1.5.0 in that form. A document that uses them cannot declare 1.4, 1.5 or 1.6, so it cannot also use the features those versions gate (SREPs 57 to 75). SREPs 66 to 75 gate their features on 1.6 with an explicit list of the earlier versions. This draft keeps the engine's text verbatim, because the aim is a byte-equal vendoring; the review should decide whether the 1.3 gates become "1.3 or later" (a separate SREP that would also amend SREP 40). **Engine change:** yes, if the gates change: the engine mirrors every Schematron rule natively (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs`), so it must change those checks and its corpus, then re-vendor.
- **Merge point with SREP 70.** SREP 70 (draft) adds `parametric` and `heightfield` to the `object3D/@primitive` enumeration, the same line this SREP extends with `voxels` (`<xs:enumeration value="volume"/><xs:enumeration value="voxels"/>`). There is no conflict of meaning; whoever merges second rebases the line, and the byte-equal check then compares against the merged file. The engine must take the line as sr-core merges it. **Engine change:** none (a re-vendoring).

1. **Version gate.** VOX1 requires `version="1.3"` exactly (`/scene[@version!='1.3']`), as V8 of SREP 40 does
   (sr-core 1.5.0 `schema/scene-render.sch:509-510`). This SREP targets schema 1.6, and SREPs 66 to 75 gate their features on 1.6. As
   written, a document cannot use cells together with any 1.4, 1.5 or 1.6 feature. The text is kept verbatim from the
   engine. The editor should decide whether VOX1 becomes "1.3 or later".
2. **Merge point with SREP 70.** SREP 70 (draft) adds `parametric` and `heightfield` to the same
   `object3D/@primitive` enumeration that this SREP extends with `voxels`, plus the child elements `parametricSurface`
   and `heightfield`, and rules V14, PAR1 and PAR2 (`srep/srep-0070.md` on `origin/srep/drafts-2026-10`, lines 64-66,
   95-102). The two are textual neighbours. No semantic conflict was found: PAR2 already forbids those children on any
   other primitive, so VOX7 needs no new exclusion, and the attribute sets do not overlap. The only interaction is the
   version gate: V14 needs 1.6 and VOX1 needs 1.3 (item 1).
3. **Per-quad budget figure.** The XSD annotation of `surfaceMemoryMiB` says 1,240 bytes per quad at the peak (from
   commit `96ac10d`, 108,240 quads at 128 MiB) and lists four components. The reference at `4bf7a9e` counts 888 bytes
   (commit `89f2bd0`; `crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/surface/greedy.rs:36-42`; test `voxel_surface.rs:530`; 151,146 quads),
   with different components. srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3176 says 888, and srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3216 still says "at 1,240 bytes". The
   annotation is pasted verbatim. Either it changes to 888 or the reference changes, before Accepted.
4. **Plane rounding.** The XSD annotation of `fracture/@planes` (and srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3372) says the normal "is taken to
   2^-32 of its largest component". The reference rounds every number to multiples of 2^-20 in absolute terms
   (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_cut.rs:528-551`). A normal whose components are all under 2^-21 rounds to zero: it passes
   FRX11 and then cuts nothing, or puts every cell on one side.
5. **Palette list and file colours.** The XSD says a cell's material is "the one of its index, else the object's own
   `material`, else the colour of the file". With an explicit id list, no object `material`, and an index beyond the
   list in a file that has colours, the reference reports "no material" instead of using the file colour
   (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:225-229`, `:255-261`, `:294-299`).
6. **`palette="file"` on an asset with no colours.** The XSD says `file` "needs a file that has them". The reference
   neither refuses it nor reports it. It reads the grid's default palette, which is opaque white
   (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:225-227`, `:275-276`; `crates/sr-3d/src/occupancy.rs:17-18`). This comes from
   reading the code and is not tested.
7. **Slots for an impact fracture.** The XSD annotation of `maxFragments` describes slots: bodies that wait, one cell
   each. For a crater or a stress fracture, the reference makes slots (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:725-728`,
   `:946-990`). For an impact fracture of cells, it builds the piece bodies up front and uses `maxFragments` only as a
   cap on their number (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:787-866`). What a document sees is the same, but the body counts
   differ.
8. **Dust as particles.** The XSD annotation of `fragmentMinCells` says dust parts "leave the body and become
   particles". Only a crater cut's dust becomes particles, and only through a burst that names the crater
   (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1602-1619`). The dust of an impact fracture is recorded as lost momentum, and the dust
   of a stress break leaves silently (6.3, 7.5).
9. **The burst annotation.** The general text of `burst3DType` describes particles born "at the instant it is launched,
   at its launch distance from the impact point". For cells, every particle is born at the impact instant at its own
   cell's centre (`crates/sr-eval/src/particles3d.rs:540-567`). The added parenthesis covers only `count` and the
   angles.
10. **W09 with the default cell size.** The validator warns only when `cellSize` is written on the object or the asset
    (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:1739-1740`). An object of a `.vox` asset with the default cell size 1 and
    `scale="0.25"` is not warned, although its cells are 0.25 in the scene. srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3204 gives this exact case as
    warned, but its test writes `cellSize="1"` (`crates/sr-model/tests/rules/voxel_rules.rs:117-124`).
11. **Extension case.** VOX2 and rules.rs match `.vox` and `.srvol` with case (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:1884`).
    The loader ignores case (`crates/sr-eval/src/voxel_asset.rs:359`). So `model` on `X.VOX` with no `format` is
    refused by VOX2, but the same file loads.
12. **SRVOL index convention and transform.** For a voxel cache, an index is a cell's corner
    (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/srvol.rs:3-4`). The SRVOL section says integer index coordinates identify voxel centres
    (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3482). A grid transform that is not a pure uniform scale is ignored without a report, and the cell
    size then falls back to the object's or 1 (`crates/sr-3d/src/voxel/srvol.rs:85-89`). SRVOL version 1 has no
    provenance field (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3305-3307).
13. **World scale of fracture owners.** VOX13 reads only the object's own scale. The reference checks the world scale
    at run time for a crater owner (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1236-1240`). No such check was found for a fracture
    owner. The partition uses `scale[0]` to convert the plane units (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1181-1187`), while
    pieces handle non-cubic cells (`crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs:114-117`).
14. **Piece caps the schema does not show.** `fracture/@pieces` allows up to 4,096 seeds, but a stress body takes at
    most 1,024 pieces (`crates/sr-sim/src/physics3d/stress.rs:33`). The component split can also make more pieces than
    `pieces`.
15. **Limits of the stress model.**
    - No stress wave, no elastic energy, and an impact is one step of load.
    - A joint in a cycle takes its plane's cut, not the true load path.
    - Torsion uses the polar moment, which understates a square's largest shear by 13.3%.
    - Friction on a landing or bounce step whose last sub-step has no friction vector is read as zero.
    - The 3.53e4 Pa resting-block artefact (7.6).

    Sources: srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3416-3425.
16. **Limits of the crater on cells.** Ground that is an edge, a bowl, a ridge, or steeper than 30 degrees is
    untested. The "ceiling" rule, which removes cells up to one crest radius above the plane, is stated but not tested
    against the law (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3457; `tools/evidence/cinematic-impact.json`, milestone 65, `limits`, item 4).
17. **Stale engine prose.** srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3344-3346 ("the evaluator ... is the next step") and srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3457 ("the
    renderer does not read what a frame says of them yet, so an object that is cut is still drawn from its asset")
    contradict the code at `4bf7a9e` (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_voxels.rs:8-10`, `:192-217`;
    `crates/sr-eval/tests/voxels/corpus.rs`). srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3368 says thrown particles carry their palette colour; they
    do not (Not implemented, below).

**Unverified.**

- No test, build or render was run for this draft, because the host must not run heavy jobs. Every test outcome and
  measurement quoted here is the engine's own record, read from the source, srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md and commit messages.
- That documents with no cells render exactly as before (from the dispatch code only).
- The convention for a negated axis in the `.vox` rotation. The engine says it is "not checked against a file that
  MagicaVoxel wrote" (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3267-3270).
- `MATL` id equals palette index. The format does not say it; the engine relies on `ogt_vox.h` and two files
  (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3252-3255).
- Crack-free surfaces under T-junctions. Probed only for a ball of radius 6 cells at 64 x 64 (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3194-3198).
- The behaviour in Open issue 6 (white cells), and whether a fracture of cells under a non-uniformly scaled parent
  (item 13) behaves correctly.
- References for Rankine's criterion and Maxwell's Z-model. The engine names both but cites neither.

**Not implemented at `4bf7a9e`.**

- Thrown-cell particles do not carry their palette colour. The palette index stays in the cut, and a particle `Birth`
  has no colour (`crates/sr-sim/src/particles3d.rs:167-173`; ledger milestone 62 and 65, `limits`). They are drawn in
  the `particles3D` colour.
- A moving crater owner's motion is not given to the dust of its cut (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim3d.rs:1603`).
- Dust from impact and stress fractures emits no particles (item 8).
- A physics cache (`physics@cache`, or bake) is refused for any world with bodies of cells (`crates/sr-eval/src/sim.rs:828-832`).
- `surface` has no value other than `blocks`.

None of the features are refused by name: the evaluator wires bodies, craters, impact and stress fractures, bursts and
the drawing of cut bodies (`crates/sr-eval/src/pending.rs`, empty table; `crates/sr-eval/tests/voxels/corpus.rs`).
