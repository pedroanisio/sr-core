# SREP 31 — Add overshoot and period to keys of back and elastic curves

## Abstract

The `back-*` and `elastic-*` curves use fixed constants (the Penner values 1.70158 and a period of 0.3 of the segment),
so an author cannot say how far a move overshoots or how fast it rings. A key gains two optional attributes:
`overshoot` for `back-in`, `back-out` and `back-in-out`, and `period` for `elastic-in`, `elastic-out` and
`elastic-in-out`. Without them the curves are unchanged. Setting one on a segment whose curve does not read it is
reported as an inert attribute.

## Motivation

A reference character-motion test (a four-bar dance phrase at 86 BPM) needed arm overshoots of 1.1 to 1.4 in
Penner's constant, i.e. about 6 % and 14 % of the travel. The engine's `back-out` has one constant that gives about 10 %.
Evaluating the same arm motion as sparse keys with `back-out` and as a per-frame baked curve differed by 4.3° RMS
(max 12.1°), all of it from the fixed constant. The same limit is documented for `elastic-*`. The only free
overshoot today is `cubic-bezier` with y outside [0, 1], which cannot express a back or an elastic shape.

## Specification

### Syntax

```xml
<!-- keyType gains -->
<xs:attribute name="overshoot" type="nonNegativeDecimal">
  <xs:annotation><xs:documentation>The constant of a back-in, back-out or back-in-out segment (default 1.70158, about 10 %
  overshoot; 0 is the plain cubic). Read from the key the segment leaves.</xs:documentation></xs:annotation>
</xs:attribute>
<xs:attribute name="period" type="positiveDecimal">
  <xs:annotation><xs:documentation>The ringing period of an elastic-in, elastic-out or elastic-in-out segment as a fraction
  of the segment (default 0.3; 0.45 for elastic-in-out). Read from the key the segment leaves.</xs:documentation></xs:annotation>
</xs:attribute>
```

New optional attributes with no default, accepted in every version. No Schematron rules.

### Semantics

A key's attributes govern the segment from that key to the next (the leaving-key rule of `keyType`). For progress `u ∈ [0, 1]`:

**`overshoot` = c** (default 1.70158):
- `back-in`: `(c + 1)u³ − c·u²`
- `back-out`: `1 + (c + 1)(u − 1)³ + c(u − 1)²`
- `back-in-out`, with `c₂ = 1.525·c`: for `u < 0.5`, `((2u)²·((c₂ + 1)·2u − c₂)) / 2`; otherwise
  `((2u − 2)²·((c₂ + 1)(2u − 2) + c₂) + 2) / 2`.

With `c = 1.70158` these are Penner's equations; with `c = 0`, `back-out` is `1 − (1 − u)³`.

**`period` = p** (default 0.3 for `elastic-in` and `elastic-out`, 0.45 for `elastic-in-out`), with `w = 2π · 0.1 / p` and
`h = 2.5 · p`; the value is 0 at `u = 0` and 1 at `u = 1`, and otherwise:
- `elastic-in`: `−2^(10u − 10) · sin((10u − 10 − h) · w)`
- `elastic-out`: `2^(−10u) · sin((10u − h) · w) + 1`
- `elastic-in-out`: for `u < 0.5`, `−2^(20u − 10) · sin((20u − 10 − h) · w) / 2`; otherwise
  `2^(−20u + 10) · sin((20u − 10 − h) · w) / 2 + 1`.

With the default periods these are Penner's equations (`w = 2π/3`, `h = 0.75` for 0.3; `w = 2π/4.5`, `h = 1.125` for 0.45).

**Ignored attributes.** `overshoot` on a segment whose curve is not one of the three `back-*` curves, and `period` on one
that is not one of the three `elastic-*` curves, has no effect. A validator or engine MUST report it as an inert
attribute (SREP 18 amendment draft, rule I12 proposed there).

### Defaults and the neutral case

Documents that set neither attribute render as before: the curves are bit-identical to Penner's.

## Rationale

- **On the key, like the other curve parameters.** `bezier`, `steps` and `stiffness` are key attributes read from the
  leaving key; two more follow the same rule.
- **Not new curve names.** Forty-one curves exist; a parameter on three of them is smaller than parameterised names.
- **`overshoot` as Penner's constant**, not a percentage: the constant is what the published equations use, so authors
  coming from easing libraries find the numbers they know. `period` as a fraction of the segment, because the segment is
  the only time base a curve has.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Use `cubic-bezier` with y above 1.** Cannot express the cubic "back" shape or elastic ringing.
- **A percentage overshoot.** Not what the published equations use, and not invertible to a single constant for in-out.

## Backwards compatibility

Class: Added, MINOR in effect, accepted in every version (neutral defaults, SREP 0). No document changes validity or
rendering.

## Conformance

| Case | Checks | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| `srep-NNNN-back-overshoot` | `back-out` at `u = 0.5` over 0 to 100: 108.77 with the default, 87.5 with `overshoot="0"`, 125 with `overshoot="3"` | 0.01 |
| `srep-NNNN-back-in-out` | `back-in-out` is 50 at `u = 0.5` for any constant, and negative at `u = 0.2`, deeper for larger constants | exact |
| `srep-NNNN-elastic-period` | `elastic-out` with the default and with `period="0.6"` at `u = 0.25`: the two values differ and each matches the formula | 0.01 |
