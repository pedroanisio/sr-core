# SREP 78 — Let a pyro window follow its plume, and add blasts

## Abstract

`pyro` gains three optional attributes, `follow`, `followMargin` and `followLoss`. With `follow="true"` the window of an
open smoke domain moves by whole cells, in the object's own axes, to keep the smoke away from the faces it is going
toward. The number of cells, and so the memory and the cost of a step, do not change. `pyro` also gains a child,
`pyroBlast`: a point release of `energy` joules whose front follows the Sedov-Taylor law until the end of its strong
phase, carried by the incompressible smoke solver as the air that a spherical piston displaces. Four rules check the
follow (PYRO9 to PYRO11) and a blast's domain (PYC5); one more, in a new rule context, checks a blast's place (PYC6).
A document without the new attributes and without `pyroBlast` renders as before.

## Motivation

This is one of six SREPs (76 to 81) that take upstream what the reference engine's vendored schema has and sr-core 1.5.0 lacks, so that the engine can vendor sr-core's XSD and Schematron byte for byte again. `conformance/tools/engine_gap.py` measures the gap between sr-core at tag schema-1.5.0 and the engine at commit 4bf7a9e (whose `schema/UPSTREAM` cites schema-1.5.0 at 3f14a20): every element, attribute, enumeration value, documentation block and Schematron pattern, rule, let and assertion the engine has and sr-core lacks or words differently, 222 items, with the engine commit that introduced each one, in `conformance/tools/engine-gap-1.5.0.json`. None of them belongs to SREPs 66 to 75 (the tool checks their declarations and rule ids), the engine has nothing that sr-core has and it lacks, and no new rule id collides with an existing one. This SREP takes 23 of them: 17 XSD items added and 0 changed, 6 Schematron items added and 0 changed.

**The plume that an open domain cuts.** An open face is a zero-pressure outlet: what crosses it is lost
(`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:788-789`). On the engine's hero scene (`examples/cinematic-impact/hero.scene.xml`,
192 x 156 x 192 units in cells of 3, open, 24 steps a second) the top face cuts the plume from about 5 s. The smoke
in the three cells at the top face is 0.30 of 3526 at 5 s and 98 of 2972 at 6 s (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:839-842`).
A domain of 468 units shows the uncut plume: at 6 s the smoke above a thousandth of the peak spans 145 units
(`:842-844`). Holding that plume with a fixed window and a 12-cell margin needs about 240 units, 64 x 80 x 64 cells
against 64 x 52 x 64: 54 % more cells, with the memory and step cost that go with them (`:848-850`).

With `follow="true" followMargin="3" followLoss="0.001"` the same 52-row window holds the plume: at 5.96 s the smoke
in the three cells at the top face is 0.17 of 2999, against 88.5 of 2996 without follow (`:845-848`). In the
evaluator test, a blob in an 8 x 60 x 8 domain accelerated upward at 6 units/s² is gone from the fixed domain at
5 s; the following domain holds it 14 units above the old top face (`:835-837`;
`crates/sr-eval/tests/volume/pyro_follow.rs:49-55`, which asserts only that smoke is more than 5 units above it).

**The margin and W02.** SREP 40 warns (`W02`) when a pyro source is less than 12 cells from an open face. It measured
the peak density of the hero plume at 6 s as 0.307, 0.476, 0.525, 0.673, 0.675, 0.688 and 0.686 with the source
3, 5, 7, 13, 19, 33 and 59 cells from the bottom face (SREP 40, `srep/srep-0040.md:739-750`). The engine's default
`followMargin` is that same 12 cells (`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:115-117`).

**A blast with no physical size.** Before this SREP the only way to push air out of a point was `expansion`, "an
authored cinematic divergence source, not a shock-physics equation of state" (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:769-770`).
The author had to guess its size and duration. `pyroBlast` derives the displaced volume from an energy and an
ambient gas instead. No scene in the engine's examples uses `pyroBlast` at 4bf7a9e (checked with `git grep`), so
there is no scene measurement for this half.

## Specification

The key words MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD and MAY are used as in RFC 2119. "The domain" is the grid of a `pyro` element:
n_a cells on axis a (a = x, y, z) of side h = `voxelSize`, in the volume object's own axes. A "slab" of axis a is the
set of cells with one index i along a.

### Semantics

#### 1. A window that follows the plume

**1.1 Activation.** The window follows when `follow` is `true` or `1`. When `follow` is absent or `false` the
window MUST NOT move (`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:116`). With follow, the margin m is `followMargin` (12 when
absent) and the loss L is `followLoss` (0 when absent) (`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:117`). The engine defaults are not
XSD defaults, so that PYRO10 can refuse them without follow (XSD annotations of `followMargin` and `followLoss`).

**1.2 Where the window is.** At the start the domain is centred on the object: its origin is
b = -(width, height, depth) / 2 in the object's axes (`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:93`). The state holds an integer
offset w (whole cells, initially 0). The origin MUST be computed afresh as o = b + w h, never accumulated
(`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:613-616`, `:958-959`). The window moves in the object's own axes.

**1.3 When the decision is made.** Before each fixed step, a renderer MUST decide the move from the state committed by
the previous step and from the sources acting in the coming step. If the window moved, it MUST sample that step's
inputs again, so that they are those of the window the step has (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1414-1429`, `:2254-2259`).
The decision MUST NOT depend on the order or history of time requests.

**1.4 The decision on one axis.** For each axis a, with sums taken serially in cell-index order (x fastest), so that
the result does not depend on the thread count (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:798-825`):

- M[i] = sum of the density of slab i.
- H[i] = number of cells of slab i with |T - T_amb| > 1e-9 T_amb. Smaller differences are rounding, not heat
  (`:31-33`, `:811`).
- F[i] = sum over the cells of slab i with nonzero density ρ of ρ ū, where ū is the mean of the cell's two face
  velocities along a (`:815-821`).
- D = Σ_i M[i], the total density; A = (L / 2) D (`:838-839`).

*The smoke's span.* Walk from the low end. Slab i is trimmed while, for L = 0, M[i] = 0 and H[i] = 0, or, for L > 0,
(density trimmed so far) + M[i] ≤ A. The first slab not trimmed is lo. If every slab is trimmed, the axis has no
smoke and does not move. Walk from the high end the same way, with its own running total, not past lo; the first
slab not trimmed is hi (`:843-863`).

*The reach of the fastest air.* r = min(ceil(max|u_a| dt / h), max(0, floor((n_a - 1) / 2) - m)), where the maximum
is over all faces of the velocity component along a. A non-finite value gives r = 0 (`:826-837`). The width kept
from a face is W = m + r (`:864`).

*Which faces ask for room.* The low face asks for room only if Σ F[lo .. min(lo + W, hi)] < 0. The high face asks
only if Σ F[max(hi - W, lo) .. hi] > 0. Both ranges are inclusive. Smoke at rest asks for none (`:865-871`).

*The move.* need_low = max(W - lo, 0) if the low face asks, else 0. need_high = max(hi + W + 1 - n_a, 0) if the high
face asks, else 0 (`:873-876`).

- Both nonzero: the axis is blocked. It MUST NOT move, and the smoke is cut as without follow (`:877-878`).
- need_low > 0: the window moves toward the low face by k = keep(min(need_low, n_a - 1 - hi)) cells, counting the
  slabs to leave behind from the high end (`:879-883`).
- need_high > 0: the window moves toward the high face by k = keep(min(need_high, lo)) cells, counting from the low
  end (`:884-887`).

*The source rule.* keep(k) is the number of slabs, out of the first k to be left behind, before the first one that
holds a cell whose centre lies in the shape of a source acting in the step. A window MUST NOT leave such a slab,
whatever the loss (`:893-916`). The acting sources are each `pyroSource` (including those of a crater) whose active
interval overlaps the step with positive length, and each `pyroImpulse` that fires in the step
(`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:516-530`). A `pyroBlast` is not an acting source.

**1.5 Applying the move.** The three axes move together by one integer vector s. The cell that was at c + s is at c.
Kept cells MUST keep their values bit for bit. Cells that come in take the background of an open face: density 0,
temperature T_amb, air at rest (`:918-920`, `:972-974`). The density in the cells left behind MUST be added to the
state's lost density, which is never silent (`:928-938`, `:968`; XSD annotation of `followLoss`). Then w becomes
w + s (`:958`).

**1.6 Bound on the loss.** Each move leaves behind only slabs that the span walk trimmed, at one end per axis. A move
therefore lets go of at most (L / 2) D per axis, and at most 1.5 L D over the three axes. The reference test asserts
the 1.5 L bound, and that the density before a move equals the density after it plus what it let go of, to 1e-12
relative (`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/follow.rs:209-232`). With L = 0 only slabs with no density and no heat are let go
of, so nothing is lost. A plume's numerical tail is almost never exactly zero, so a window with L = 0 rarely moves
(`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:824-831`; `crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/follow.rs:198`).

**1.7 Turbulence belongs to the cell of space.** With follow, the seeded turbulence of cell c MUST be keyed by the cell
of space g = c + w, not by the cell's index in the window (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1752-1755`). The key is:

- if 0 ≤ g_a < n_a on every axis: the index of g in the starting box, g_x + n_x (g_y + n_y g_z). This is the key the
  cell has without follow, so a following window that has not moved has the same noise as a window without follow;
- otherwise: 2^63 | p(g_x) | p(g_y) << 21 | p(g_z) << 42, with p(v) = (v + 2^20) & 0x1FFFFF
  (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1734-1745`).

Without follow the key is the cell's index in the window, as before (`:1755`).

**1.8 Determinism under seeking and checkpoints.** The offset w and the lost density are part of the state, so they
are in every checkpoint and in the state's equality (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:609-619`, `:2263-2273`). A seek
backward MUST restore them and replay the same moves. Any order of requested times and a fresh evaluator MUST give the
same states (`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/follow.rs:295-315`; `crates/sr-eval/tests/volume/pyro_follow.rs:66`).

**1.9 Placement of the exported volume.** The exported grids MUST be placed by the state's current origin o, so the
smoke does not move in the world when the window moves (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1068-1070`). A baked sequence of a
following plume keeps each frame's transform (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:819-822`).

**1.10 Limits (informative).** A plume has to fit in its window, stem and diffused tail included. A plume that does not
fit is cut as before; the remedy is a larger window (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:867-872`; XSD annotation of
`follow`). The decision reads every cell once a step. The engine measured its cost at 2.2 % of a step at
128 x 104 x 128 and 2.4 % at 192 x 156 x 192 (`:872-875`).

#### 2. A blast in the smoke (`pyroBlast`)

**2.1 Inputs.** E = `energy` (J), t_b = `time` (s, on the simulation clock, as for `pyroImpulse`), c = (`x`, `y`,
`z`) in the pyro's own axes (the domain's coordinates, centred on the object at the start), ρ0 = `ambientDensity`
(kg/m³), p0 = `ambientPressure` (Pa), γ = `gamma` (`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:272-283`;
`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:389-392`). The object's `animationSpeed` and `animationOffset` map its local time into
simulation time, as for every pyro input (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:704-705`).

**2.2 Metres to the volume's units.** Let ppm be `physics/@pixelsPerMeter`, or 100 when there is no `physics` element
(`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:269`; XSD default of `pixelsPerMeter` is 100). Let M be the linear part of the map from
scene space to the volume's own space in the frame sampled for the step. The images M(ppm e_x), M(ppm e_y) and
M(ppm e_z) MUST have one length k, within 1e-6 relative, and be pairwise orthogonal, within 1e-6 k². Otherwise the
volume has no sphere, and a renderer MUST report an error. This refuses a shear as well as a stretch
(`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:246-256`, `:268-271`). Then one metre is k volume units. Radii are computed in metres and
multiplied by k. Divergence is volume per volume per second and needs no conversion
(`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:917-919`).

**2.3 The constant of the front.** The front is R(τ) = ξ0 (E τ² / ρ0)^(1/5) metres, τ seconds after the release
(`crates/sr-sim/src/sedov.rs:159-162`). ξ0 depends only on γ. It MUST be obtained from the self-similar solution, not
from a table. The reference procedure is `crates/sr-sim/src/sedov.rs:36-132`:

1. Unknowns, with ξ = r / R: u = Ṙ U(ξ), ρ = ρ0 G(ξ), p = ρ0 Ṙ² P(ξ) (`:4-5`).
2. Equations (mass, momentum, entropy):
   (U - ξ) G' + G (U' + 2U/ξ) = 0; (U - ξ) U' - 3U/2 + P'/G = 0; (U - ξ)(P'/P - γ G'/G) = 3.
   Eliminated: with c² = P/G,
   U' = [3U(U - ξ)/2 - 3c² + 2γ c² U/ξ] / [(U - ξ)² - γ c²],
   (ln G)' = -(U' + 2U/ξ) / (U - ξ), (ln P)' = γ (ln G)' + 3 / (U - ξ) (`:54-65`).
3. Start at the shock, ξ = 1: U = 2/(γ+1), G = (γ+1)/(γ-1), P = 2/(γ+1) (`:6-7`, `:79`).
4. Integrate in x = ln ξ from 0 to ln(1e-5) by classical fourth-order Runge-Kutta, 40 000 equal steps, on
   (U, ln G, ln P) (`:28-32`, `:76-100`).
5. Over each step, add by Simpson's rule the integrals, in x (ξ² dξ = ξ³ dx), of G ξ² (mass), G U² ξ² / 2 (kinetic)
   and P ξ² / (γ - 1) (thermal). The midpoint state is the cubic Hermite value (y0 + y1)/2 + (h/8)(k1 - k4), with h the
   (negative) step and k1, k4 the slopes at the ends (`:81-108`).
6. Add the tail below ξ = 1e-5 to the thermal integral: P(1e-5) (1e-5)³ / 3 / (γ - 1) (`:115-117`).
7. J = kinetic + thermal; ξ0 = (25 / (16 π J))^(1/5) (`:118-120`). This follows from
   E = (16π/25) J ρ0 R⁵ / τ² (`:8-9`).
8. If any state is not finite, or ξ0 is not finite, the gas cannot be solved: an error (`:111-113`, `:121-123`).

The mass integral is not used; it is the check (1/3, to 1e-6 for γ = 1.2, 1.4, 5/3:
`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/sedov.rs:23-37`). The solution is computed once per γ and reused
(`crates/sr-sim/src/sedov.rs:40-52`). The reference's tests pin ξ0(1.4) = 1.0328 to 5e-4
(`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/sedov.rs:17-18`). The engine's draft gives ξ0(5/3) = 1.1517
(`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:889`); the test checks it only to 2 % of 1.15 (`sedov.rs` test `:13-14`).

**2.4 The end of the strong phase.** The front stops at R_max = 0.3 (E / p0)^(1/3) metres and stays there
(`crates/sr-sim/src/sedov.rs:186-190`; `crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:466-469`). So, in volume units,
R_v(τ) = k min(ξ0 (E τ²/ρ0)^(1/5), 0.3 (E/p0)^(1/3)), and R_v(τ) = 0 if τ ≤ 0 or E = 0
(`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:461-471`). At R_max the strong-shock pressure 2 ρ0 D² / (γ + 1), with D = 2R / (5τ), is
11.85 ξ0⁵ / (γ + 1) times p0: 5.8 for air (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:891-893`; checked by derivation:
8 ξ0⁵ / (25 · 0.027 (γ + 1)) = 11.85 ξ0⁵ / (γ + 1)). The 0.3 is the engine's choice, with no published value (`:893-895`).

**2.5 The radii of a step.** Let s_b be the fixed step that contains t_b: the step index of t_b / dt, with the
boundary roundoff correction of `pyroImpulse` (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:476`, `:2280-2285`;
`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:615-617`). For step s with s < s_b, nothing happens. Otherwise
r1 = R_v((s + 1) dt - t_b), and r0 = 0 if s = s_b, else R_v(s dt - t_b) (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:473-483`). If
r1 > r0 the front sweeps V = (4π/3)(r1³ - r0³) cubic volume units in the step; otherwise nothing happens
(`:485-490`). Over the strong phase the swept volumes sum to the sphere of R_max (`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/blast.rs:63-65`).

**2.6 The divergence source of a step.** For each blast that sweeps V in step s (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1531-1540`,
`:2349-2396`):

- The reach is ρ_r = max(r1, h): a front smaller than a cell is the sphere of one cell, and V is still the volume swept
  (`:1538`).
- The given cells are the non-solid cells of the window whose centres p satisfy |p - c|² ≤ ρ_r² (`:2358-2361`). If
  there is none, the blast does nothing in that step (`:2362-2364`).
- The share N is the number of cells that share V. If the sphere's bounding box lies inside the window, N is the
  number of given cells. Otherwise N = max(N_lattice - N_solid, N_given), where N_lattice is the number of centres
  o + (i + ½) h of the infinite lattice inside the sphere, by the same test, and N_solid is the number of solid window
  cells inside it (`:2365-2373`). When 2 ρ_r / h > 30 000, N_lattice = floor((4π/3)(ρ_r/h)³) (`:2398-2404`).
- Each given cell's target divergence is increased by d = (V / dt) / (N h³) (1/s) (`:2374-2380`). The air that the whole
  sphere lets out is then exactly V, whatever the cells make of the sphere.
- A starting velocity, the analytic flow of the piston, is added on every face of the window. At a face centre p with
  δ = p - c and r = |δ|: u_a += (d / 3) δ_a if r ≤ ρ_r, else u_a += Q / (4π r³) δ_a, with Q = V / dt (`:2381-2395`).
  Several blasts in one step add.

**2.7 Order within a step.** A step with at least one blast that sweeps air MUST run as follows
(`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1473-1598`):

1. the smoke's own step: advection, sources and impulses, forces, projection (`:1473`, `:1476-1530`, `:1542`, `:1550`);
2. the blast flow: a copy of the state with all face velocities set to zero, the pistons of §2.6 added, and every solid
   face at rest. What a moving collider does to the air belongs to the smoke's own flow (`:1559-1577`);
3. the projection of that flow alone, to the blasts' target divergence, with open faces at zero pressure and the
   pyro's own solver, iteration limit and tolerance (`:1578`). The projection is linear, so this flow is the blasts'
   share of the step's flow;
4. the density and the temperature carried once over dt by that flow, with the pyro's advection scheme and no
   dissipation or cooling. The smoke's step has already applied those (`:1580-1592`);
5. the blast flow is discarded. The velocity of the state MUST be the velocity of step 1, bit for bit
   (`:1554-1557`, `:1597-1598`; `crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/blast.rs:619`). If it were kept, the potential flow would
   never decay: a projection with no divergence does not remove it when the faces are open
   (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:905-907`).

A blast heats nothing and makes no smoke (XSD annotation of `pyroBlastType`; `crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:403-404`).

**2.8 A blast in a following window.** The blast's centre is fixed in the object's axes, not in the window
(`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:2354-2359` uses the state's current origin). A window that the sphere cuts, or that is not
centred on the blast, is pushed from the blast and not from its own middle (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:907-908`).
A move of the window drops the blast flow of the last step, which was indexed on the old window
(`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1424-1426`).

**2.9 Limits (informative).** No shock, no sound and no overpressure field are carried. The interior flow is that of a
uniform divergence, not Sedov's profile. After R_max nothing is modelled. The smoke's own velocity gets no impulse
from the blast (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:954-959`). The strong phase ends within the first step whenever
E < E* = [ξ0 (dt²/ρ0)^(1/5) p0^(1/3) / 0.3]^(15/2), which scales as dt³: 1.91e12 J at dt = 1/24 s and 2.64e10 J at
1/100 s (`:923-926`; pinned to 1e-3 by `crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/blast.rs:89-94`). Below E* the blast is one pulse of
the sphere of R_max. There are no sub-steps: the projected flow depends on the volume swept, not on its history
within the step (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:925-926`).

### Errors and warnings

Schema rules (the Schematron in the patch; the Rust twin is `crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:1471-1488`, `:1569-1581`):

- **PYRO9**: a pyro with `follow` true or 1 needs `boundary="open"`.
- **PYRO10**: `followMargin` and `followLoss` need `follow` true or 1. `follow="false"` with either one is invalid.
- **PYRO11**: when `followMargin` is given, 2 · followMargin · voxelSize < width, height and depth. At least one cell
  is left between the margins on every axis.
- **PYC5** (context `pyro`): a pyro with a `pyroBlast` child needs `boundary="open"`. A blast is a source of
  divergence that a closed domain cannot let out.
- **PYC6** (new context `pyroBlast`): each given coordinate lies in [-size/2, +size/2] of the parent's width, height
  or depth. The check is against the domain as it is at the start; a window that has followed may since have moved.

**W02** (warning, SREP 40). W02 is not reported for a source or impulse of a pyro whose `follow` is true or 1
(`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:538-544`; test `crates/sr-model/tests/rules/pyro_rules.rs:196-204`). The reason given
in commit 162a8ba is that a following window keeps the smoke from its faces. W02 never applies to `pyroBlast`: its
contexts are `pyroSource` and `pyroImpulse` (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:1582-1583`).

Runtime errors. A renderer MUST report these. A failed step MUST leave the previous state and clock unchanged
(`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:627-629`).

- A follow with a loss outside [0, 1], a closed domain, a margin of 0, or a margin with 2m ≥ n_a on some axis is
  refused when the simulation is built (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1342-1353`). This includes the engine default
  m = 12 when `followMargin` is absent, which PYRO11 does not check (see Open issues).
- A move of the window by n_a or more cells on an axis (`:924-926`). A window that has gone so far that its origin
  cannot represent a voxel centre (`:958-962`).
- A blast in a domain that is not open, if one reaches the solver (`:1533-1536`).
- A blast with a place that is not finite or a negative time (`:427-429`); a negative or non-finite energy, or an air
  of non-positive density (`:430-432`); a non-positive pressure or `pixelsPerMeter` (`:433-439`).
- A gas that cannot be solved: "a blast needs a ratio of specific heats from 1.1 to 3" (`:440-441`). The solver refuses
  γ outside [1.1, 3] or non-finite (`crates/sr-sim/src/sedov.rs:70-72`), an integration that leaves the finite numbers
  (`:111-113`), and a non-finite ξ0 (`:121-123`). Commit 361c88f records that γ = 1.05 lost the integration and
  produced NaN after the schema had accepted it. The least γ became 1.1 in the solver, the schema and the blast.
- A volume that is not scaled the same on every axis, or is sheared: "a blast needs a volume that is scaled the same
  on every axis and not sheared" (`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:270-271`; tests
  `crates/sr-eval/tests/volume/pyro_blast.rs:77-104`).
- An unconverged projection of the blast flow fails the step, as any unconverged projection does
  (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1578`; `srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:627-629`, `:659-662`).

### Defaults and the neutral case

- `follow` absent or `false`: the window never moves and the turbulence key is the cell's index. The state is the one
  before this SREP (`crates/sr-eval/tests/volume/pyro_follow.rs:58-63`).
- `follow="true"` on a window that never moves: bit for bit the state without follow, seeded turbulence included
  (`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/follow.rs:259-292`; `crates/sr-sim/src/pyro/window.rs:78-85`).
- `followMargin` absent with follow: 12 cells. `followLoss` absent with follow: 0, so only empty, unheated slabs are
  let go of (`crates/sr-eval/src/pyro.rs:117`).
- No `pyroBlast`: no pulse, and the step is the step before this SREP. A blast with `energy="0"`, or one released after
  the last simulated step, changes nothing, bit for bit (`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/blast.rs:167-198`;
  `crates/sr-eval/tests/volume/pyro_blast.rs:66-74`).
- `pyroBlast` attribute defaults: x = y = z = 0 (the domain centre at the start), `ambientDensity` 1.2,
  `ambientPressure` 101325, `gamma` 1.4 (XSD).

## Rationale

**Follow.**

- *Same cells, same cost.* The alternative is a larger window: 54 % more cells for the hero plume
  (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:848-850`). Follow is "the saving in cells for a plume that has left its source
  behind, not a free height" (`:869-870`).
- *Whole cells.* A move is a copy: kept cells are bit for bit and nothing is resampled (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:918-920`).
- *Decided from the state.* Seeking, checkpoints and thread count cannot change where the window is (§1.3, §1.8).
- *Room only where the smoke goes.* A plume rising away from a face is no reason to move toward that face. The oracle
  test is `a_window_does_not_move_against_the_drift_of_its_smoke_to_make_room_behind_it`
  (`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/follow.rs:355-368`; `srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:802-806`).
- *The source slab.* A plume begins at its source. A window that left the source would part the plume from it
  (`:813-815`).
- *Loss default 0, always counted.* No smoke is let go of unless the author allows it, and what is let go of is in
  the state (`:824-835`). The margin of 12 and the loss are engine values with no published source (`:872`).

**Blast.**

- *The law is computed, not quoted.* ξ0 for any γ in [1.1, 3] comes from the same integration, checked against
  conserved mass and energy (`crates/sr-sim/src/sedov.rs:1-10`; `crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/sedov.rs:23-37`).
  The values for air and for a monatomic gas are compared with the literature the engine's tests cite: Sedov (1959) and
  Landau and Lifshitz, *Fluid Mechanics*, §106 (`crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/sedov.rs:1-20`). The engine gives no DOI or
  URL for either (see Unverified).
- *A piston, because the solver is incompressible.* The smoke solver cannot carry a shock. What it can carry exactly is
  the displacement of the air by the front (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:896-900`). The divergence is spread so
  that the outflow is the swept volume to 1e-6. That holds for a 1 J blast too: 1.1e-6 m³, not the volume of a cell
  (`:897-900`; commit 361c88f).
- *The whole sphere shares the volume.* A window that cuts the sphere then has the divergence and flow of the sphere
  in free space, to 5 % in the test (`:900-902`, `:934-937`; commit 8c01652).
- *The flow is not kept.* See §2.7.5.
- *Units through `pixelsPerMeter`.* The same blast moves smoke the same distance in metres at 1, 37 and 100 scene units
  to the metre (`crates/sr-eval/tests/volume/pyro_blast.rs:46-64`; `crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/blast.rs:110-128`).

The pressure projection and advection reused here follow Bridson and Müller-Fischer's SIGGRAPH 2007 fluid notes,
<https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rbridson/fluidsimulation/>, as cited for the pyro solver (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:623-624`).

## Rejected alternatives

From the engine's history:

- **A larger fixed window.** Kept as the remedy when a plume does not fit, not as the default: 54 % more cells on
  the hero scene (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:848-850`, `:869-870`).
- **Asking for room on every face** (d87fc65). The expanding fireball moved the window toward its bottom face early.
  An intermediate policy (cfc88fe) asked only on the face that the smoke's drift pointed to, when the drift was at
  least a fifth of its speed. It was replaced by the per-end flux test (80f70d5)
  (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:806-810`; commit messages of cfc88fe and 80f70d5). The engine's draft withdraws a
  22-unit figure that it had given for the first policy (`:809-810`).
- **Noise keyed by the cell of the window** (d87fc65), then **by the cell of space for every cell** (8ad805c). The
  first made the noise at a point change when the window moved. The second gave a following window other bits even
  before it moved. Replaced by §1.7 (8c30823) (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:856-861`; commit message of 8c30823).
- **A move that refuses to leave any smoke.** The first window (3b15c37) refused a move that would leave smoke behind.
  A plume's tail is never exactly zero, so it barely moved: a loss of zero kept 0.38 of 164.3 (commit message of
  931addb). `followLoss` replaced it. The refusing form remains as the solver's manual shift
  (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1405-1413`).
- **Divergence of 1 in the covered cells** (before 361c88f). The displaced volume was always 7 to 8 cells: 1 J
  displaced 0.9 m³ (commit message of 361c88f).
- **Divergence without the analytic piston flow** (before 361c88f). The projection made the flow come from the
  middle of the window, not from the blast (commit message of 361c88f).
- **Sharing by the cells in the window** (before 8c01652). The divergence was 1.67 times the free-space value for a
  cut sphere, and 5.8 times for a window inside the sphere (commit message of 8c01652).
- **Keeping the blast flow in the velocity** (361c88f, replaced by 8c01652). It left 50 to 200 % of the pulse a step
  after the front stopped (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:905-907`; commit message of 8c01652).
- **Carrying with decay and cooling, and solids moving for the blast flow** (before 45dcad1). Decay and cooling were
  applied twice, and a moving collider's flow was counted twice (commit message of 45dcad1).
- **Copying the whole state for the carry** (before 4b50406). 248 bytes a cell at peak against 206 now
  (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:913-915`; commit message of 4b50406).
- **γ down to 1.05** (f1269d9). The integration was lost, so the minimum is now 1.1 (361c88f).
- **Sub-steps for the blast.** Not used: they would not change the projected flow (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:925-926`).

## Backwards compatibility

Every document valid under sr-core 1.5.0 (after SREPs 76-77) stays valid. The change adds three optional attributes
and one optional element, and touches no existing default. No valid document renders differently. Without `follow`
the window and the turbulence key are unchanged. Without `pyroBlast` the step is unchanged. Both are bit-for-bit
tested (see Defaults and the neutral case). The change is MINOR.

Version gate: this SREP adds none. `pyro` is already gated by SREP 40's V8, which allows it only in
`version="1.3"` documents. So follow and blasts are usable only there, and the `pyroBlastType` annotation says
"version 1.3". See Open issues.

## Open issues

- **Version gate (all of SREPs 76 to 80).** The engine's text gates these features on `scene/@version="1.3"` exactly: BH1 and VOX1 directly, and the attributes on SREP 40 elements through SREP 40's own gates (V8 for volumes, media and pyro, CRT1, FRX1, P3D1, OCN1), which are already in sr-core 1.5.0 in that form. A document that uses them cannot declare 1.4, 1.5 or 1.6, so it cannot also use the features those versions gate (SREPs 57 to 75). SREPs 66 to 75 gate their features on 1.6 with an explicit list of the earlier versions. This draft keeps the engine's text verbatim, because the aim is a byte-equal vendoring; the review should decide whether the 1.3 gates become "1.3 or later" (a separate SREP that would also amend SREP 40). **Engine change:** yes, if the gates change: the engine mirrors every Schematron rule natively (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs`), so it must change those checks and its corpus, then re-vendor.

- **Version gate (flagged for review).** `pyro` is a SREP 40 element. Its gate V8 (`/scene[@version!='1.3']`
  forbids `.//pyro`) admits only `version="1.3"`. This SREP targets schema 1.6, and SREPs 66-75 gate on 1.6. A 1.6
  document cannot contain `pyro`, so it cannot use follow or blasts either. The `pyroBlastType` annotation says
  "(version 1.3)". The patch text is kept verbatim. The editor should decide whether V8 changes, under this SREP or
  another.
- **`followLoss` "each side" against the code.** The annotation says the share may be let go of "each side". The
  reference trims at most L/2 of all density at each end of an axis, so a move lets go of at most L/2 per axis and
  1.5 L in all (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:839`, `:848`; `crates/sr-sim/tests/pyro/follow.rs:227`). The engine's draft
  says both "half of followLoss ... each" (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:800`) and "on each side" (`:824`). §1.4 and
  §1.6 follow the code.
- **The 12 cells: top face or bottom face.** The `followMargin` annotation and the engine's draft
  (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:790-791`) say 12 is where "the top face" was measured to have no effect. W02's
  measurement varied the bottom face (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:513-515`; SREP 40 `srep/srep-0040.md:747-749`). The
  top face was moved 26 cells, and that text says "nothing changes (peak 0.481 against 0.525)", which is itself an 8 %
  change. The annotation should probably say "bottom".
- **Default margin not checked by the schema.** PYRO11 applies only when `followMargin` is given. A document with
  `follow="true"`, no `followMargin`, and an axis of fewer than 25 cells is valid, but the reference refuses it when
  the simulation is built (2 · 12 ≥ n_a; `crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:1349-1352`). Options: extend PYRO11 to the
  default 12, or document the refusal.
- **The `follow` annotation predates the face policy.** It says the smoke is kept "from each face" (162a8ba). From
  cfc88fe and 80f70d5 on, a face asks for room only when the smoke nearest it moves toward it (§1.4).
- **The `pyroBlastType` annotation is less specific than the code.** It does not say that solid cells take none of
  the volume, that the sphere has at least the radius of one cell, that the analytic piston flow is added before the
  projection, or that the flow carries the smoke once and is not kept. Nothing in it contradicts the code; §2.6-§2.7
  give the details.
- **Heat let go of is not counted.** With L > 0 a slab with heat and no density can be trimmed, and only density
  enters the lost total (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:844-849`, `:937`).
- **A blast and a following window.** A blast is not an acting source for the source rule (§1.4), so a window may
  leave a blast's centre behind. PYC6 checks the place only against the starting domain. A sphere that holds no free
  cell of the window does nothing in that step (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs:2362-2364`). Whether this is intended is
  not recorded.
- **W02 under follow** is suppressed even when the window is blocked or cannot move (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:539`).
- **Cross-engine tolerance for ξ0.** Another quadrature gives other last bits. The tolerance a second engine must meet
  is not set.
- **Not done** (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:959-960`): coupling of the blast to rigid bodies and to the ocean.

- ξ0(5/3) = 1.1517 and the central-pressure ratio 0.3062 are the engine draft's figures (`:889-891`). The tests check

- "An independent integration in Python agrees to 2e-6" (`srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:891`): no such script is in
  the repository at 4bf7a9e.
