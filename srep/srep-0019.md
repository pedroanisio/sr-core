# SREP 19 — Add legibility checks for video text and captions

## Abstract

`accessibility` gains a legibility check: reading speed in characters per second, a minimum display time
and a minimum text size, measured on what the output actually draws and reported through the render report
of [SREP 18](srep-0018.md) as `LEG-SPEED`, `LEG-SHORT` and `LEG-SIZE`. Reading speed can be set per caption
track, because the right limit depends on the language and the audience. The check is off by default.

## Motivation

- **Captions outrun readers.** The 65 production scenes on the maintainers' machines carry 1,315 caption
  cues:
  - median speed 16.3 characters per second; 90th percentile 19.1;
  - 44 of the 278 Portuguese cues exceed 17 cps, the limit Netflix's Brazilian Portuguese guide sets for
    adult subtitles;
  - 49 of the 1,037 English cues exceed 20 cps, its English (USA) limit;
  - 31 cues are shown for less than the 5/6-second minimum of Netflix's general requirements.
- **The format can ask for captions, not for readable ones.** `requireCaptions` and `contrastCheck` exist;
  nothing checks speed, display time or size.
- **Text size varies widely.** The production scenes set text at a median of 2.78% of the frame's short
  side, and a tenth of it below 1.67%.

## Specification

### Syntax

```xml
<!-- accessibilityType gains -->
<xs:attribute name="legibilityCheck" default="off">
  <xs:simpleType><xs:restriction base="xs:string">
    <xs:enumeration value="off"/><xs:enumeration value="warn"/><xs:enumeration value="error"/>
  </xs:restriction></xs:simpleType>
</xs:attribute>
<xs:attribute name="readingSpeed" type="positiveDecimal"/>
<xs:attribute name="minDisplayTime" type="nonNegativeDecimal" default="0.8333"/>
<xs:attribute name="minTextSize" type="lengthType"/>

<!-- captionTrackType gains -->
<xs:attribute name="readingSpeed" type="positiveDecimal">
  <xs:annotation><xs:documentation>
    Characters per second this track may reach; overrides accessibility/@readingSpeed.
  </xs:documentation></xs:annotation>
</xs:attribute>
```

New attributes with neutral defaults, accepted in every version. No Schematron rules.

### Semantics

When `legibilityCheck` is `warn` or `error`, the engine checks every caption cue and every text layer drawn
in the output, and reports at that severity.

1. **Visible time.** Let V be the part of the output's time range in which the text is drawn with nonzero
   opacity. For a caption cue, that is the cue's [start, end); for a text layer, its window with its
   ancestors' windows and clocks applied.
2. **Characters.** Let N be the number of Unicode code points of its text after NFC, not counting line
   breaks.
3. **`LEG-SPEED`:** N / |V| > the limit, in characters per second. For a cue, the limit is its track's
   `readingSpeed`, else `accessibility/@readingSpeed`; for a text layer, `accessibility/@readingSpeed`.
   Without a limit there is no check.
4. **`LEG-SHORT`:** |V| < `minDisplayTime`, in seconds.
5. **`LEG-SIZE`**, when `minTextSize` is set:
   - the drawn size is the text's `size` (after `autoFit`) times the layer's world scale, √|det| of its 2D
     world matrix, in output pixels;
   - it is reported when below `minTextSize`, resolved against the output frame (`vh`, `vmin`, …, or
     pixels);
   - one finding per layer, with the time range in which the size was below.
6. Text revealed progressively by a `textAnimator` is measured over its whole visible time.
7. Burned-in captions are measured as laid out; sidecar captions from their cues.

Findings carry `measured` and `limit`, in `cps`, `s` or `px`.

### Defaults and the neutral case

`legibilityCheck="off"` measures nothing. The other attributes only set thresholds, and findings never
change pixels.

## Rationale

- **Thresholds come from subtitle practice.**
  - Netflix's general requirements set a minimum event duration of 5/6 of a second: the default of
    `minDisplayTime`.
  - Its reading-speed limits depend on language and audience: 17 cps for adult Brazilian Portuguese
    subtitles (13 for children, 20 for SDH), 20 for adult English (USA) subtitles (17 for children). So
    `readingSpeed` has no default, and a caption track can set its own.
- **`minTextSize` has no default.** The right floor depends on the delivery target (a phone held upright,
  a television across a room), which the brief decides and the format cannot assume.
- **Measured on the render.** A text layer's drawn size includes its scale and the output's size, which
  the document alone does not give.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Legibility errors by default.** 93 production cues (7%) would fail at their language's limit, and a new
  default must not break renders.
- **A fixed `readingSpeed` or `minTextSize`.** Both depend on the language, the audience and the target.
- **Grapheme clusters instead of code points.** More exact for scripts with combining marks, but UAX #29
  segmentation would have to agree across four engines; see Open issues.

## Backwards compatibility

- **Class: Added, MINOR (1.2).** New attributes with neutral defaults, accepted in every version.
- No valid document becomes invalid, and none renders differently.
- **Evidence.** The XSD changes were applied to a copy of the canonical schema, and the 2,600 local scene
  documents that declare 1.0 or 1.1 were validated with both copies. No verdict changed.

## Conformance

| Case | Checks | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| `srep-0019-speed` | a caption track with `readingSpeed="20"` and one cue of 30 characters over 1 s; `legibilityCheck="warn"`; `output/@report` | `LEG-SPEED`, severity `warning`, `measured` 30 ± 0.1, `limit` 20 |
| `srep-0019-short` | one cue of 0.5 s | `LEG-SHORT`, `measured` 0.5 |
| `srep-0019-size` | a 1080-pixel-high output, `minTextSize="3vh"`, a text layer of `size="24"` at scale 1 | `LEG-SIZE`, `measured` 24, `limit` 32.4 |
| `srep-0019-off` | the `speed` case with `legibilityCheck="off"` | no `LEG-*` finding |

## Open issues

- **Counting characters.** Code points after NFC over-count scripts with combining marks relative to grapheme
  clusters (Unicode UAX #29). Whether Netflix's counts include spaces is not stated in the guides consulted;
  this SREP counts them.
