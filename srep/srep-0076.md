# SREP 76 — Add a Schwarzschild black hole and a geodesic camera

## Abstract

Two nodes, `blackHole` and `accretionDisk`, and one camera attribute, `camera/@geodesics`, let a document show a
non-rotating black hole: its shadow, the lensed sky and a thin, opaque, Keplerian disk of gas whose colour is a black
body at the redshifted local temperature. A camera with `geodesics="true"` traces each ray along a null geodesic of
the Schwarzschild metric instead of rasterising or path tracing; with it the scene may contain no other 3D content
(BH6). Eight Schematron rules (BH1 to BH8) bound what a renderer can draw, and three validator warnings report a hole
nobody draws, a denoise that is not done and lights that are not used. Without the new elements and with
`geodesics` absent, nothing renders differently.

sr-core 1.5.0 has no way to show light bent by gravity. A lens effect on a 2D layer can fake a distortion, but not the
shadow of radius `sqrt(27) M`, the secondary image of the disk behind the hole, or the Doppler and gravitational

The reference engine (`rs-scene-render`) added the feature on 2026-10-06 in three steps:

- a CPU reference in double precision, `sr_sim::gr` (merge 262de59: commits d68474b, 5e33e9d, bc4e7d1, c7fcc13),
  that no render calls and that the renderer is compared with (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3074-3075);
- the schema: `blackHole`, `accretionDisk`, `camera/@geodesics`, BH1 to BH8, W03 to W05 (commit b385cb8, merged by
  ada1875);
- the GPU pass and its wiring into the 3D pass (merge e19e541: commits 99f4d2e, 74ebc2d, 7df721d, e4b1f85).

Before these commits the engine had no geodesic tracing. The evidence ledger records a 30 s film at 1280 x 800 and
64 rays per pixel, rendered in 78.7 to 90.1 s per 240 frames, and a 320 x 200 pixel-art version; the films are not
kept in the repository (`tools/evidence/cinematic-impact.json:3412-3413`). No published scene is named as the need.

## Specification

The key words MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD and MAY are those of RFC 2119. Units are G = c = 1: lengths are scene units,
`M` = `blackHole/@mass` is a length, and geometric time is a length. Angles in attributes are degrees; angles in
formulas are radians.

### Semantics

**1. Activation.** A renderer MUST draw the black hole pass when the active camera has `geodesics="true"`. The pass
replaces the rasteriser and the path tracer for that 3D block: `renderer="pathtrace"` on the same camera has no effect
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/three.rs:1660-1665`). The pass draws the hole, at most one disk and a sky; it draws nothing else of
the 3D scene (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:2997-2998). In the reference, a node of another 3D kind in the same block as the hole makes
the frame fail (see Errors).

**2. The hole.** Its centre `H` is the point (`x`, `y`, `z`) under its parent's transform, as for an `object3D`
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2722`, `world3` at :256-270, `local3` at :157-168). The horizon is the sphere
`r = 2M` about `H`, the photon sphere `r = 3M`, the innermost stable circular orbit `r = 6M`
(srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3000-3001; `crates/sr-sim/src/gr.rs:4-6`). `r` is the Schwarzschild radial coordinate and is taken to
be the Euclidean distance from `H` in scene units.

**3. The metric and the ray equation.** The metric is
`ds^2 = -(1 - 2M/r) dt^2 + dr^2 / (1 - 2M/r) + r^2 dOmega^2` (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3022). A light ray stays in a plane
through `H`. With `u = 1/r`, `phi` the angle in that plane and `b` the impact parameter, the ray obeys Binet's equation
and its first integral (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3024-3025; `crates/sr-sim/src/gr.rs:8-10`):

```
u'' + u = 3 M u^2            (u')^2 = 1/b^2 - u^2 + 2 M u^3          (' = d/dphi)
```

Derived quantities: critical impact parameter `b_c = sqrt(27) M`; a ray with `b < b_c` is captured, one with
`b > b_c` escapes; for an observer at distance `d`, the shadow's angular radius is
`psi_sh = asin(b_c sqrt(1 - 2M/d) / d)` (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3025-3029).

**4. The observer and the ray of a pixel.** The camera is a static observer at `r_o = |E - H|`, `E` the camera's
eye after its pose, target, animation and shake (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2723`, :2780). `fov`, `roll`,
`target`, `yaw` and `pitch` keep their meaning (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3009-3010). Let `fwd`, `right`, `down` be the camera's
unit axes (`right x down = fwd`), `f` the focal length in pixels and `W x H_px` the frame size
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2773-2785`). The ray of a sample at offset `(j_x, j_y)` in pixel `(x, y)` is

```
d = normalize(fwd f + right (x + j_x - W/2) + down (y + j_y - H_px/2))
```

(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:364`; `(0.5, 0.5)` for one sample, :360). Then, with `n = (E - H) / r_o`
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:275-292`; `crates/sr-sim/src/gr.rs:321-329`):

```
cos a = -d . n        perp = d + n cos a        sin a = |perp|
e1 = n                e2 = perp / sin a   (if sin a <= 1e-6: e2 = normalize(n x X), or n x Y when |n.x| > 0.9)
b  = max(r_o sin a / sqrt(1 - 2M/r_o), 1e-5)
ingoing = (cos a > 0)
```

The point of the ray at angle `phi` and radius `r` is `H + r (cos(phi) e1 + sin(phi) e2)`; `phi` grows from `e1`
toward `e2` along the path traced from the camera outward.

**5. Integration and termination.** A renderer MUST integrate `u'' = -u + 3 M u^2` in `phi` from
`u = 1/r_o`, `w = du/dphi = +sqrt(max(1/b^2 - u^2 + 2 M u^3, 0))` for an ingoing ray and its negative otherwise
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:75-78`). The reference uses the classical fourth-order Runge-Kutta method with the
fixed step `h = 0.02` rad and at most 4096 steps (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:32-33`, :93-102;
`crates/sr-sim/src/gr.rs:23-28`). After a step to `u_new`:

- `u_new >= 1/(2M)`: the ray is captured (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:103-106`);
- `u_new <= 0`: the ray has escaped at `phi_inf = phi + h u / (u - u_new)` (:107-111);
- after 4096 steps without either: captured (:127; srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3078-3079). With `h = 0.02` this captures a ray
  that circles for more than about 82 rad (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3137).

The engine states that the step and the number of steps are the renderer's, and that a renderer is accepted by the
physical quantities of srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3058-3072 within the error of its integration (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3071-3072). The
integrator is therefore a SHOULD; the outcomes and the quantities in item 11 are the MUST.

**6. The disk's plane.** The disk's axis is `A = R (0, -1, 0)`, `R` the disk's rotation `Rz(rotation) Ry(rotationY)
Rx(rotationX)` under its parent's transform; translation is ignored and the disk is centred on `H`
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2743`, `local3` at :157-168). The unit frame of the disk is
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.rs:107-113`):

```
dz = normalize(A)          (A = 0: (0, -1, 0))
helper = X if |dz.x| < 0.9, else Y
dx = normalize(helper - dz (helper . dz))        dy = dz x dx        (dx x dy = dz)
```

The plane of the ray meets the disk's plane at `phi_k = phi0 + k pi`, with (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:51-59`)

```
a = e1 . dz,  c = e2 . dz;   none if a^2 + c^2 < 1e-12
phi0 = atan2(-a, c);  phi0 += pi if phi0 < 0;  phi0 += pi again if phi0 < 1e-6       (phi0 in (0, pi])
```

The step that would pass `phi_k` is shortened to land on it, and the radius there is `1/u`
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:86-92`, :115-125). Only `k < 4` is followed. The pixel takes the first crossing
with `innerRadius <= r <= outerRadius`; the disk is opaque, so the ray ends there
(srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3054-3055, :3095-3096).

**7. The gas.** Circular orbits in the disk's plane with `Omega(r) = sqrt(M / r^3)`, turning counter-clockwise seen
from the tip of `dz` (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3035-3037). At a hit at radius `r` and angle `phi_k`
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:246-259`; `crates/sr-sim/src/gr.rs:70-80`):

```
p   = cos(phi_k) e1 + sin(phi_k) e2
psi = atan2(p . dy, p . dx)                                  azimuth in the disk
h_z = (e1 x e2) . dz
g   = sqrt(max(1 - 3M/r, 0)) / (1 + Omega(r) b h_z)          observed / emitted energy
```

This is `g = sqrt(1 - 3M/r) / (1 - Omega lambda)` with `lambda = -b h_z`, the photon's angular momentum about the
axis over its energy, because the path is traced against the photon's motion (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3038-3043, :3097-3098).
`timeScale` does not enter `g`.

**8. Temperature and colour.** With `r_in = innerRadius` (6M when absent) and `x = r / r_in`
(srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3050-3053; `crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:188-191`):

```
T(r) = temperatureScale * f(x) / f(49/36),   f(x) = x^(-3/4) (1 - x^(-1/2))^(1/4) for x > 1, 0 otherwise
f(49/36) = (6/7)^(3/2) 7^(-1/4) = 0.4878713...
```

`temperatureScale` is the temperature at the maximum, `r = 49/36 r_in`, before the shift of light. The reference
shader divides by the rounded constant 0.4880 instead (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:190`), which makes its peak
`0.99974 temperatureScale`; see Open issues. No Novikov-Thorne factors are applied (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3053).

The emitted colour is a black body at the observed temperature `g T(r)`, which carries the `g^4` of bolometric
intensity (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3046-3048):

```
C_disk = B(g T(r)) * intensity * P(r, psi, t)
```

`B(T)` is linear-sRGB visible radiance of a black body, normalised so that a 6500 K black body has luminance
`Y = 1` (`crates/sr-volume/src/thermal.rs:1-9`, :72-84): Planck radiance integrated over 360 to 830 nm in 5 nm
trapezoidal steps against the CIE 1931 fits of Wyman, Sloan and Shirley (2013), converted XYZ to linear sRGB, negative
channels clipped to 0. The reference samples it from a table of 1025 entries at `50000 K (i/1024)^2`, interpolated
linearly in `1024 sqrt(T / 50000)`, with `T` clamped to `[0, 50000]` K (`crates/sr-volume/src/thermal.rs:16-18`,
:86-95; `crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:232-237`). A renderer MUST clamp `g T` to 50000 K as well, or the
saturation of very hot disks differs.

**9. The pattern `P`.** `t_g = t * timeScale`, `t` the frame's time in seconds
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2768`). `s = ln(r / r_in)`, `alpha = (psi - Omega(r) t_g) mod 2 pi`
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:214-222`). `S` is the disk's `seed` converted to a 32-bit unsigned integer
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2742`). Hashes, on 32-bit unsigned integers with wrapping arithmetic
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:133-145`):

```
pcg(v):   s = v * 747796405 + 2891336453;  w = ((s >> ((s >> 28) + 4)) ^ s) * 277803737;  return (w >> 22) ^ w
hash3(a, b, c) = pcg(a ^ pcg(b + pcg(c)))
unit(h) = (h >> 8) / 16777216
```

- `none`: `P = 1`.
- `clumps`: `P = exp(3 contrast (N - 0.5))`, `N = 0.65 V(alpha, s, 32, 0.18, S) + 0.35 V(alpha, s, 64, 0.09, S + 7)`
  (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:223-226`). `V(alpha, s, n, ds, seed)` is value noise with smoothstep weights:
  `X = alpha / (2 pi) * n`, `Y = s / ds`, corners `x0 = floor(X) mod n`, `x1 = (x0 + 1) mod n`,
  `y0 = floor(Y) + 4096`, `y1 = y0 + 1`, corner values `unit(hash3(seed, xi, yj))`, weights
  `f^2 (3 - 2 f)` of the fractional parts, bilinear mix (:193-211).
- `spiral`: `P = exp(1.5 contrast cos(m alpha + 3 s + phase))`, `m = 2 + (S mod 3)`,
  `phase = 2 pi unit(pcg(S + 11))` (:227-229).

**10. The sky.** A ray that escapes ends in the world direction `D = cos(phi_inf) e1 + sin(phi_inf) e2`
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:317-319`). The reference draws there a procedural star field, not an authored layer
or environment (`tools/evidence/cinematic-impact.json:3413`; `crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:147-182`):

```
face, major, uv: the cube face of D by its largest |component| (+x 0, -x 1, +y 2, -y 3, +z 4, -z 5);
                 uv = (D.y, D.z), (D.x, D.z) or (D.x, D.y) for x, y or z faces
st = (uv / major * 0.5 + 0.5) * 512;  cell = floor(st);  l = st - cell
hS = hash3(face + Q * 8, cell.x, cell.y)       Q = the disk's seed S, or 0 without a disk
star iff unit(hS) < 0.006
centre = (0.3 + 0.4 unit(pcg(hS + 1)), 0.3 + 0.4 unit(pcg(hS + 2)))
radius = clamp(0.6 (1/f) / ((pi/2) / 512), 0.02, 0.3)        (cell units; hard edge, |l - centre| < radius)
colour = mix((0.62, 0.74, 1.0), (1.0, 0.86, 0.7), unit(pcg(hS + 4))) * (0.25 + 1.5 unit(pcg(hS + 3))^3)
```

The sky is not shifted by `g` (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:317-320`). The star seed is the disk's `seed`
(`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2789`).

**11. What a pixel shows.** Captured: black. Disk hit: `C_disk`. Escaped without a hit: the sky. A pixel is the mean
of `N` samples, times `2^exposure` of the camera, then encoded with the sRGB curve when the working space blends on
encoded values (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:312-321`, :356-367; `crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:418`, :2951).
The pass writes alpha 1 at every pixel (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:367`), so its picture is opaque where it is
composited, in document order like any 3D block (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2652-2657`). Physical checks a
renderer MUST meet for the cases it can reach, within the error of its integration (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3058-3072): the
shadow's radius `psi_sh` (0.48336 rad at `d = 10M`, 0.10200 at `50M`, 0.0051910 at `1000M`); the deflection
`alpha(b)` (0.590396 rad at `b = 10M`, 0.236136 at `20M`, 0.0412225 at `100M`); `g = sqrt(1 - 3M/r)` for
`lambda = 0`, 0.70711 at `r = 6M`.

**12. Antialiasing.** `N = clamp(pathSamples, 1, 4096)` (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2775-2777`). Sample
`s` of pixel `(x, y)` is offset by (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:356-364`)

```
j0 = (unit(hash3(x, y, 1)), unit(hash3(x, y, 2)))
j_s = fract(j0 + s (0.7548776662, 0.5698402910))        (N > 1);   j = (0.5, 0.5) for N = 1
```

`denoise` is not applied (W04); lights are not used (W05); `depthOfField`, `lensDistortion`, `ambientOcclusion` and
`screenSpaceReflections` are not read by the pass (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2778-2792` carries only the eye,
the axes, the focal length, the samples and the exposure).

**13. Time.** Only the pattern depends on time: `r`, `psi` and `g` do not (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3138-3139). The hole, the
disk's tilt and the camera MAY be animated through the usual attribute animation; the reference reads them per frame
through the same attribute access as other nodes (`crates/sr-gpu/src/vector.rs:63-79`).

### Defaults and the neutral case

`camera/@geodesics` defaults to `false`: the camera there always was. A document without `blackHole` and
`accretionDisk` renders as before. A `blackHole` or `accretionDisk` under a camera without geodesics draws nothing,
is warned (W11, engine W03) and noted at render time (`crates/sr-gpu/tests/path_tracer/geodesic_scene.rs:76-86`).

Defaults of the new attributes (diff; the typed model fills them, `crates/sr-model/tests/rules/black_hole.rs:31-54`):
`blackHole` `x = y = z = 0`; `accretionDisk` `innerRadius` absent = `6 mass`, `seed = 0`, `angularPattern = clumps`,
`contrast = 0.5`, `intensity = 1`, `timeScale = 1`, `rotationX = rotationY = rotation = 0` (axis `(0, -1, 0)`, the
scene's up). `intensity = 0` draws the disk black. `contrast = 0` makes `P = 1` for every pattern.

## Rationale

- **An error, not a silence (BH6).** The pass cannot compose a rasterised object with a lensed one, so a document that
  asks for both is refused instead of drawing half of it (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3014-3016; commit 7df721d message).
- **6M as the inner edge.** It is the innermost stable circular orbit of the Schwarzschild metric; inside it gas is
  not on circular orbits and `Omega` and `g` of item 7 would not describe it (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3034-3036).
- **3M as the camera's limit.** `b = r_o sin a / sqrt(1 - 2M/r_o)` assumes a static observer
  (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3027-3028). The reference requires the camera outside the photon sphere (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3008-3009)
  and gives as reason "within 3 mass ... where no observer is at rest" (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2726`).
  That reason is not shown: see Open issues.
- **A disk with no position.** Gas on geodesics of the hole's metric is centred on it, so a place of its own would be
  an inconsistency to validate (`crates/sr-model/tests/rules/black_hole.rs:45-51` refuses `x` on a disk).
- **`temperatureScale` at the peak.** An author sets the hottest colour directly, before the shift of light
  (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3050-3053).
- **Accepted by quantities, not by an image.** The shadow radius, the deflection and `g` are closed forms checked by
  quadrature, independent of the integrator (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3058-3072, :3110-3112).

The colour function `B` reuses the black-body table of the volumetric effects of SREP 40
(`crates/sr-volume/src/thermal.rs:86-87`: "shared across all media and renderers").

## Rejected alternatives

None recorded in the engine's history. The engine's draft lists limits of the chosen design (no rotation, charge or
frame dragging; no vertical structure, radial flow or self-irradiation of the disk; Novikov-Thorne factors not
applied; a static observer only) without discussing alternatives (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3132-3143).

## Backwards compatibility

Every document valid under sr-core 1.5.0 stays valid: the change adds two elements to `nodeChoice` and one optional
attribute with a default to `cameraType`. No valid document renders differently. The change is MINOR under SREP 4.

The version gate is BH1: the new elements, and any `camera/@geodesics` even `false`, require `version="1.3"` exactly.
This is the engine's text and matches SREP 40's own gates (`P3D1`, `OCN1`, `FRX1` test `/scene/@version='1.3'`), but
it does not match SREP 4 (a MINOR release adds its MAJOR.MINOR to `scene/@version`) nor SREPs 66 to 75, which gate
new features on 1.6 (for example V15 of SREP 74). A document of version 1.4, 1.5 or 1.6 cannot use a black hole. See
Open issues.

## Open issues

- **Version gate (all of SREPs 76 to 80).** The engine's text gates these features on `scene/@version="1.3"` exactly: BH1 and VOX1 directly, and the attributes on SREP 40 elements through SREP 40's own gates (V8 for volumes, media and pyro, CRT1, FRX1, P3D1, OCN1), which are already in sr-core 1.5.0 in that form. A document that uses them cannot declare 1.4, 1.5 or 1.6, so it cannot also use the features those versions gate (SREPs 57 to 75). SREPs 66 to 75 gate their features on 1.6 with an explicit list of the earlier versions. This draft keeps the engine's text verbatim, because the aim is a byte-equal vendoring; the review should decide whether the 1.3 gates become "1.3 or later" (a separate SREP that would also amend SREP 40). **Engine change:** yes, if the gates change: the engine mirrors every Schematron rule natively (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs`), so it must change those checks and its corpus, then re-vendor.
- **Which of the issues below need an engine change.** The W03/W04 collision (item 1): yes, the engine renumbers its black-hole warnings (`codes.rs`, `rules.rs`, the corpus manifest) and the kit cases `srep-0076-valid-w03-no-lens` and `srep-0076-valid-w04-denoise` change with it. SREP 74 against BH6 and BH8 (item 3): only if both SREPs are accepted and BH1 is relaxed; then BH6 and BH8 change in the Schematron and in their native mirror in `rules.rs`. The boolean forms (item 4) and items 6 to 11: an engine change or a documentation change, as each item says.

1. **Warning codes collide.** The engine uses W03 and W04 for two unrelated findings: SREP 57's generated-marker
   warnings (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:2356-2383`) and the black-hole warnings (:674, :690). `codes.rs` carries
   both meanings in one summary (`crates/sr-model/src/codes.rs:42-51`), so `scene-render explain W03` cannot tell
   them apart. sr-core uses W01 to W04 (SREP 40, SREP 57); the engine also uses W05 to W10 (W06 to W10 for whitewater,
   voxels and media, `crates/sr-model/src/codes.rs:53-77`). This draft proposes **W11** for the undrawn hole or disk
   and **W12** for denoise on a geodesic camera, and keeps W05. The engine change is needed before Accepted.
2. **Version gate.** BH1 requires `version="1.3"` exactly, kept verbatim from the engine, while this SREP targets
   schema 1.6. A later SREP or the review should decide whether the gate becomes "at least 1.3" (the form of V15:
   refuse in 1.0 to 1.2) or "at least 1.6". As written, BH1 also refuses a `camera` that carries `geodesics="false"`
   explicitly in a document of any other version.
3. **SREP 74 (`viewport3D`).** `viewport3D` needs version 1.6 (V15) and the black hole needs 1.3 (BH1), so today no
   document has both. Once BH1 is relaxed:
   - BH6 tests `/scene//object3D` and `/scene//particles3D`, so an `object3D` inside any `viewport3D` refuses a
     geodesic camera elsewhere, although SREP 74 §5 isolates the viewport's objects from the document's 3D scene.
   - A `camera` child of a `viewport3D` is a `cameraType` and may carry `geodesics="true"`; `blackHole` is not a
     permitted child of `viewport3D`, so BH5 would accept it on the strength of a hole in the main composition that
     the viewport cannot see, and BH8 and W11 count it as the document's geodesic camera.
   The rules should be scoped to the document's 3D scene, and a geodesic camera inside a viewport should be refused
   or defined.
4. **`xs:boolean` lexical forms.** BH5 to BH8, W11 (engine W03), W12 and W05 test the literal `'true'`
   (`@geodesics!='true'`; `crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:604`, :686), but the model parses `"1"` as true
   (`crates/sr-model/src/parse.rs:44-52`) and the renderer reads the parsed value
   (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:146-148`, :2695). `geodesics="1"` renders the geodesic pass with none of
   BH5 to BH8 checked. The same holds for W12 and `denoise="1"`.
5. **W12 (engine W04) and the default of `denoise`.** `denoise` defaults to `true` in `cameraType`, so every
   geodesic camera "has" `denoise="true"`, but the warning fires only when it is written
   (`crates/sr-model/src/rules.rs:671`; the corpus scene `valid/black-hole.scene.xml` has no `denoise` and no
   warning). The XSD documentation says "`denoise="true"` ... not used (W04)". Decide which is meant.
6. **The background.** The XSD documentation of `camera/@geodesics` says the pass renders "the lensed image of the
   background" and "the 2D background". The reference lenses a procedural star field seeded by the disk's `seed`
   (item 10), not an authored layer, and writes an opaque picture (alpha 1), so 2D content painted before the 3D
   block is covered. The XSD does not mention the star field, that `accretionDisk/@seed` also seeds it, or that it
   exists without a disk (seed 0). Either the documentation or the renderer should change.
7. **Peak constant.** The shader normalises by 0.4880 (`crates/sr-gpu/src/geodesic.wgsl:190`) where the profile's
   maximum is `f(49/36) = 0.48787` (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3052): the drawn peak is 0.99974 `temperatureScale`. This draft
   specifies the exact value; the engine should use it.
8. **`pathSamples` bounds.** The schema allows up to 65536; the pass clamps to 4096
   (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2777`) while the path tracer clamps to 65536 (:411). The code's fallback when
   the attribute is absent is 16 (:2776), not the schema's 64.
9. **Several disks.** Nothing in the schema limits the number of `accretionDisk` elements per hole. The pass draws
   only the first one in its block that names the hole and ignores the rest without a note
   (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2733-2737`). A rule (one disk per hole) or a warning is missing.
10. **Seed range.** `seed` is `xs:unsignedLong`; the reference reads it as a double and casts it to 32 bits
    (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2742`), so seeds above 2^32 - 1 saturate and seeds above 2^53 lose
    precision. The spec should state the reduction (for example `seed mod 2^32`).
11. **Hole and disk in different blocks.** The pass collects the hole and its disk from one 3D block, the run of
    sibling 3D nodes not interrupted by a drawn 2D sibling (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2652-2677`). A disk
    under another parent, or separated from the hole by a 2D sibling, is not drawn with it, and its own block
    reports `camera geodesics="true" needs a blackHole`. The schema says nothing about where the two may be placed.
12. **XSD wording.** The `blackHoleType` documentation attaches "(BH1)" to the horizon `r_s = 2 mass`; BH1 is the
    version gate. The `accretionDisk` documentation calls `contrast` "its amplitude, 0 to 1"; the reference uses it as
    an exponent (`P = exp(3 contrast (N - 0.5))`, `exp(1.5 contrast cos(...))`), so the mean brightness of a
    patterned disk is not that of a smooth one.
13. **Why 3M (BH7).** A static observer has 4-velocity `(1 - 2M/r)^(-1/2) d/dt`, which is timelike wherever
    `g_tt = -(1 - 2M/r) < 0`, that is for every `r > 2M` (from the metric of item 3). So static observers exist
    between 2M and 3M, and the runtime message's reason (`crates/sr-gpu/src/render_three.rs:2726`) does not hold
    as stated. The engine records no other reason for 3M. Either the rule is kept as a design limit (for example,
    to keep the observer where the integration and the acceptance figures were checked) and the message is
    corrected, or the limit becomes `r_o > 2M`. Undecided.
14. **Physics references.** The engine's draft names Luminet 1979 (Astron. Astrophys. 75, 228), Shakura and Sunyaev
    1973 (Astron. Astrophys. 24, 337), Darwin 1959, Chandrasekhar 1983 and Keeton and Petters 2005 (Phys. Rev. D 72,
    104006), and says none was read and all are cited from memory (srep-0000-cinematic-impact.md:3044, :3051, :3108-3112). No DOI or
    URL is given; this draft does not cite them. The formulas used are derived in the engine's draft and checked by
    quadrature.
