# SREP 0 — Add ragdolls and physics interchange to 3D rigid bodies

## Abstract

[SREP 12](srep-0012.md) names, as its S4 building block, "seeded Voronoi pre-fracture with breakable glue
joints; articulated bodies with motors blending from animation; rigid bodies (SREP 11) read from and written
to UsdPhysics and glTF physics (KHR_physics_rigid_bodies, once ratified)", with the bars "fragment volumes sum
to the whole; no glue breaks below its threshold" (line 297), and records what landed: "Voronoi, plane and
label fracture at an impact or by joint stress, on meshes and voxel cells (SREPs 40, 77, 80); ragdolls and the
round trip are open". This SREP closes the row in three parts. The landed glue (a `fracture` with
`mode="stress"` and a `strength`, SREP 80 section 6) is restated as pure functions so that the plan's two bars
are measurable rules: the pieces of a seeded Voronoi partition sum to the whole in cells, mass and momentum,
and a joint whose principal tension is below the strength does not break. A `ragdoll` child of an `object3D`
with an imported model turns the model's nodes into capsule limbs joined by ball or hinge joints, with a
`blend` weight that is kinematic at 0, a PD drive toward the animated pose scaled by 1 − blend above it, and
the drawn pose the slerp of the animated and the simulated one. `physics/@src` reads a UsdPhysics stage or a
glTF with KHR_physics_rigid_bodies into SREP 11 rigid bodies and constraints, and the `physics-export` tool
action writes them back; the mapping table is normative, every attribute without a counterpart rides in
`customData` or `extras` under the `sr:` prefix so that the round trip is the identity, and what the format
cannot express is a typed error. `conformance/tools/ragdoll_reference.py` restates every rule and measures the
bars: the glued cantilever carries the beam formula 3 ρ g L² / a at its root and breaks there first; a free
pendulum limb swings with its period within 10⁻⁴ and a double pendulum keeps its energy within 10⁻⁸ of its
scale; the driven limb settles where the drive balances its weight; a hinge limit holds; the round trip through
USD and glTF returns the document; a run is a function of the step count.

## Motivation

- **The S4 row** (SREP 12 line 297) is half landed: the engine has Voronoi, plane and label partitions on
  cells (`crates/sr-3d/src/pieces.rs`), the stress rule on their joints (`crates/sr-sim/src/stress.rs`) and
  the timed fracture (`physics3d/fracture.rs`), but no articulated figure and no physics import or export.
- **The peers** (SREP 12 line 187): Houdini RBD and Unreal Chaos both have ragdolls that blend from
  animation, and every DCC tool reads and writes UsdPhysics (Omniverse, Houdini, Blender); the plan asks for
  parity.
- **Characters** (the characters draft) pose a skeleton by `joint` elements; a hit, a fall or a stunt needs
  the skeleton to go limp under the simulation and come back, which is what the blend weight does.
- **Interchange** lets a scene's rigid bodies be set up in a DCC tool and brought into the document, and lets a
  document's set-up be checked in another engine; the plan reserves the glTF side until the extension is
  ratified.

  weight), `driveStiffness` (`positiveDecimal`, default 50: newton-metres per radian per kilogram of the

  limbs' default radius as a fraction of their bone length), `friction` and `restitution` as `rigidBody3DType`
  (defaults 0.5 and 0), `anchor` (one of `none`, `pin`; default `none`: with `pin` the root limb is held to
  the world by a ball joint at its node), `collisionGroup` and `collidesWith` as `rigidBody3DType`,
  `selfCollision` (`xs:boolean`, default false: whether limbs collide with each other).
- Children of `ragdoll`: the animation elements for `blend`; `limb` (one or more): `name` (`xs:string`,
  required: the model node, as `jointType/@name` at 4083), `parent` (`xs:string`: the name of the parent

  `radius` (`positiveDecimal`, model units), `mass` (`positiveDecimal`, kilograms) or `density`

  `axisZ` (`xs:double`: the hinge axis in the node's own axes, default (0, 0, 1)), `minAngle` and `maxAngle`

  180: the half-angle of a ball joint's cone; 180 is no limit), `twist` (`nonNegativeDecimal`, degrees, default
  180: the ball joint's twist limit each way), `blend` (`unitDecimal`: overrides the ragdoll's for this limb).
- `physicsType` (6136): `src` (`xs:anyURI`: a UsdPhysics stage, `.usda` or `.usdc`, or a glTF, `.gltf` or
  `.glb`, with KHR_physics_rigid_bodies; its bodies, materials, groups and joints become 3D rigid bodies and
  constraints of the document as if written in it), `srcFormat` (one of `auto`, `usd`, `gltf`; default
  `auto`: by the extension; RD7 only with `src`).
- A tool action (SREP 4's CLI): `scene-render physics-export DOC --usd OUT.usda` and `--gltf OUT.gltf` write
  the 3D world at `physics@start` by the mapping of rules 7 to 9.
- Schematron (`schema/scene-render.sch`):
  - **RD1** `ragdoll` only on an `object3D` with `primitive="mesh"` and a model `src`.
  - **RD2** limb names are unique within a ragdoll, and `parent` names another limb of the same ragdoll.
  - **RD3** exactly one limb has no `parent`.
  - **RD4** `axisX`, `axisY`, `axisZ`, `minAngle` and `maxAngle` only with `kind="hinge"`; `swing` and

  - **RD5** `minAngle` and `maxAngle` both or none, with `minAngle` ≤ `maxAngle`.
  - **RD6** `mass` and `density` not both on a limb.
  - **RD7** `physics/@srcFormat` only with `physics/@src`.
  - **V17** `ragdoll` and `physics/@src` need `scene/@version="1.8"` or later.

### Semantics

`ragdoll_reference.py` states every rule as a function. Rules 1 and 2 restate the landed glue of SREP 80
section 6 and `crates/sr-sim/src/stress.rs` in the same numbers; rules 3 to 6 are the ragdoll, measured on
planar hinge chains in reduced coordinates (a ball joint's cone is rule 6 and open issue 4); rules 7 to 11
are the interchange, in SREP 11's physics frame, which is UsdPhysics's: metres, y up, z toward the camera.

1. **The partition** (`splitmix64`, `seeds_in`, `nearest`, `voronoi`, `components`, `partition`, `moments`).
   The `pieces` seeds are drawn from `seed`: axis a of seed s is lo_a + splitmix64(seed + (3 s + a + 1) ·
   0x9E3779B97F4A7C15) mod (hi_a − lo_a + 1) in the doubled box [2 min + 1, 2 max + 1] of the cells
   (`pieces.rs:314-352`); splitmix64 is the finaliser of Steele, Lea and Flood (2014), whose first output from
   state 0 is 0xE220A8397B1DCDAF. Each cell goes to the seed with the least squared distance from its doubled
   centre 2 key + 1, in exact integers, a tie to the lowest index (`pieces.rs:362-373`). Each label's cells
   split into face-connected components; the pieces are numbered by their first cell in scan order (z, then y,
   then x) and list their cells in that order (`pieces.rs:388-438`). **Fragment volumes sum to the whole**: the
   pieces' cell counts sum to the body's, every cell is in exactly one piece and every piece is connected
   (`volume_sum_test`), and for a rigid motion the pieces' linear and angular momenta, from their exact cuboid
   moments, sum to the whole's within 10⁻¹² (`momentum_test`): a cut keeps what the body had.
2. **The glue** (`joints`, `section`, `cut_stress`, `principal_tension`, `cantilever`, `broken`). A joint is
   the faces two pieces share; its section has their area, centroid, exact second moments about the centroid
   (each face's own a² A / 12 in its plane plus its area at its distance), the box of the faces and the mean
   normal from a to b. A load (a force at a point and a moment about it) on a cut of joints is carried as one
   rigid section (`stress.rs:114-230`): the normal stress is the pull over the area, N / A, positive in tension;
   the shear is the force across the normal over the area; the bending stress is g · r over the corners of the
   joint's box with g = J⁻¹ (n × M) from the section's second moments (6 M / a³ on a square face, the beam
   formula); the twist is T r / J_p with r the farthest corner. The joint breaks when the **principal tension**
   σ / 2 + √(σ² / 4 + τ²), σ the normal and bending stress added and τ the shear and twist added, reaches
   `strength`: Rankine's criterion for a brittle bond, so a push alone breaks nothing (`stress.rs:19-21`).
   **No glue breaks below its threshold**: `broken` lists the joints at or above the strength and no other. A
   glued **cantilever** of n cells of side a, cell 0 welded to the wall and loaded by its own weight across
   the bar, has at the joint between cells 0 and 1 the bending stress 3 ρ g L² / a and the shear ρ g L with
   L = (n − 1) a (`cantilever_root`), its stresses fall along the bar, and with a strength just under the root's
   principal tension the root breaks alone (`glue_test`).
3. **Limbs** (`capsule_volume`, `capsule_inertia`, `limb`, `chain`, `mass_matrix`). A limb is a capsule
   along its bone, from its node to its child node, radius `radius` (default `ragdoll/@radius` times the
   length), mass `mass` or density times the capsule's volume π r² L + 4 π r³ / 3; its inertia about its
   centre is the cylinder's and two hemispheres' (83 / 320 m r² about a hemisphere's own centre, 3 r / 8 from
   the flat face) by the parallel axis theorem. The joint to the parent limb sits at the limb's node. The
   reference states the chain in reduced coordinates, the absolute angles φ_i of a planar chain hanging from
   the origin (φ = 0 along gravity): the mass matrix is M_ij = I_i δ_ij + Σ_k m_k ℓ_ki ℓ_kj (e_i · e_j), ℓ_ki
   the lever of joint i on the centre of limb k (L_i for i < k, d_k for i = k, 0 beyond) and e_i the tangent
   of limb i (Featherstone 2008, chapter 6); two hanging unit rods give the classic (4/3, 1/2; 1/2, 1/3).
4. **The blend** (`drive_torque`, `blend_angle`, `drawn`, `step` at w = 0). Let w be the limb's `blend`. At
   w = 0 the limb is **kinematic**: it follows the posed skeleton (the model's clip and `joint` elements),
   its velocity the difference of the pose over the step, as SREP 11's kinematic bodies follow their
   animation. At w > 0 the limb is dynamic and its joint is driven toward the animated relative pose by a PD
   drive, τ = (1 − w) (k (θ_a − θ) + c (θ̇_a − θ̇)) with k = `driveStiffness` · m and c = `driveDamping` · m,
   m the limb's mass (about the hinge axis; about the three axes of a ball joint). The node's drawn pose is
   the **slerp** of the animated and the simulated pose by w (the lerp of the angles in the plane), so the
   figure is its animation at 0, its simulation at 1 and goes limp continuously as `blend` ramps between.
5. **The dynamics** (`accelerations`, `step`, `run`, `energy`, `pendulum_period`, `settled_angle`). The
   reference integrates Lagrange's equations M φ̈ = Q − Ṁ φ̇ + ∂T/∂φ, Q the gravity and the joint torques,
   with the classical Runge–Kutta step at the document's step and the limits of rule 6 after it; the engine
   steps Rapier's joints by SREP 11's substeps. The rules are the invariants, not the integrator's numbers: a
   free limb hanging from a hinge swings with the period 2 π √((I + m d²) / (m g d)) within 10⁻³
   (`period_test`); a free double pendulum keeps its energy within 10⁻³ of m g L over 1600 steps
   (`energy_test`); a driven limb at w = 1/2 settles where (1 − w) k (θ_a − θ) = m g d sin θ within 10⁻⁵
   (`settle_test`); a kinematic chain is its animation exactly (`seek_test`). A run is a function of the step
   count and the document (SREP 14's **seek check**): the state after n steps rendered alone equals the state
   played from 0, and running 60 steps twice equals running 120.
6. **Limits** (`_limits`, `limit_test`). A hinge's relative angle stays in [`minAngle`, `maxAngle`]: when a
   step leaves the range the sub-chain beyond the joint is turned back onto the limit and the relative angular
   velocity is set to zero, an inelastic stop. A ball joint's swing (the angle between the limb's axis and
   its parent's) stays within `swing` and its twist about its own axis within ±`twist`, by the same rule on
   Rapier's spherical joint limits.
7. **Frame and units** (`to_metres`, `to_pixels`, `flip_vec`, `flip_quat`, `shape_volume`, `body_mass`). The
   interchange frame is SREP 11's physics frame: a scene point (x, y, z) in pixels is (x, −y, −z) / ppm
   metres, a quaternion (qx, qy, qz, qw) is (qx, −qy, −qz, qw), velocities px/s are m/s by ppm, angular
   velocities are degrees per second in USD and radians per second in glTF, and the frame is written as a y-up
   stage with `metersPerUnit` 1 (`upAxis` "Y"). A body's mass is its `mass`, or `density` times its shape's
   volume (box w h d; sphere 4 π r³ / 3; capsule π r² (h − 2 r) + 4 π r³ / 3; cylinder π r² h; cone π r² h /
   3, r half the width); a mesh shape's volume needs the triangles (the engine's, rule 10).
8. **Bodies to UsdPhysics** (`to_usd`, `from_usd`, `usda`). The stage has `/World/PhysicsScene`
   (`physics:gravityDirection` and `physics:gravityMagnitude` from `gravityX/Y/Z`; `customData`
   `sr:pixelsPerMeter`, `sr:fixedStep`, `sr:solverIterations`, `sr:start`, `sr:bounds`, `sr:frame`); one prim
   per body at `/World/<id>`: a `Cube` of size 1 scaled by the size for `box`, a `Sphere`, `Capsule`,
   `Cylinder` or `Cone` with `axis` "Y" (the capsule's `height` is its cylinder part), `xformOp:translate`
   and `xformOp:orient`, the API schemas `PhysicsRigidBodyAPI` (`physics:rigidBodyEnabled` false for `static`,
   `physics:kinematicEnabled` for `kinematic`), `PhysicsMassAPI` (`physics:mass` or `physics:density`) and
   `PhysicsCollisionAPI`; `physics:velocity` and `physics:angularVelocity`; a `Material` prim at
   `/World/Materials/<id>` with `PhysicsMaterialAPI` (`friction` to both `physics:staticFriction` and
   `physics:dynamicFriction`, `physics:restitution`) bound by `material:binding:physics`; a
   `PhysicsCollisionGroup` at `/World/Groups/g<n>` per `collisionGroup` with its members in
   `collection:colliders:includes` and, from `collidesWith`, the groups it does not meet in
   `physics:filteredGroups`; `bounds="floor"` a static `Cube` at `/World/Ground` (SREP 11's slab) and
   `"frame"` six at `/World/Frame/<side>`. Everything without a counterpart rides in the prim's `customData`
   under the `sr:` prefix when it differs from its default: `sr:linearDamping`, `sr:angularDamping`,
   `sr:sensor`, `sr:fixedRotation`, `sr:bullet`, `sr:activateAt`, `sr:collidesWith`. `usda` prints the stage
   as deterministic usda text. **The round trip is the identity**: `from_usd(to_usd(w))` equals w and
   `to_usd` of that equals the stage (`round_trip_test`).
9. **Joints to UsdPhysics** (`_joint_frame`, `_joint_to_usd`, `_joint_from_usd`). Each constraint is a prim at
   `/World/Joints/<id>` with `physics:body0` a and `physics:body1` b (absent for the world): `ball` a
   `PhysicsSphericalJoint`; `hinge` and `motor` a `PhysicsRevoluteJoint`; `slider` a `PhysicsPrismaticJoint`;
   `weld` a `PhysicsFixedJoint`; `distance`, `rope` and `spring` a `PhysicsDistanceJoint` (`physics:minDistance`
   and `physics:maxDistance` the rest length, a rope with the maximum only); `pin` a spherical joint to the
   world, or a distance joint of length 0 with `sr:stiffness` when it has a stiffness. The joint frame is at
   the anchor (SREP 11's rule: the given point, else b's centre) in each body's frame (`physics:localPos0/1`,
   `physics:localRot0/1`); its `physics:axis` token is the axis of the hinge, motor or slider when that axis
   is along a scene axis (else "X"), and the frame's rotation takes the token's axis onto the scene axis in
   the physics frame (an antiparallel axis turns 180° about the next axis), so limits and motor speeds keep
   their document signs: `physics:lowerLimit` and `physics:upperLimit` in degrees (a slider's in metres), a
   motor's `PhysicsDriveAPI:angular` with `drive:angular:physics:targetVelocity` (degrees per second),
   `drive:angular:physics:type` "acceleration" (SREP 11's acceleration-based motor) and
   `drive:angular:physics:maxForce`; `breakForce` to `physics:breakForce`. `customData` carries `sr:type` (the
   document's kind, which the prim type does not determine), `sr:axis`, a spring's `sr:stiffness` and
   `sr:damping`, and `sr:unset`, the names of the attributes the document left to their defaults (the anchor,
   the rest length, a spring's gains) so that they return unset.
10. **glTF** (`to_gltf`, `from_gltf`). A glTF 2.0 document with `extensionsUsed` KHR_physics_rigid_bodies and
    KHR_implicit_shapes: one node per body (`translation`, `rotation` in the physics frame) whose extension
    holds `motion` (`isKinematic`, `mass`, `linearVelocity`, `angularVelocity` in radians per second; absent
    for a static body), a `collider` (or a `trigger` for a sensor) with its `geometry.shape` into the
    KHR_implicit_shapes `shapes` (`box`, `sphere`, `capsule`, `cylinder`; a cone is a cylinder with
    `radiusTop` 0), its `physicsMaterial` into `physicsMaterials` (`staticFriction`, `dynamicFriction`,
    `restitution`) and its `collisionFilter` into `collisionFilters` (`collisionSystems` ["g<n>"] and
    `collideWithSystems` from `collidesWith`); each constraint two nodes, the joint node a child of a at the
    frame of rule 9 with `joint.connectedNode` the other node, a child of b (or a root node at the world
    anchor), and `joint.joint` into `physicsJoints`, whose `limits` lock the axes the kind locks
    (`linearAxes` and `angularAxes` with min = max = 0), limit the hinge or slider axis (radians, metres)
    and, for a distance joint, bound the distance; a motor is a `drives` entry (`type` "angular", `mode`
    "acceleration", `axis`, `velocityTarget` in radians per second, `maxForce`). The document's
    `gravityX/Y/Z`, `pixelsPerMeter`, `fixedStep`, `solverIterations`, `start`, `bounds` and `frame` ride in
    the root `extras` under `sr:`, a density in the node's `extras` as `sr:density`, and the rest as in rule 8
    (`sr:bullet`, `sr:activateAt`, dampings, `sr:fixedRotation`, a joint's `sr:type`, `sr:axis`, `sr:unset`,
    `sr:stiffness`, `sr:damping`, `sr:breakForce`). The round trip is the identity. The extension is a Khronos
    draft, not ratified: this rule is informative until it is (open issue 1), and the engine refuses `@src`
    glTF unless `srcFormat="gltf"` is given.
11. **Foreign assets** (`from_usd`, `from_gltf`, `unsupported_test`). A stage without `sr:` data fills the
    format's defaults: `pixelsPerMeter` 100 and SREP 11's step, iterations and gravity; a body without mass
    takes UsdPhysics's default density 1000 kg/m³ (or its material's `physics:density`) times its shape's
    volume; a collider without `PhysicsRigidBodyAPI` is static; friction 0.5 and restitution 0 without a
    material; lengths scale by `metersPerUnit`; a joint's kind comes from its prim type (a revolute joint with
    a drive is a motor, a spherical joint to the world a pin), its axis from the token and the frame. What the
    format cannot express is an `InterchangeError`, never a silent drop: a generic `PhysicsJoint`, a one-sided
    limit, a z-up stage, a `Mesh` collider (the engine reads `PhysicsMeshCollisionAPI` on the object's own
    triangles; the reference covers the primitive shapes), a round shape not upright, a frustum, an unknown
    `physics:` attribute or `extras` key, a `gravityFactor`, and a glTF joint without `sr:type` (its limit set
    alone does not name a kind).
12. **Bounds.** At most 4096 seeds and pieces (`MAX_SEEDS`, `MAX_PIECES`, the engine's), seeds within 2⁴⁰
    cells of the origin, 256 limbs per ragdoll, 65536 steps per run, 65536 bodies and 65536 constraints per
    world; a partition over the limit, a run over the limit or a world over the limit is an error.

## Rationale

- **Glue as conformance, not as new syntax.** SREP 80's `mode="stress"` with `strength` is the glue the plan
  asks for; what was missing was the two bars as measurable rules. The reference restates the engine's
  numbers (its seeds, its tie rule, its beam section) so that the kit can check them on any engine.
- **A blend weight, not a switch.** Unreal's physics blend weight and Houdini's "animated to active" both
  blend the drawn transform; driving the joints by 1 − blend as well keeps the simulation near the animation
  while the weight is small, so the hand-off has no jolt in either direction.
- **Reduced coordinates in the reference, maximal in the engine.** The reference's chain has no constraint
  drift and an exact mass matrix, so its invariants (period, energy, settling) are the rule; Rapier's joints
  (SREP 11) approach them with substeps, and the bars leave room (10⁻³) for that.
- **The physics frame is USD's.** SREP 11 already defines the 3D world in metres, y up, z toward the camera,
  which is UsdPhysics's default; the mapping is a change of units and a sign flip, with no re-orientation.
- **`sr:` metadata for the identity.** UsdPhysics and KHR have no sensor, no bullet, no activation time and no
  fixed rotation; losing them on export would make the round trip lossy. Custom data is what both formats
  provide for exactly this, and a foreign consumer ignores it.
- **Typed errors for the rest.** A six-axis `PhysicsJoint` or a one-sided limit has no reading; inventing one
  would produce a document that behaves differently from the asset.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Ragdolls on `rigidBody` children of `joint` elements.** Every limb would repeat the body attributes and
  the parent links would be implicit in the model's hierarchy; a `limb` that names its node and its parent is
  what the authoring tools expose.
- **A position-based drive on the drawn pose only (no joint drive).** The simulation would diverge from the
  animation at small weights and snap when the weight returns to 0.
- **Re-orienting z-up stages on import.** A rotation of the whole stage changes what "up" means for the
  document's gravity and bounds; the stage's author sets `upAxis` and the engine reports the mismatch.
- **Mapping `spring` to a USD joint with a drive.** UsdPhysics's distance joint has no stiffness; a drive on
  a D6 joint would read back as a generic joint. The spring's gains ride as `sr:` data on a distance joint,
  which a foreign engine reads as a rigid distance, the nearest meaning it has.
- **Waiting for the glTF ratification before specifying.** The mapping is stated now against the draft so
  that the engine work is scoped; it is informative until ratified (open issue 1).

## Backwards compatibility

New elements and attributes at schema 1.8 (V17); documents at 1.7.1 are unchanged, and `fracture`,
`rigidBody` and `constraint` keep their meaning. The physics frame and the joint semantics are SREP 11's, so a
document exported and re-imported simulates as before.

## Open issues

1. **KHR_physics_rigid_bodies is a draft.** The Khronos extension (with KHR_implicit_shapes) is not ratified;
   rule 10 is stated against the draft and is informative until it is. The property names may change.
2. **z-up stages.** `from_usd` refuses `upAxis` "Z"; a re-orientation by 90° about x is mechanical but changes
   the meaning of the document's gravity and bounds, and the editor should decide whether the engine offers it.
3. **Mesh colliders.** `convex-hull`, `trimesh` and `decomposition` bodies export as `PhysicsMeshCollisionAPI`
   with approximations "convexHull", "none" and "convexDecomposition" on the object's own mesh prim; the
   reference has no triangles and covers the primitive shapes.
4. **Ball joints in the reference.** The chain is planar with hinges; the cone and twist limits of rule 6 are
   stated for the engine and not measured by the reference.
5. **Collision filters are pairwise.** UsdPhysics and KHR filter group pairs symmetrically; SREP 11's
   `collidesWith` on one body is restored exactly by `sr:collidesWith` and approximated by the filtered pairs
   for a foreign reader.
6. **Motor damping.** SREP 11's motor has Rapier's damping factor 10³ m; UsdPhysics's `PhysicsDriveAPI`
   expresses a velocity drive by `damping` in its own units, which the reference does not write.
