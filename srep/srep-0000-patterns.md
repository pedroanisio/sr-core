# SREP 0 — Add pattern systems to the scene

## Abstract

SREP 12's G2: five 2D pattern nodes beside the engine's `flock` and `slime`, each a simulation box of `width` ×
`height` pixels with `resolution` cells along its longer side, stepped at `rate` steps per second from an initial
state the random streams fix, and drawn through a `colorLow`–`color` ramp. **`reaction`** is **Gray**–**Scott**
reaction–diffusion (**Pearson** 1993): `feed`, `kill`, `diffuseU`, `diffuseV`, a seeded `spot` with `noise`, the
**nine-point** **Laplacian** on a **torus**, and a classifier of the pattern it makes (spots, labyrinth, extinct).
**`lenia`** is **Lenia** (**Chan** 2019): a ring kernel of radius `R` with shell weights `beta`, the Gaussian growth
of `mu` and `sigma`, time step 1/`T`, circular convolution (by **FFT** or directly), and the **Orbium** **glider** as
its built-in `creature`, whose **mass** stays and whose speed is steady. **`automaton`** is a **Life**-like
cellular automaton (**Conway**'s **Life** is `rule` B3/S23, **HighLife** B36/S23) on a torus with a **Golly**
run-length `pattern` or a random `density`; the **blinker** has **period** 2, the glider period 4 with a (1, 1)
shift. **`aggregation`** is **diffusion-limited** aggregation (**Witten** and **Sander** 1981): `particles` lattice
walkers whose every step is a stream draw, a `stick` probability, a `launch` margin, and the **radius of gyration**
dimension **1.71** ± 0.05. **`growth`** is **differential growth** of a closed curve: `nodes` on a circle of
`radius`, attraction, alignment and repulsion within `repulsion` cells, stream `jitter`, `insert` nodes a step,
edges split at `maxEdge`, frozen at `maxNodes`. The state at time t is the state after min(`maxSteps`, `prewarm` +
floor(t · `rate`)) steps, so every frame renders alone (SREP 14) and the step index is the frame's, as SREP 69's
stepping programs. Rules PT1–PT5 and V17; the schema files are unchanged, the diff is inside the draft. Reference
module `conformance/tools/patterns_reference.py` (requires **numpy**); engine card SR-111.

## Motivation

- **SREP 12 names the deltas.** Row G2 of the plan: "Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion (Pearson 1993), Lenia (Chan
  2019), cellular automata, diffusion-limited aggregation, differential growth" with the tests "Gray–Scott pattern
  classes; Life periods; DLA fractal dimension 1.71 ± 0.05; Orbium glider mass and speed". This draft is that row.
- **Two agent systems exist, no field system.** The engine's `flock` (Reynolds) and `slime` (Jones) are nodes with
  a box, a resolution, a seed and a ramp; the five systems here are the field-based siblings every science
  explainer about morphogenesis, artificial life or crystal growth reaches for, and none can be declared today.
- **Programs are the wrong level.** SREP 67's `compute` and `iterate` nodes and SREP 69's stepping programs can
  express every one of these systems, but a scene that wants coral or a glider should not ship a WebAssembly
  module; a named node with a dozen attributes is what an author, a validator and a kit case can read.
- **Every frame renders alone.** A frame at t replays the steps from the initial state (SREP 14); the step count
  is a function of t, `rate`, `prewarm` and `maxSteps`, so a seek, a drop and a render from the start agree.
- **The draws are the streams'.** Initial noise, walker steps and curve jitter are draws of the random-streams
  draft indexed by cell, walker and step; a frame can reproduce any one of them.

## Specification

### Syntax

The diff against `scene-render.xsd` 1.7.1 and `scene-render.sch`; the schema files are unchanged in this draft,
the editor applies the diff on acceptance.

Five node elements (`nodeAttributes`, `nodeBehaviour` children, `blend`), all with the **common frame** of
`slime`:

| Attribute | Type | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `width`, `height` | `positiveDecimal` | required | the simulation box in pixels |
| `resolution` | `xs:positiveInteger` | 128 | cells along the longer side, 8 to 2048 (PT1); the other side in proportion |
| `rate` | `positiveDecimal` | 30 | steps per second (PT1) |
| `prewarm` | `xs:nonNegativeInteger` | 0 | steps taken before t = 0 (PT1) |
| `maxSteps` | `xs:positiveInteger` | 100000 | the bound of the step count (PT1) |
| `seed` | `xs:unsignedLong` | — | part of the element seed |
| `saturation` | `positiveDecimal` | 1 | the field value at which the colour reaches `color` (PT1) |
| `color`, `colorLow` | `paintType` | #FFD27AFF, #00000000 | the ramp's ends; literal colours only |
| `blend` | `blendType` | normal | the composite onto what is below |

**`reactionType`** (`reaction`): `feed` (xs:double, 0.055), `kill` (xs:double, 0.062), `diffuseU` (xs:double, 1),
`diffuseV` (xs:double, 0.5), `spot` (unitDecimal, 0.1; the seeded square's side as a share of the shorter side),
`noise` (unitDecimal, 0.01; the seed's relative noise).

**`leniaType`** (`lenia`): `R` (xs:positiveInteger, 13; the kernel radius in cells), `T` (xs:positiveInteger, 10;
steps per unit time), `mu` (xs:double, 0.15), `sigma` (xs:double, 0.015), `beta` (a list of decimals, "1"; the
kernel shell's peak heights, outer last), `creature` (orbium or noise; orbium).

**`automatonType`** (`automaton`): `rule` (xs:string, B3/S23), `pattern` (xs:string, none; Golly RLE), `x`, `y`
(xs:nonNegativeInteger, none; the pattern's top-left cell, centred when absent), `density` (unitDecimal, 0; the
share of live cells of a random start when `pattern` is absent).

**`aggregationType`** (`aggregation`): `particles` (xs:positiveInteger, 2000), `stick` (unitDecimal, 1; the
probability of sticking on contact), `launch` (positiveDecimal, 5; the launch circle's margin beyond the cluster,
cells).

**`growthType`** (`growth`): `nodes` (xs:positiveInteger, 24), `radius` (positiveDecimal, 5; the start circle,
cells), `maxEdge` (positiveDecimal, 1.5), `repulsion` (nonNegativeDecimal, 3; the repulsion radius, cells),
`attraction` (nonNegativeDecimal, 0.5), `alignment` (nonNegativeDecimal, 0.25), `repel` (nonNegativeDecimal,
0.75), `jitter` (nonNegativeDecimal, 0.05), `speed` (positiveDecimal, 0.3; the largest move a step, cells),
`insert` (xs:nonNegativeInteger, 1; nodes inserted a step), `maxNodes` (xs:positiveInteger, 5000).

**Schematron** (`scene-render.sch`):

- **PT1** On every pattern node: `resolution` in 8..2048; `rate` > 0; 0 ≤ `prewarm` ≤ `maxSteps`; `maxSteps` ≥ 1;
  `width`, `height`, `saturation` > 0.
- **PT2** On a `reaction`: `feed`, `kill`, `spot`, `noise` in [0, 1]; 0 < `diffuseV` ≤ `diffuseU` ≤ 1. On a
  `lenia`: `R` ≥ 1 and 2 `R` + 1 ≤ the shorter side in cells; `T` ≥ 1; `mu`, `sigma` > 0; `beta` non-empty with
  every value in (0, 1]; `creature` one of the two.
- **PT3** On an `automaton`: `rule` is B&lt;digits&gt;/S&lt;digits&gt; with digits 0–8 each at most once; `pattern`
  parses as RLE and fits the grid; `density` in [0, 1]; `x` < the width and `y` < the height in cells.
- **PT4** On an `aggregation`: 1 ≤ `particles` ≤ 1 000 000; 0 < `stick` ≤ 1; `launch` > 0.
- **PT5** On a `growth`: `nodes` ≥ 3; `maxNodes` ≥ `nodes`; `radius`, `maxEdge`, `speed` > 0; `repulsion`,
  `attraction`, `alignment`, `repel`, `jitter`, `insert` ≥ 0.
- **V17** (extended) the five elements require `scene/@version` 1.8 or later.

### Semantics

The reference is `conformance/tools/patterns_reference.py` (`grid`, `steps_at`, `element_seed`, `noise_field`,
`gray_scott`, `classify`, `lenia`, `lenia_track`, `automaton`, `period`, `aggregate`, `fractal_dimension`, `grow`,
`run`, `validate_document`); it uses numpy for the fields and the FFT. Cells are indexed (row, col) with row down
and col right; a field's shape is (ny, nx).

**1. The frame.** `grid`: nx = `resolution` and ny = round(`resolution` · `height` / `width`) when `width` ≥
`height`, the mirror otherwise, at least 1. `steps_at`: the step count of a frame at time t is
min(`maxSteps`, `prewarm` + max(0, floor(t · `rate` + 10⁻⁹))); the state drawn at t is the initial state stepped
that many times. The count is a function of the frame alone (SREP 14); it is the step index of SREP 69's
`step`, counted from 0 including prewarm steps, and `maxSteps` is the bound ordinary code owns.

**2. Draws.** The element seed is e = E(project, id, `seed`, "stream") (the random-streams draft, as `flock` and
`slime`). `noise_field`: the initial noise of cell (row, col) is S(e, 1, 0, row · nx + col) in [0, 1). Walker k of
an aggregation launches at the angle 2π S(e, 2, k, 0) and takes its i-th step in the direction of S(e, 2, k, 2i),
with S(e, 2, k, 2i + 1) its stick test. A curve node j at step s is jittered by (S(e, 3, s, 2j) − ½,
S(e, 3, s, 2j + 1) − ½), and the j-th insertion of that step among n nodes takes edge floor(n S(e, 3, s, 2n + j)).

**3. Reaction.** `reaction_initial`: U = 1 and V = 0 everywhere but a centred square of side round(`spot` ·
min(nx, ny)) cells, where U = ½ (1 + w) and V = ¼ (1 − w) with w = `noise` · (2 r − 1) and r the cell's noise draw
(Pearson's start). `laplacian`: the nine-point stencil on the torus, sides 0.2, corners 0.05, centre −1.
`gray_scott`, one step with dt = 1: U ← clip(U + `diffuseU` ∇²U − U V² + `feed` (1 − U), 0, 1) and V ← clip(V +
`diffuseV` ∇²V + U V² − (`feed` + `kill`) V, 0, 1), both from the step's start values. `classify`: with the mask
V > 0.1, its share φ, its four-connected components on the torus (`label`, `components`) and the area-weighted
mean of 4π A / P² over them (A cells, P boundary edges): extinct when φ < 10⁻³, uniform when φ > 0.99, spots when
the mean is ≥ 0.35 and there are at least four components, mixed when ≥ 0.35 with fewer, labyrinth otherwise.
Pearson's parameter points are the classes' names: (0.0367, 0.0649) divides into spots, (0.055, 0.062) grows a
labyrinthine coral, (0.03, 0.07) dies.

**4. Lenia.** `kernel_core`(r) = exp(4 − 1 / (r (1 − r))) on (0, 1), 0 outside (peak 1 at ½); `growth_map`(u) =
2 exp(−(u − `mu`)² / (2 `sigma`²)) − 1. `lenia_kernel`: on the torus with the wrapped distance d of a cell from
(0, 0) and r = d / `R`, with B = |`beta`|, K = `beta`[min(floor(B r), B − 1)] · core(B r − floor(B r)) for r < 1,
normalised to sum 1. One step: A ← clip(A + (1 / `T`) G(K ∗ A), 0, 1), K ∗ A the circular convolution (the
reference uses the FFT; a direct sum is the same to rounding). The start: `creature` orbium places the 20 × 20
Orbium unicaudatus cells (`orbium`, decoded from Bert Chan's catalogue by `decode_cells`: '.' 0, 'A'..'X' 1..24, a
prefix 'p'..'y' adds 24 a letter from 25, digits repeat, '$' ends a row, all over 255) at the grid's centre; noise
starts from the noise field. `mass` is the field's sum; `centroid` the circular mean on the torus; `lenia_track`
samples (step, mass, centroid) and `lenia_speed` the mean centroid speed in cells a step. The Orbium (R 13, T 10,
mu 0.15, sigma 0.015, beta 1) keeps its mass within 10 % over 120 steps and moves at a steady speed (the table).

**5. Automaton.** `parse_rule`: B⟨digits⟩/S⟨digits⟩, the neighbour counts (Moore, eight cells, on the torus)
that give birth to a dead cell and that let a live cell survive. `parse_rle`: Golly's run-length encoding, `b`
dead, `o` alive, `$` end of row, `!` end, a count repeating what follows it; rows pad with dead cells; a pattern
with no live cell is an error. The pattern's top-left cell is (`x`, `y`), or ((nx − w + 1) div 2, (ny − h + 1) div
2) when absent, wrapping on the torus; a pattern wider or taller than the grid is PT3. Without a `pattern` a cell
starts alive when its noise draw is below `density`. `period`(p ≤ max): the smallest p after which the start
repeats up to a translation, with that translation in its shortest wrapped form: the blinker is (2, (0, 0)), the
glider (4, (1, 1)), a still life (1, (0, 0)).

**6. Aggregation.** `aggregate`: the seed particle is the grid's centre (nx div 2, ny div 2); rmax the largest
distance of a particle from it. Walkers launch one at a time, k = 1, 2, …, from the point at rmax + `launch` cells
and the launch angle, rounded to a cell. Each step i draws u; the direction is (+x, +y, −x, −y)[floor(4u)]; the
length is floor(d − rmax − 1) cells when the walker is farther than rmax + 2 from the seed (no particle can lie on
that jump), else 1; a step onto an occupied cell is not taken. When a four-neighbour of the walker is occupied it
sticks (becomes the next particle) with probability `stick`, drawn as rule 2 says. A walker farther than
2 (rmax + `launch`) + 10 from the seed or stepping outside the grid is lost. The aggregate stops when it has
`particles` particles, when the launch circle no longer fits the grid, or after 100 · `particles` launches. The
state at a frame is the first min(steps, `particles`) particles: one particle a step. `fractal_dimension` of a
growth sequence: with Rg(m) the radius of gyration of the first m points, D = 1 / slope of log Rg against log m,
least squares over twelve counts spaced geometrically from n / 8 to n (Witten and Sander 1981); the example
cluster of 2000 is within 0.05 of 1.71 and a disc grown outward gives 2.

**7. Growth.** `growth_initial`: `nodes` nodes on a circle of `radius` cells about the box's centre. A step, for
every node with neighbours prev and next along the closed curve: force = `attraction` (û(prev) + û(next)) +
`alignment` ((prev + next) / 2 − p) + `repel` Σ (p − q) (1 − d / `repulsion`) / d over the other nodes q within
`repulsion` cells + `jitter` · the node's jitter draw; the node moves by `speed` · force / max(1, |force|) and is
clamped to the box. Then `insert` nodes are added at the midpoints of the stream-chosen edges (rule 2), and every
edge longer than `maxEdge` is split at its midpoint (`split_edges`), the whole split skipped when it would pass
`maxNodes`; insertion stops at `maxNodes`, and a curve with `maxNodes` nodes takes no further step (frozen).
`curve_length` and `self_intersections` (pairs of non-adjacent edges that cross) describe the result; the example
curve grows from 24 nodes without a crossing (the table).

**8. Drawing.** A `reaction` draws V, a `lenia` draws A, both divided by `saturation`, clamped to [0, 1] and mapped
through `ramp`: `colorLow` at 0 to `color` at 1, each channel rounded. An `automaton` draws its live cells in
`color` on `colorLow`; an `aggregation` its particles in `color` (one cell each); a `growth` its curve as a closed
polyline in `color`, one cell wide. The box is composited with `blend`, as `slime`.

**9. Output.** `run`(doc, t) reports every element's step count and a summary (class, cover and means of a
reaction; mass, centroid and peak of a lenia; live cells of an automaton; particle count, radius and launches of an
aggregation; node count, length and crossings of a growth) and a digest of them all; the digest at t is the same
however the frame is reached.

## Rationale

- **Five elements, not one.** The attributes of the five systems barely overlap (a reaction has rates, an
  automaton a rule string, a curve a node budget); one `pattern` element with a `kind` would carry thirty optional
  attributes and a Schematron rule per kind anyway. Five typed elements read as `flock` and `slime` do.
- **The step count belongs to the frame.** A fixed `rate` makes the state a pure function of t; a per-frame step
  count would tie the picture to the frame rate. `prewarm` lets a reaction start mid-pattern, `maxSteps` bounds
  the replay however far t goes.
- **Pearson's normalisation at Du = 1.** With dt = 1 and the nine-point stencil the update at Du = 1 is the
  neighbourhood average, the stability edge; Pearson's (f, k) map transfers with Dv = Du / 2.
- **Lattice walkers with stream steps.** An off-lattice walker needs a neighbour search per step and a floating
  contact test; the lattice walk is exact, every step a named draw, and the jump outside the cluster keeps it fast
  without changing where it can stick.
- **Growth by insertion.** Repulsion alone cannot lengthen a curve whose nodes are pulled together; the classic
  algorithm grows by inserting nodes and lets repulsion fold the surplus, and that is what `insert` does.
- **A frozen curve.** At `maxNodes` the edges would stretch past `maxEdge` with no split allowed; freezing is the
  bound that keeps every invariant.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Programs (SREP 67, SREP 69) only.** Rejected: an author cannot read a WebAssembly module, and a kit case
  cannot name its parameters.
- **A `kind` attribute on one element.** Rejected for the attribute sprawl above.
- **Off-lattice aggregation.** Rejected for the per-step neighbour search and the rounding of the contact test.
- **Box-counting dimension.** Rejected: a 2000-particle cluster has one-cell branches at small boxes and the
  estimate drifts toward 1; the radius-of-gyration scaling is Witten and Sander's measurement.
- **A numpy-free reference.** Rejected: 4000 steps of a 128 × 128 reaction and the FFT of a 64 × 64 Lenia are
  minutes in pure Python; `srep39_reference.py` already requires numpy and CI installs it.

## Backwards compatibility

New elements only; a 1.7 scene is unchanged and a scene that uses one of them declares 1.8 (V17).

## Open issues

1. **Three-dimensional fields.** Lenia and Gray–Scott extend to 3D volumes (the scalar-field draft's grids); this
   draft is planar.
2. **Multi-channel Lenia.** Chan (2020) adds channels and kernels between them; one channel and one kernel here.
3. **Automaton families.** Larger-than-Life, Generations and one-dimensional elementary rules are not expressible
   in B/S.
4. **Sticking to a mask.** An aggregation that grows from a shape (a terrain mask, text) rather than a point.
5. **Growth in three dimensions and on surfaces.** The curve is planar; differential growth of a mesh is not here.
6. **Rendering of the curve.** One-cell polyline; a stroke width and a fill are open.
7. **Lenia orientation.** The Orbium's direction of travel is the catalogue's; a `rotate` of the creature is open.
