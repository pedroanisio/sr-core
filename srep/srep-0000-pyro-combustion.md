# SREP 0 — Add combustion and sparse tiles to pyro

## Abstract

[SREP 12](srep-0012.md) names, as its S2 building block, "stable fluids with MacCormack advection and vorticity
confinement, fuel–temperature combustion, on sparse grids, rendered through A2", with the bars "divergence below
ε after projection; symmetric plume from a symmetric set-up" (line 295), and records that the `pyro` solver of
[SREP 40](srep-0040.md) and [SREP 78](srep-0078.md) has landed its advection, confinement and projection while
"combustion and sparse grids are open". This SREP adds the two: a **fuel** channel fed by `pyroSource/@fuelRate`
and `pyroImpulse/@fuel` and burnt by a `pyroCombustion` child (ignition temperature, burn rate, heat, expansion
and soot per unit fuel), so that fire is hot smoke whose glow is the A2 medium's blackbody emission of the
temperature channel; and **sparse tiles** (`pyro/@tile`, `@tileMargin`, `@tileThreshold`), a step that advects,
forces and projects only the tiles holding smoke, fuel or heat and their margin, with the rest of the domain at
rest. It also states the two bars as rules with their tolerances and adds a floor to the confinement direction so
a symmetric set-up stays symmetric. `conformance/tools/pyro_reference.py` restates the solver of SREP 40 in the
standard library and measures the bars: an RMS divergence error below 10⁻⁸ after the projection, a plume that
mirrors within 5 × 10⁻¹⁰ of its peak, fuel conserved through burning, a fire that rises faster than its smoke,
and a sparse run within 3 × 10⁻⁴ of the dense run with 56 % of the tiles active.

## Motivation

- **Shot 29** (SREP 12 line 617), the boosters tumbling away with exhaust plumes, is "S2 through A2": the plumes
  are burning fuel, not smoke, and their light is their own. Without a fuel channel the author keys a temperature
  by hand and the plume cannot flare, choke or burn out.
- **Memory and time grow with the box, not with the smoke.** A `pyro` domain of 128³ cells costs the same
  whether the plume fills it or a candle flickers in one corner (`crates/sr-sim/src/pyro.rs`, `max_bytes`); the
  following window of SREP 78 moves the box but does not shrink the work. Tiles make the cost follow the smoke.
- **The bars of the plan are not yet rules.** SREP 40 states the projection's tolerance as a global RMS
  (line 627) and nothing states symmetry. The reference module here gives both a number.

### Semantics

The rules restate the solver of SREP 40 where this draft touches it and add the rest; `pyro_reference.py`
states every rule as a function. Positions are in cells (cell (i, j, k) has its centre at (i + ½, j + ½,
k + ½)); the vertical axis is the document's y, and hot air accelerates toward negative y (SREP 40 line 508).

1. **The grid** (`grid`, `index`, `coords`, `sample`, `velocity_at`). Density, temperature and **fuel** are
   cell-centred; each velocity component sits on the faces normal to its axis (`cells[a] + 1` along that axis).
   Fuel starts at 0 everywhere. A cell-centred field is sampled trilinearly between the eight nearest centres,
   clamped to the border cells; a face component between its faces.
2. **Advection** (`advect`, SREP 40 lines 637 to 660). Each channel is carried by the midpoint semi-Lagrangian
   trace, or by MacCormack with the extrema limiter when `advection="maccormack"`; density, temperature and fuel
   share their traces. Dissipation and cooling then apply to the carried value: density × e^(−dissipation dt),
   excess temperature × e^(−cooling dt); **fuel is neither dissipated nor cooled**.
3. **Sources** (`apply_sources`). A source active at the step's start time adds `densityRate` dt,
   `temperatureRate` dt and `fuelRate` dt to each cell whose centre it contains, `velocityRate*` dt to the faces
   of those cells, and its `expansion` to the step's divergence target in those cells. An impulse adds its
   `density`, `temperature` and `fuel` once.
4. **Combustion** (`burn`). After the sources, in every cell with fuel above 0 and temperature at or above
   `ignition`, the fuel burnt is the cell's fuel × (1 − e^(−rate dt)); the fuel falls by it, the temperature
   rises by `heat` × burnt, the density by `soot` × burnt, and the divergence target by `expansion` × burnt / dt.
   Cold fuel waits, and burning conserves fuel plus burnt exactly; the kit's hot pocket burns 99.98 % of 2 units
   over 40 steps and reaches 1100 K from 600 K. Fire is then hot smoke: its glow is the medium's blackbody
   emission of the temperature channel (SREP 40 line 325, `blackbody="true"`), scaled by `temperatureScale`.
5. **Forces** (`forces`, `curl`). Each cell's acceleration is the external one, minus `buoyancy` × (T − ambient)
   along y, plus the confinement `vorticity` × h × (N × ω) with ω the curl of the interpolated velocity by central
   differences one cell apart and N = ∇|ω| / |∇|ω|| by central differences over the neighbouring cells, clamped
   at the walls. **Cells whose |∇|ω|| is below 10⁻⁹ × max|ω| / h get no confinement force** (`CONFINEMENT_FLOOR`):
   on the mirror plane of a symmetric set-up ∇|ω| is rounding, and normalising it would give a force whose
   direction is noise. An interior face takes dt × the mean of its two cells' accelerations; a domain face takes
   its one cell's when the boundary is open and nothing when closed.
6. **The projection** (`project`, `divergence`, SREP 40 lines 585 to 632). With u the face velocities, the
   divergence of a cell is the sum of its outward face velocities over h. The step solves −∇²p = (target − div u)
   / dt over the fluid cells by Jacobi-preconditioned conjugate gradients from p = 0, with reductions in cell
   index order: a closed wall is a no-through face (its velocity is zeroed first; a pure Neumann system has the
   mean of its right-hand side removed, which stays in the error measured after the solve, so **a closed domain
   cannot expand**), an open wall and a cell outside the fluid set are at zero pressure. Iteration stops when
   dt × the RMS residual is at most `pressureTolerance`; reaching `pressureIterations` first is an error, as is a
   recomputed RMS error above 1.01 × the tolerance. The faces between fluid cells, and the open faces of fluid
   cells, lose dt (p_hi − p_lo) / h. **The bar:** the kit's open 8-cube with an RMS divergence of 3.76 reaches an
   error of 8.4 × 10⁻⁹ in 32 iterations with the worst cell at 2.6 × 10⁻⁸.
7. **The step** (`step`, `run`). A step is: advect, sources, combustion, forces, projection; the step count
   rises by one. The state after n steps is a function of n and the document alone: `run(12)` equals `run(6)`
   continued by `run(6)`, so a frame rendered alone equals the frame played (the seek check of
   [SREP 14](srep-0014.md) rule 1).
8. **Active tiles** (`active_tiles`). With `tile` t, the domain is tiles of t³ cells. Before a step the active
   set is decided from the committed state: the tiles holding a cell with density above `tileThreshold`, fuel
   above it, or |T − ambient| above `tileThreshold` × ambient, dilated by `tileMargin` tiles along every axis
   (Chebyshev). **Velocity alone wakes no tile**, and the thresholds are not zero: a semi-Lagrangian support
   grows by one cell per step at any speed, so a zero floor would wake the whole domain in as many steps as it
   has tiles. The kit's one lit cell wakes 27 tiles of 4 with a margin of 1.
9. **The sparse step** (`step_sparse`). The faces with no active cell are cleared, so the cells outside the
   active set are at rest, ambient and empty; advection, forces and the projection run over the active cells
   only, the projection taking the cells outside the set at zero pressure (rule 6). A sparse run is a function
   of the step count as a dense one is. **The bar:** on the kit's 16 × 12 × 16 open plume with tiles of 4 and a
   margin of 1, 56 % of the tiles are active after 12 steps and the density is within 3 × 10⁻⁴ of the dense
   run's (peak 0.6); the deviation is the pressure that the dense run spreads beyond the margin.
10. **The symmetric plume** (`symmetric_plume`, `mirror_error`). A set-up that mirrors across a cell plane of
    the grid (the kit's hot source at the centre of the bottom of a closed 8 × 12 × 8 box, with buoyancy and
    confinement) gives a density that mirrors within 10⁻⁷ of its peak after 30 steps with either advection
    scheme; the measured errors are 7 × 10⁻¹⁰ (semi-Lagrangian) and 7 × 10⁻⁹ (MacCormack, whose limiter is the
    more sensitive), the residue of the ordered reductions of rule 6. The plume rises: its density centroid is
    above the source after 30 steps, and a fire of the same source with fuel rises faster and hotter than its
    smoke (1747 K against 887 K).
11. **Bounds.** 2²¹ cells, 65536 steps per run, 10000 conjugate-gradient iterations (`pressureIterations`),
    tiles of 4 to 32 cells; every loop is owned by the document and the solver, none by convergence.

## Rationale

- **Fuel as a fourth channel, not a particle system.** Fuel is carried by the same traces as smoke, so a flame
  is where its fuel and heat are, and the seek check holds with no extra state. The model is the gas-phase
  reduction of Nguyen, Fedkiw and Jensen (2002): a burn rate above ignition, heat and expansion from the fuel
  burnt, soot as the product; their level-set flame front is not needed for cinematic fire.
- **Expansion through the divergence target.** The step already projects to an authored target (SREP 40's
  `expansion`); combustion adds to it, so the solver is unchanged and PYRO15 follows from the closed-domain rule.
- **Tiles over a sparse page table.** The rule is stated on the dense arrays so the reference is a few lines;
  SPGrid (Setaluri et al. 2014) is the storage a renderer would use, with the same active set.
- **The threshold is a document value.** What counts as nothing is a rendering question (how faint a smoke still
  shows); the default 10⁻⁶ is below any visible density and well above the trilinear leak of a slow trace.
- **The confinement floor is relative.** The engine's absolute 10⁻¹² (`pyro.rs:1808`) is passed by rounding on
  a mirror plane; a share of the peak curl over h scales with the scene.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Waking tiles by velocity.** It is what the following window of SREP 78 does for its reach, but for tiles it
  grows the active set to the domain within a few steps (every face next to the active set moves).
- **Sealing the active set's frontier.** A no-through frontier makes the active region a closed box, so a burning
  plume with expansion could not run; zero pressure outside lets the air leave as it does through an open wall.
- **A level-set flame (Nguyen et al. 2002).** Exact for premixed flames, but a second simulation with its own
  reinitialisation; the burn-rate model gives the look of shot 29 with one channel.
- **Adaptive projection tolerances per tile.** A tolerance that depends on the active set would make the result
  depend on the tiling; the tolerance stays global.

## Backwards compatibility

New optional attributes and a new child at schema 1.8 (V17); a document without them renders as before, bit for
bit, except where confinement acted on a cell whose |∇|ω|| was between 10⁻¹² and 10⁻⁹ × max|ω| / h, which the
editor may accept as a fix or gate behind the version (open issue 1).

| `srep-<n>-pyro-tiles.xml` | the plume of rule 9 with `tile="4"` and without: the active tiles are below 60 % and the density within 10⁻³ of the dense run's peak | to be measured |
| `srep-<n>-pyro-closed-fire.xml` | a burning source with expansion in a closed domain is refused by PYRO15 | to be measured |

## Open issues

1. **The confinement floor and old documents.** Whether the relative floor applies to every version (a fix of a
   rounding artefact) or only from 1.8 (bit-exact replay of old bakes, SREP 40's cache contract).
2. **Oxygen.** The burn rate ignores oxygen; a fire in a sealed room does not starve. An oxygen channel consumed
   by burning would add one more field.
3. **Tiles and the following window.** Both compose (the window moves, the tiles live inside it), but the window's
   decision of SREP 78 rule 1.4 sums every slab; it could sum the active tiles only.
4. **Multigrid on tiles.** The aggregation of SREP 40 over an active set with holes needs a statement of its
   coarse operator.
5. **Soot colour.** Soot is density; a `sootAlbedo` on the medium would darken it against white smoke.
