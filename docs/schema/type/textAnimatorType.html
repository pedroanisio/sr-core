    textAnimatorType

    Type of <textAnimator> in layerType

    Per-character/word/line animation of a text layer (After Effects text animators, Lottie t.a, Rive text modifiers). A range selector picks the units in [start, end] + offset (percent or index) with a falloff @shape; each selected unit receives the property offsets below scaled by the selection amount. @stagger/@overlap give preset-style sequential timing. @preset expands to a documented animator (typewriter, word-by-word, ...). A nested expression with property="selector" makes an expression selector.

    preset
    one of 24 valuestypewriter fade-in fade-out word-by-word letter-by-letter line-by-line slide-up slide-down slide-left slide-right pop scale-in blur-in wave bounce spin ascend shift scramble counter karaoke highlight tracking-in mask-reveal

    Applies a built-in text animation (such as typewriter, fade-in, wave or counter); explicit property attributes override the preset's values. Of the selector only order, amount and seed take part, not the range window.

    presetStartxs:double

    Time on the layer's parent timeline (composition time for a top-level layer), not an offset from the layer's start; when absent the preset starts at the layer's own start.

    presetDurationpositiveDecimal

    Length in seconds of the preset's run across all its units when no stagger is given; 1 second when absent. Has no effect without a preset.

    presetEaseone of linear quad-in quad-out cubic-out expo-out back-out bounce-out

    The curve of a preset, in place of the preset's own (most use cubic-out, which is front-loaded: at 40 % of the duration it is 78 % done). Without @preset it has no effect.

    unitone of character character-no-space word line spancharacter

    Which text units the animator selects: each character, each non-space character, each word, each line, or each span. A preset's own unit is replaced only by a value other than character.

    spanxs:NMTOKEN

    Restrict to the span whose @role matches.

    selectorone of range wiggly expressionrange

    Selection method: range (a start/end window with a falloff), wiggly (animated noise), or expression. A nested expression with property="selector" always selects the expression method.

    rangeUnitsone of percent indexpercent

    Whether @start, @end and @offset of the range selector are percentages of the unit count (percent) or zero-based unit indices (index). Not used when @preset is set.

    startxs:double0

    Start of the range selector window, in percent of the units or in unit indices (see @rangeUnits); 0 by default. Not used when @preset is set.

    endxs:double100

    End of the range selector window, in percent of the units or in unit indices (see @rangeUnits); 100 by default. Not used when @preset is set.

    offsetxs:double0

    Shifts the whole range selector window by this amount, in percent of the units or in unit indices (see @rangeUnits). Not used when @preset is set.

    amountpercentType100

    Selection strength in percent (default 100): selected units get this share of the animator's property offsets; negative values reverse them. For the wiggly selector it is the noise amplitude.

    shapeone of square ramp-up ramp-down triangle round smoothsquare

    Falloff of the range selector across its window: square (hard edge), ramp-up, ramp-down, triangle, round or smooth (raised cosine). Outside the window the others give 0; ramp-up gives 1 after it, ramp-down 1 before.

    smoothnessunitDecimal1

    For the square shape only, blends from a hard edge (0) to fractional coverage of the units at the window's ends (1, default). Not used when @preset is set.

    easeHighpercentType0

    Percent. Together with @easeLow, blends the range falloff toward a smooth-step curve by (easeHigh + easeLow) / 200, clamped to 0-1; 0 leaves the shape unchanged. Not used with @preset.

    easeLowpercentType0

    Percent. Together with @easeHigh, blends the range falloff toward a smooth-step curve by (easeHigh + easeLow) / 200, clamped to 0-1; 0 leaves the shape unchanged. Not used with @preset.

    orderone of forward reverse center-out edges-in randomforward

    Order in which units are placed along the range selector window: forward, reverse, center-out, edges-in, or a seeded random permutation. A preset takes its unit order from it too. Range selector only.

    staggernonNegativeDecimal

    Delay in seconds between the starts of consecutive units, for presets other than typewriter, word-by-word, wave, scramble and counter. Absent, the delay follows from presetDuration and the overlap.

    overlapunitDecimal0

    Fraction 0-1 of each unit's window that overlaps the previous unit's, for presets other than typewriter, word-by-word, wave, scramble and counter. 0 or absent keeps the preset's own overlap.

    seedxs:unsignedLong

    Seed for this animator's random order and wiggly selector, combined with the project seed (alone when absent); scramble letters also depend on the seeds of every animator on the layer.

    wiggleRatenonNegativeDecimal2

    Speed of the wiggly selector's noise, in noise steps per second (default 2). Only used with selector wiggly, and not when @preset is set.

    combineone of add multiply replaceadd

    How offsets (position, rotation, skew, tracking, ...) combine with earlier animators on a unit: add, multiply as 1+(v-1)*selection, or replace. Scale and opacity always multiply, or replace.

    xxs:double

    Horizontal offset in pixels, applied to a unit in proportion to its selection. On text with a textPath it is applied before placement on the path, so it follows the glyph direction unless perpendicular is off.

    yxs:double

    Vertical offset in pixels (positive is down), applied to a unit in proportion to its selection. With a textPath it is applied before placement on the path, so it follows the glyph direction unless perpendicular is off.

    zDepthxs:double

    Depth offset in pixels at full selection; larger values push the unit away and shrink it with a perspective factor of 1000 / (1000 + z).

    scalexs:double

    Uniform scale factor at full selection (1 = unchanged); a unit's scale is 1 + (scale - 1) times its selection.

    scaleXxs:double

    Horizontal scale factor at full selection (1 = unchanged), multiplied with @scale.

    scaleYxs:double

    Vertical scale factor at full selection (1 = unchanged), multiplied with @scale.

    rotationxs:double

    Rotation in degrees about the unit's pivot, applied in proportion to its selection.

    rotationXxs:double

    Rotation in degrees about the horizontal axis, shown as vertical foreshortening by the cosine of the angle.

    rotationYxs:double

    Rotation in degrees about the vertical axis, shown as horizontal foreshortening by the cosine of the angle.

    skewxs:double

    Horizontal skew angle in degrees, applied in proportion to the unit's selection.

    opacityunitDecimal

    Opacity factor 0-1 at full selection (1 = unchanged); a unit's opacity is multiplied by 1 + (opacity - 1) times its selection.

    fillpaintType

    Fill paint that selected units blend toward by their selection share (clamped to 0-1); gradient and image paints switch at half share instead. For the highlight preset it is the box paint instead.

    strokepaintType

    Stroke paint that selected units blend toward by their selection share (clamped to 0-1); gradient and image paints switch at half share. A unit without a stroke gets one only if strokeWidth or the share is above 0.

    strokeWidthnonNegativeDecimal

    Stroke width offset in pixels, added to the unit's stroke width in proportion to its selection. It shows only where the unit has a stroke, from the text style or from @stroke.

    trackingxs:double

    Extra space after each unit's characters, in thousandths of an em, applied in proportion to the selection; it accumulates along the line.

    lineSpacingxs:double

    Extra vertical offset in pixels per line index (line 0 unchanged, line 1 once, ...), applied in proportion to the selection.

    blurnonNegativeDecimal

    Blur radius in pixels at full selection, scaled by the unit's selection; a radius under 0.5 px draws no blur.

    baselineShiftxs:double

    Baseline shift in pixels; positive raises the unit, applied in proportion to its selection.

    characterOffsetxs:integer

    Shifts each letter A-Z, a-z or digit by this many positions (rounded, scaled by the unit's selection) within its own class, wrapping; other characters, including non-ASCII letters, are unchanged.

    variationxs:string

    Variable-font axis targets such as "wght 700, wdth 80"; selected units move toward these axis values by their selection share.

    anchorXxs:double

    Horizontal offset in pixels of the pivot for rotation, scale and skew from the centre of the unit's box.

    anchorYxs:double

    Vertical offset in pixels of the pivot for rotation, scale and skew from the vertical centre of the unit's line box.
