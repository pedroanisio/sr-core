    softBodyType

    Type of <softBody> in layerType, shapeType

    Content

    Empty: attributes only.

    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    masspositiveDecimal1

    Total mass of the soft body in kilograms, shared equally among its simulated points (a rope simulates only its chain points). Default 1.

    stiffnesspositiveDecimal20

    Stiffness in newtons per metre of the lattice springs (a cloth's shear and bend springs use weaker fractions of it); higher values hold the shape more rigidly. Default 20.

    dampingunitDecimal0.1

    Damping along each spring, as a fraction of 2*sqrt(spring stiffness * point mass); 0 adds none and larger values resist the springs' relative motion more strongly. Default 0.1.

    pressurexs:double0

    Jelly only: strength of the force that pushes the outline outward when its area falls below the rest area and inward when it grows; 0 (default) adds none.

    rowsgridSizeType4

    Number of lattice rows (2 to 16, default 4); jelly and cloth have rows+1 point rows. A rope is one chain of cols points, whatever rows is.

    colsgridSizeType4

    Number of lattice columns (2 to 16, default 4); jelly and cloth have cols+1 point columns, a rope has this many points along its chain.

    pinone of none top bottom left right cornersnone

    Lattice points held fixed: none (default), the top, bottom, left or right edge, or the four corners. A rope's chain point is held when any lattice point in its column is.

    kindone of jelly cloth ropejelly

    jelly (default): a lattice of full-strength springs, optionally pressurised; cloth: a sheet with weak shear and bend springs so it drapes; rope: a single chain of points along the middle of the box.

    selfCollisionxs:booleanfalse

    When true, lattice points closer than half the shortest spring rest length push each other apart; false (default) lets the body pass through itself.
