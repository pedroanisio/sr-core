    masterType

    Type of <master> in audioMixType

    Master bus. normalize="integrated" measures ITU-R BS.1770 integrated loudness of the full mix and applies static gain to reach @loudness LUFS, then the true-peak limiter caps at @truePeak dBTP.

    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    volumeunitDecimal1

    Linear level multiplier of the whole mix from 0 to 1, applied after the master effects; defaults to 1, and it can be animated.

    normalizeone of none integrated dynamicnone

    Loudness normalisation of the mix: none (default), integrated for one static gain reaching the target loudness, or dynamic for slowly riding gain that follows short-term loudness.

    loudnesslufsType-14

    Target loudness in LUFS for integrated or dynamic normalisation; defaults to -14.

    truePeakdecibelType-1

    Ceiling in dBTP of the true-peak limiter, which runs only when limiter is true or normalize is not none; defaults to -1.

    limiterxs:booleanfalse

    Whether the true-peak limiter runs even without normalisation; it always runs when normalisation is on; defaults to false.

    ditherxs:booleantrue

    Whether TPDF dither is added when the mix is written directly as a 16- or 24-bit audio-only WAV file; defaults to true. Other outputs do not use it.
