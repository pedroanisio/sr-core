    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    id requiredxs:ID

    Identifier of the light, required: a viewport3D names it in lights, a lighting effect in its lights, and the light's animated properties and constraints are keyed by it.

    type requiredone of ambient directional point spot rect-area disk-area sphere-area dome

    Kind of light: ambient, directional, point, spot, rect-area, disk-area, sphere-area or dome. A dome lights from an environment image or a gradient sky; with neither it lights uniformly like an ambient light.

    colorcolorType#FFFFFFFF

    Colour of the light, default white; a colorTemperature tint multiplies it.

    colorTemperaturekelvinType

    Colour temperature in kelvin that tints the light with the matching blackbody colour, multiplied with the colour; absent means no tint. A lighting effect ignores it.

    intensitylightIntensityType1

    Brightness multiplier of the light; defaults to 1.

    exposurexs:double0

    Brightness adjustment in stops: the intensity is multiplied by 2 to the power of this value; defaults to 0. A lighting effect ignores it.

    rangepositiveDecimal

    Distance in scene units at which a point, spot or area light fades to zero; absent or 0 means no cutoff. A lighting effect instead reads it in node pixels, with absent meaning half the frame height.

    falloffpositiveDecimal2

    Exponent of the distance attenuation of point, spot and area lights: intensity falls as 1 over (distance/100) to this power; defaults to 2.

    spotAnglespotAngleType45

    Full opening angle of a spot light's cone in degrees; defaults to 45.

    innerConeAnglenonNegativeDecimal

    Full angle in degrees of the spot cone's fully lit core, with a smooth edge out to the spot angle; absent means 80 percent of the spot angle.

    castShadowxs:booleanfalse

    Whether the light casts shadows; defaults to false. It has no effect on ambient and dome lights.

    shadowSoftnessnonNegativeDecimal0

    Width of the shadow edge blur, in shadow-map texels, with a minimum effective value of 1; defaults to 0. Only lights with castShadow true use it.

    shadowBiasxs:double0.0005

    Depth offset, in normalised device depth, subtracted before a surface is compared with the shadow map, to hide self-shadowing; larger values detach shadows from their casters. Default 0.0005.

    xxs:double0

    Horizontal position of the light in scene units; defaults to 0.

    yxs:double0

    Vertical position of the light in scene units; defaults to 0.

    zxs:double0

    Depth position of the light in scene units; defaults to 0.

    yawxs:double0

    Rotation of the light about the vertical axis in degrees; defaults to 0.

    pitchxs:double0

    Rotation of the light about the horizontal axis in degrees; defaults to 0.

    rollxs:double0

    Rotation of the light about its own pointing axis in degrees; defaults to 0.

    widthpositiveDecimal

    Width of a rect-area light in scene units (absent means 50). A disk-area light takes its radius from half of it only when radius is absent.

    heightpositiveDecimal

    Height of a rect-area light in scene units; absent means the same as the width.

    radiuspositiveDecimal

    Radius of a disk-area or sphere-area light in scene units; absent means half the width for a disk and 10 otherwise.

    iesxs:anyURI

    Path to an IES photometric profile that shapes the light's angular distribution; an unreadable profile is reported as an error.

    environmentxs:anyURI

    type="dome": equirectangular HDRI used for image-based lighting and reflections.

    environmentVisiblexs:booleanfalse

    Whether a dome light's environment or sky is drawn as the background; defaults to false.

    affectsDiffusexs:booleantrue

    Whether the light contributes to diffuse shading; defaults to true.

    affectsSpecularxs:booleantrue

    Whether the light contributes to specular highlights; defaults to true.

    shadowMapSizexs:positiveInteger in [16, 8192]2048

    Side of the square shadow map in texels, 16 to 8192, default 2048; shadow-casting lights share one map size, the largest requested, and it may be reduced to fit a memory budget.

    contactShadowsxs:booleanfalse

    Whether screen-space contact shadows add fine shadow detail the shadow map cannot resolve; defaults to false.

    contactShadowLengthpositiveDecimal20

    Distance in scene units that contact shadows search from each surface toward the light; defaults to 20. Used only when contactShadows is true.

    skyone of none gradientnone

    On a dome light, selects a procedural gradient sky instead of an environment image; none is the default. Only dome lights may set it, and a sky excludes an environment image.

    skyZenithcolorType#3A6EA5FF

    Colour of the procedural sky straight overhead; defaults to a mid blue.

    skyHorizoncolorType#C8D8E8FF

    Colour of the procedural sky at the horizon; defaults to a pale blue-grey.

    skyGroundcolorType#4A4038FF

    Colour of the procedural sky below the horizon; defaults to a dark brown.

    skyExponentpositiveDecimal0.5

    Exponent of the procedural gradient sky's blend from the horizon colour to the zenith, and to the ground below the horizon: the mix is the sine of the elevation raised to this power, so values below 1 pull the zenith colour toward the horizon. Default 0.5.

    sunAzimuthxs:double0

    Compass direction of the sun in the procedural sky in degrees; 0 faces +z and positive values turn toward +x; defaults to 0.

    sunElevationxs:double45

    Height of the sun above the horizon in the procedural sky in degrees; defaults to 45.

    sunSizenonNegativeDecimal0.53

    Angular diameter of the sun disc in degrees; 0 removes the sun; defaults to 0.53.

    sunColorcolorType#FFFFFFFF

    Colour of the sun disc in the procedural sky; defaults to white.

    sunIntensitynonNegativeDecimal0

    Brightness multiplier of the sun disc in the procedural sky; 0, the default, shows no sun.
