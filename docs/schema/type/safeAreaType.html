    safeAreaType

    Type of <safeArea> in safeAreasType

    Regions the platform UI covers. Presets carry the renderer's maintained insets for that placement; explicit top/right/bottom/left (fractions of the frame) override them. enforce=warn|error checks that text, captions and nodes tagged "cta" or "logo" stay inside the safe region.

    Starting insets for a platform or broadcast placement (title-safe, action-safe, a social-video preset); explicit top, right, bottom and left override them. custom (default) has none and needs all four.

    topunitDecimal

    Inset of the safe region from the top edge, as a fraction of the frame height; overrides the preset's value. Required when preset is custom or absent.

    rightunitDecimal

    Inset of the safe region from the right edge, as a fraction of the frame width; overrides the preset's value. Required when preset is custom or absent.

    bottomunitDecimal

    Inset of the safe region from the bottom edge, as a fraction of the frame height; overrides the preset's value. Required when preset is custom or absent.

    leftunitDecimal

    Inset of the safe region from the left edge, as a fraction of the frame width; overrides the preset's value. Required when preset is custom or absent.

    enforceone of off warn errorwarn

    What happens when text, burned captions or nodes tagged cta or logo cross the safe region: off ignores it, warn reports a finding, error fails the run. warn when absent.
