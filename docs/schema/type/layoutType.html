    layoutType

    Type of <layout> in layoutsType

    An alternative frame (e.g. 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9). Relative lengths and align* attributes reflow automatically; override children handle the rest. An output selects it with @layout.

    aspectaspectType

    Optional assertion of the frame ratio W:H: W and H are positive and W * height equals H * width (C82). Equivalent unreduced ratios are accepted; width and height determine the frame.

    safeAreaxs:IDREF

    Id of the safeArea that applies when this layout is selected, in place of the project's.

    reframeone of reflow crop fit fit-blurreflow

    How the composition fills the layout frame: reflow re-lays it out at that size; crop, fit and fit-blur render it at the project size, then crop it, fit it at the resolved focus, or fit it over a blurred copy. reflow when absent.

    focusXunitDecimal0.5

    Horizontal anchor (0 left to 1 right) of the crop, fit or fit-blur reframe; 0.5 when absent. A segment's own focusX replaces it. Provisional (SREP 93): Reframe anchor for crop, fit and fit-blur, with a segment value taking precedence over the layout value and a centred default of 0.5.

    focusYunitDecimal0.5

    Vertical anchor (0 top to 1 bottom) of the crop, fit or fit-blur reframe; 0.5 when absent. A segment's own focusY replaces it. Provisional (SREP 93): Reframe anchor for crop, fit and fit-blur, with a segment value taking precedence over the layout value and a centred default of 0.5.
