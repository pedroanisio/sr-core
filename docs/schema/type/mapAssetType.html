    mapAssetType

    Type of <map> in assetsType

    A projected map drawn as vectors: geoLayer, graticule, route and pin children paint in document order over @background (the globe or world outline filled), with @outline stroking that outline. zoom counts doublings from the fitted view: zoom 0 shows @fit (the whole sphere when absent) inside the frame less @fitPadding, centred on centerLon/centerLat (default: the middle of the fit target). centerLon, centerLat, zoom and rotation (degrees, clockwise on screen) are animatable; flyTo children make smooth camera moves. Tilt a map in perspective with its layer's 3D rotation.

    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    id requiredxs:ID

    Identifier by which layers and expressions refer to this map; its animated attributes are addressed by it.

    width requiredxs:positiveInteger

    Width of the map's drawing frame in pixels. A map that textures a globe is drawn over the whole world at twice its height instead.

    height requiredxs:positiveInteger

    Height of the map's drawing frame in pixels.

    projectionprojectionTypeequal-earth

    Map projection, default equal-earth; the cylindrical, pseudo-cylindrical, conic (albers, lambert-conformal) and azimuthal (including orthographic) families are supported. Not used when the map textures a globe.

    parallelsnumberListType

    Two standard parallels in degrees for the albers and lambert-conformal projections (defaults 29.5 and 45.5, and 30 and 30); ignored by other projections and when fewer than two values are given.

    centerLonlongitudeType

    Longitude in degrees the view is centred on; defaults to the middle of the fit target (0 for the whole sphere). May be animated. Not used when the map textures a globe.

    centerLatlatitudeType

    Latitude in degrees the view is centred on; defaults to the middle of the fit target (0 for the whole sphere). May be animated. Not used when the map textures a globe.

    zoomxs:double0

    Zoom counted in doublings of scale; 0 shows the fit target with other projections and a 512 px wide world with web-mercator, 1 magnifies twice. May be animated.

    rotationxs:double0

    Turn of the map on screen in degrees, clockwise; 0 when absent. May be animated. Not used when the map textures a globe.

    fitxs:IDREFS

    Ids of geo assets that the map's zoom 0 fits inside the frame; the whole sphere when absent. With web-mercator it only sets the default centre.

    fitPaddingnonNegativeDecimal0

    Margin in pixels kept free on each side of the frame when fitting the map; 0 when absent. Has no effect with the web-mercator projection. Not used when the map textures a globe.

    backgroundpaintType

    Paint that fills the map's outline (the globe, or the whole world on flat projections), below the vector layers and above raster basemaps; nothing is filled when absent.

    outlinepaintType

    Paint of the stroke drawn around the map's outline (the globe or the whole world) over the layers; nothing is stroked when absent.

    outlineWidthnonNegativeDecimal1

    Width in pixels of the outline stroke; 1 when absent.

    precisionpositiveDecimal0.7071

    Resampling precision in pixels for projected lines, greater than 0; smaller values follow curves more closely. Default 0.7071.
