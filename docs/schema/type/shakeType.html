    shakeType

    Type of <shake> in cameraType

    Content

    Empty: attributes only.

    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    amplitudenonNegativeDecimal10

    Peak camera displacement of the shake in scene units along the x and y axes of the camera's parent frame (not the view direction); default 10.

    frequencypositiveDecimal2

    Speed of the shake noise: it is sampled at frequency times the camera time in seconds, so higher values shake faster. Default 2.

    rotationnonNegativeDecimal0

    Peak roll of the shake in degrees; 0 (default) adds no roll.

    zoomnonNegativeDecimal0

    Strength of the zoom pulsing of the shake on a perspective camera: it zooms by a factor of 1 plus zoom times the noise (the field of view is divided by it); 0 (default) adds none.

    octavesxs:positiveInteger2

    Number of noise layers (detail levels) summed in the shake, each at half the weight of the one before; values below 1 count as 1 and above 16 as 16. Default 2.

    seedxs:unsignedLong

    Seed of the shake noise; absent, the project seed is used.

    startxs:double0

    Seconds after the camera becomes live (its own start) at which the shake begins; default 0.

    endxs:double

    Seconds after the camera becomes live (its own start) at which the shake stops (exclusive); absent, it continues.
