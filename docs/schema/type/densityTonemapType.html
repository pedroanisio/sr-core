    densityTonemapType

    Type of <tonemap> in computeType

    Content

    Empty: attributes only.

    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    scaleone of log linearlog

    How density maps to brightness: log (default) compresses large counts logarithmically, linear scales them in proportion to the reference density.

    gainpositiveDecimal1

    Multiplier on density inside the logarithm of the log scale (default 1); larger values brighten low densities. Not used by linear scale.

    gammapositiveDecimal1

    Exponent applied as 1/gamma to the normalised density before colouring (default 1); above 1 brightens mid-tones.

    referencepositiveDecimal or one of maxmax

    "max" (the largest density in the frame) or a positive number: the density that maps to 1.

    rampxs:string#000000FF #FFFFFFFF

    Space-separated #RRGGBB[AA] colours placed at equal steps from low to high density and interpolated between (default black to white); used only with one channel.

    alphaone of opaque densityopaque

    Whether the picture is opaque (default) or its alpha is scaled by the normalised density, so empty cells are transparent.
