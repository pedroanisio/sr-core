    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    shape requiredone of rect ellipse rounded-rect polygon star line path stroke-text parametric

    Kind of outline drawn: rect, rounded-rect, ellipse, polygon, star, line (across the middle of the box), path (from @path), stroke-text (from @text) or parametric (from a parametricPath).

    widthpositiveLengthType

    Width of the shape's box: pixels, or a percentage of the parent box or vw/vh/vmin/vmax. Required unless the shape takes its box from a region or is parametric.

    heightpositiveLengthType

    Height of the shape's box: pixels, or a percentage of the parent box or vw/vh/vmin/vmax. Required unless the shape takes its box from a region or is parametric.

    pathxs:string

    Outline as SVG path data (all absolute and relative commands, arcs included), in the shape's local pixel space; used by shape="path".

    textxs:string

    The string drawn as pen strokes by shape="stroke-text"; a line feed starts a new line. Required with stroke-text; ignored on other shape kinds.

    strokeFontxs:IDREF

    Id of the strokeFont asset whose single-line glyphs lay out the text of a stroke-text shape. Required with stroke-text; ignored on other shape kinds.

    fontSizepositiveDecimal48

    Cap height, in local units, of the glyphs of a stroke-text shape; default 48. Ignored on other shape kinds.

    fillRulefillRuleTypenonzero

    Rule that decides which areas of a self-overlapping outline are inside the fill: nonzero (default) or evenodd.

    radiusnonNegativeDecimal0

    Corner radius in local units for rect and rounded-rect shapes; default 0 gives square corners. Radii that do not fit are scaled down. cornerRadii overrides it; other shape kinds ignore it.

    cornerRadiinumberListType

    Per-corner radii TL TR BR BL; overrides radius.

    pointsxs:positiveInteger5

    Number of vertices of a polygon or tips of a star; default 5. Values below 3 (polygon) or 2 (star) are raised to that minimum. Other kinds ignore it.

    innerRadiusnonNegativeDecimal

    Radius, in local units around the box centre, of a star's inner vertices; absent means half the outer radius. Only stars read it.

    outerRadiusnonNegativeDecimal

    Radius, in local units around the box centre, of a polygon's vertices or a star's tips; absent means the ellipse inscribed in the box.

    innerRoundnessunitDecimal0

    Rounding of a star's inner vertices from 0 (sharp, default) up to 1; larger values give longer curved tangent handles. Only stars read it.

    outerRoundnessunitDecimal0

    Rounding of a polygon's vertices or a star's tips from 0 (sharp, default) up to 1; larger values give longer curved tangent handles.

    fillpaintType#FFFFFFFF

    Paint (colour or url(#id) of a gradient, pattern or noise) that fills the outline; default opaque white. Not used by stroke-text.

    strokepaintType#00000000

    Paint (colour or url(#id) of a gradient, pattern or noise) for the outline; default fully transparent. The outline is drawn only when strokeWidth is above 0.

    strokeWidthnonNegativeDecimal0

    Width of the outline stroke in local units; default 0, which draws no stroke. Nothing is drawn either while the stroke paint is transparent (its default).

    blendblendTypenormal

    Compositing mode that combines the shape with the content beneath it in its parent; normal (default) draws it over, the others combine by the named rule (multiply, screen, add, stencil-alpha, behind, ...).

    effectsxs:IDREFS

    Space-separated ids of effects applied to the shape's picture, in the order listed; disabled effects are skipped.

    regionxs:IDREF

    Id of a region of a pdf asset: the shape takes its box from it (grown by regionPadding) and follows the layer named by regionLayer. Its width and height are unused; it must have no parent and no transformConstraint.

    regionLayerxs:IDREF

    Id of the layer that shows the pdf page; a shape with a region is placed through that layer and is not drawn while the layer is out of the frame. Required with region.

    regionPaddingxs:double0

    Margin added on every side of the region's box, in the page image's pixels; default 0.

    from attribute group nodeAttributes
    id requiredxs:ID
    namexs:string

    Provisional (SREP 93): Human label for render-report findings and debug output, with no effect on geometry or colour.

    tagsxs:NMTOKENS

    Space-separated free-form labels on the node. With safeArea enforcement on, a node tagged cta or logo is checked to stay inside the safe region unless safeAreaForce exempts it.

    safeAreaForcexs:booleanfalse

    true exempts this node and everything it contains (for a symbol: every instance of it, and what it draws) from the safeArea enforce check (SA01); validate lists every node that carries it (SA02), so a deliberate placement outside the safe region is never silent.

    zxs:int0

    Stacking order among siblings: nodes with a higher integer draw above lower ones; 0 when absent. Not used for stacking by 3D objects, 3D particle nodes, ocean nodes and cameras.

    visiblexs:booleantrue

    When false, the node and everything it contains are not drawn; true when absent.

    opacityunitDecimal1

    Node opacity from 0 (transparent) to 1 (opaque), multiplied with the opacity of its ancestors; 1 when absent. Provisional (SREP 93): Own evaluated opacity and copy opacity factors are restricted to 0 through 1, with NaN treated as zero.

    startxs:double0

    Time in seconds, on the parent's timeline, at which the node first appears; 0 when absent. Added to the marker time when startMarker is set. A transition on the node can make it appear earlier.

    endxs:double

    Time in seconds, on the parent's timeline, at which the node disappears (exclusive); when absent it stays until its parent ends. Added to the marker time when endMarker is set. A transition can keep it longer.

    shutterAnglexs:double in [0, 720]

    The shutter angle, in degrees, of this node's motion blur, instead of the project's: the nearest node up its parents that sets it, else project/@shutterAngle. 0 draws the node sharp; 360 blurs over the whole frame interval. Only where motion blur is on for the node.

    startMarkerxs:NCName

    Name of a scene marker, or a beat.N or bar.N id of the beat grid, whose time is added to start to give the node's appearance time.

    endMarkerxs:NCName

    Name of a scene marker, or a beat.N or bar.N id of the beat grid, whose time plus end (0 if absent) gives the node's disappearance time.

    conditionexpressionString

    Node is rendered only when the expression is truthy (e.g. "param('showPrice')").

    motionBlurtriStateTypeinherit

    Turns motion blur on or off for this node and the nodes it contains; inherit (the default) takes the nearest ancestor's setting, then the project's.

    mattexs:IDREF

    Track matte: any node whose alpha or luma gates this node. The matte node is not drawn itself unless matteVisible="true".

    matteModematteModeTypealpha

    How the matte node gates this node: alpha or luma takes the matte's alpha or brightness as coverage, and the -inverted modes use one minus it; alpha when absent.

    matteVisiblexs:booleanfalse

    On a node that names a matte: when true the matte node is also drawn; by default (false) the matte is hidden and only gates this node.

    edgeBlendone of inherit linear encodedinherit

    The space in which this node's own pixels (their colour C and coverage x opacity a) composite onto what lies beneath (B): linear, out = a C + (1 - a) B in the linear working space; encoded, the same on display-encoded values (the sRGB transfer function), decoded back; inherit, linear when project/@linearLight is true and encoded when it is false. A group's value applies when its offscreen composites; children do not inherit it. SREP 72.

    parentxs:IDREF

    Transform parent outside the XML tree (After Effects parenting); shorthand for a transformConstraint type="parent".

    threeDxs:booleanfalse

    2.5D: place this 2D node in camera space at depth zDepth with rotationX/rotationY.

    zDepthxs:double0

    Depth of the node in the frame's 2.5D camera space, in frame pixels, used only when threeD is true; 0 when absent. Provisional (SREP 93): Used only with threeD enabled; explicitly setting it on a 2D node produces an informational inert-attribute finding.

    rotationXxs:double0

    Rotation about the horizontal axis through the node's pivot, in degrees, used only when threeD is true; positive values turn the top edge away. Provisional (SREP 93): Used only with threeD enabled; explicitly setting it on a 2D node produces an informational inert-attribute finding.

    rotationYxs:double0

    Rotation about the vertical axis through the node's pivot, in degrees, used only when threeD is true; positive values turn the right edge away. Provisional (SREP 93): Used only with threeD enabled; explicitly setting it on a 2D node produces an informational inert-attribute finding.

    alignXone of left center right stretch

    Responsive anchoring: overrides x so the node's box aligns within alignTo.

    alignYone of top middle bottom stretch

    Responsive vertical placement of the node's box within the alignTo rectangle: top, middle or bottom, or stretch to fill it minus margin; unset leaves y alone, as does a node with no box size.

    alignToone of parent frame safe-areaparent

    Rectangle that alignX and alignY align within: the parent's box, the output frame, or the frame's safe area; parent when absent. Provisional (SREP 93): Used only when alignX or alignY is set; without alignment an explicit value produces an informational inert-attribute finding.

    marginlengthType0

    Inset in pixels or a relative length (a percentage is of the parent's width on both axes) kept from the alignTo edge for left, right, top, bottom and stretch alignment; 0 when absent, unused without alignX or alignY. Provisional (SREP 93): Used only when alignX or alignY is set; without alignment an explicit value produces an informational inert-attribute finding.

    xlengthType0

    Horizontal position of the node's anchor point in its parent's box, in pixels or a relative length (% of parent width, vw, vh, vmin, vmax); 0 when absent. alignX takes over on a node that has a box size.

    ylengthType0

    Vertical position of the node's anchor point in its parent's box, in pixels or a relative length (% of parent height, vw, vh, vmin, vmax), growing downward; 0 when absent. alignY takes over on a node that has a box size.

    rotationxs:double0

    Rotation about the anchor point, in degrees, clockwise on screen; 0 when absent.

    scaleXxs:double1

    Horizontal scale factor about the anchor point, applied before skew and rotation; 1 leaves the size unchanged and is used when absent.

    scaleYxs:double1

    Vertical scale factor about the anchor point, applied before skew and rotation; 1 leaves the size unchanged and is used when absent.

    anchorXlengthType0

    Horizontal pivot of the node's rotation, skew and scale, in the node's local layout coordinates (pixels or relative length, % of the parent's width); 0 when absent.

    anchorYlengthType0

    Vertical pivot of the node's rotation, skew and scale, in the node's local layout coordinates (pixels or relative length, % of the parent's height); 0 when absent.

    skewXxs:double0

    Horizontal shear angle in degrees (the tangent of the angle shifts x in proportion to y); 0 when absent.

    skewYxs:double0

    Vertical shear angle in degrees (the tangent of the angle shifts y in proportion to x); 0 when absent.

    from attribute group strokeStyle
    strokeCapstrokeCapTypebutt

    Shape of the ends of open stroke segments and dashes: butt (flat, default), round, or square (extends half the stroke width past the end).

    strokeJoinstrokeJoinTypemiter

    Shape of stroke corners: miter (sharp, default), round, or bevel (cut flat).

    miterLimitpositiveDecimal4

    Longest allowed miter, as a ratio of miter length to stroke width; sharper corners are bevelled. Defaults to 4.

    dashnumberListType

    Dash pattern as alternating dash and gap lengths in scene units; an odd count is repeated to make pairs. Absent or all-zero draws a solid stroke.

    dashOffsetxs:double0

    Distance in scene units into the dash pattern at which the outline starts, shifting the dashes along the path. Default 0.

    strokePositionstrokePositionTypecenter

    For a shape: where the stroke sits relative to the outline: center (default), inside (only the part within the shape) or outside (only the part beyond it). Provisional (SREP 93): Stroke placement inside, outside or centred on the path, with the same behavior for vector assets and shapes.

    paintOrderone of fill-stroke stroke-fillfill-stroke

    For a shape: drawing order of fill and stroke: fill-stroke (default) paints the stroke over the fill, stroke-fill paints the fill over the stroke. Provisional (SREP 93): Order of fill and stroke painting, fill-stroke or stroke-fill, for vector assets and shapes alike.

    from attribute group trimPath
    trimStartunitDecimal0

    Start of the visible part of the outline as a fraction 0-1 of its length; default 0. If start exceeds end, the two are swapped. A shape's fill is cut to the trimmed outline too.

    trimEndunitDecimal1

    End of the visible part of the outline as a fraction 0-1 of its length; default 1 (the full length). If end is below start, the two are swapped. A shape's fill is cut to the trimmed outline too.

    trimOffsetxs:double0

    Shifts the visible range along the outline in degrees, where 360 is one full length; the range wraps past the end. Default 0.

    trimModeone of simultaneous sequentialsimultaneous

    How trimming treats several subpaths: simultaneous (default) trims each by the same fractions, sequential trims across them as one continuous path.

    from attribute group strokeMarkers
    markerStartstrokeMarkerTypenone

    Marker drawn at the start of an open outline, in the stroke's paint and pointing backwards along it: none (default), arrow, open-arrow, circle, square, diamond or bar. On a shape it needs shape path or line.

    markerEndstrokeMarkerTypenone

    Marker drawn at the end of an open outline, in the stroke's paint and pointing along it: none (default), arrow, open-arrow, circle, square, diamond or bar. On a shape it needs shape path or line.

    markerSizepositiveDecimal4

    Marker size as a multiple of the stroke width; default 4.
