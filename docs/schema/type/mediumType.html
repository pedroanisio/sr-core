    densityScalenonNegativeDecimal1

    Multiplier applied to every density sample of the volume; 1 when absent.

    extinctionnonNegativeDecimal1

    Extinction coefficient per scene unit at unit density: how strongly the medium attenuates light passing through; 1 when absent.

    albedocolorType#FFFFFFFF

    Colour of the light the medium scatters rather than absorbs (white scatters all, black absorbs all); white when absent. No light is scattered when scatterBounces is 0. Provisional (SREP 93): Scattering albedo defaults to white in both the scene renderer and volume library.

    anisotropyxs:double in [-0.99, 0.99]0

    Direction of scattering from -0.99 (backward) through 0 (even) to 0.99 (forward); 0 when absent.

    emissionColorcolorType#000000FF

    Colour of the light the medium emits itself, scaled by emissionScale, so nothing is emitted while emissionScale is 0; black when absent.

    emissionScalenonNegativeDecimal0

    Brightness of the emission per scene unit at unit density; with blackbody it scales the thermal colour. 0 when absent.

    blackbodyxs:booleanfalse

    When true, the temperature channel of the volume makes it glow with blackbody colour; requires temperatureGrid on the volume asset unless the object holds a smoke simulation. false when absent.

    temperatureScalepositiveDecimal1

    Multiplier applied to the temperature samples (in kelvin) before the blackbody colour is worked out; the scaled values must stay within 0 to 50000. 1 when absent.

    stepSizepositiveDecimal1

    Largest ray-marching step through the medium, in scene units; smaller steps are more accurate and slower. 1 when absent.

    maxStepsxs:positiveInteger ≤ 655362048

    Step budget for one ray through the medium (1 to 65536); a medium that needs more steps at its stepSize is an error. 2048 when absent.

    lightingone of exact gridexact

    Version 1.3. How the medium's in-scattering is lit by the path tracer: exact marches a shadow ray from every sample; grid reads transmittance and surface visibility from per-light grids built once per frame (see the cinematic impact SREP). The lightGrid* attributes apply only with grid (VOL10).

    lightGridCellxs:positiveInteger ≤ 641

    With lighting grid, the number of voxels between nodes of the light grids (1 to 64); 1 when absent. Grid-lit media in one frame share one lattice with the finest spacing any of them asks.

    lightGridDomeDirectionsxs:positiveInteger in [8, 512]64

    With lighting grid, the number of fixed dome directions used for sky light (8 to 512); 64 when absent. Grid-lit media in one frame use the largest value any of them asks.

    lightGridMemoryMiBxs:positiveInteger ≤ 4096128

    With lighting grid, the most memory in MiB the light grids may take (1 to 4096); 128 when absent. Grids needing more are an error, and grid-lit media in one frame share the smallest limit asked.

    scatterBouncesxs:nonNegativeInteger ≤ 321

    Version 1.3. The most collisions a path may have in the medium before the light reaches the camera, counting the one that is lit directly: 1 is single scattering (what the medium does without the attribute), 0 is a medium that does not scatter (absorption and emission only), and more add the light that has been scattered more than once, estimated by the path tracer by a random walk.
