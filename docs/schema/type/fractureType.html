    fractureType

    Type of <fracture> in object3DType

    Owned seeded closed-solid fracture. at is composition time, quantized to the first physics boundary at or after that time when the source object participates. Exactly one rigidBody supplies total mass, collision properties and inherited motion. Exterior materials and attributes come from the frozen source surface; cut faces use interiorMaterial, with planar UV repeats per scene unit given by interiorUvScale. impulseX/Y/Z are the total directed world-scene impulse in kg*scene-unit/second, distributed in proportion to piece mass. radialImpulse is a nonnegative total scalar impulse, distributed by piece mass along the source-centroid-to-piece directions. maxMemoryMiB bounds component geometry/reconstruction admission, not whole-process RSS. Fracture is cinematic partitioning, not a material stress/failure calculation.
