    fractureType

    Type of <fracture> in object3DType

    Owned seeded closed-solid fracture. at is composition time, quantized to the first physics boundary at or after that time when the source object participates. Exactly one rigidBody supplies total mass, collision properties and inherited motion. Exterior materials and attributes come from the frozen source surface; cut faces use interiorMaterial, with planar UV repeats per scene unit given by interiorUvScale. impulseX/Y/Z are the total directed world-scene impulse in kg*scene-unit/second, distributed in proportion to piece mass. radialImpulse is a nonnegative total scalar impulse, distributed by piece mass along the source-centroid-to-piece directions. maxMemoryMiB bounds component geometry/reconstruction admission, not whole-process RSS. Fracture is cinematic partitioning, not a material stress/failure calculation. source names the dynamic rigid body that breaks it (FRX5): the fracture fires on the step after the first impact of that body on this one is noticed, above minImpulse, the total normal impulse of the pair in one step in kg*scene-unit/second (absent: twice the weight of the source in one step, as for a crater from an impact). Its time and its push are then derived, so at, radialImpulse and impulseX/Y/Z are not given (FRX6), and minImpulse and energyFraction belong to a fracture with source (FRX7). The pieces keep the velocity the body had after the contact, and a part of the kinetic energy of the impact relative to the pair, 1/2 mu v_n^2 (mu the reduced mass of the two bodies, v_n the closing speed), energyFraction of it (0 to 1; the engine's default is 0.3, with no published value, and not an XSD default so that it can be refused without source), becomes the pieces' push on each other, with no momentum added. The partition is the seeded one of the document and does not depend on where the impact was.

    Empty: attributes only.

    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    sourcexs:IDREF

    Id of the dynamic rigid body whose impact breaks this object: the fracture fires on the step after its first impact above minImpulse. With a source, at, radialImpulse and the impulse components are not given.

    minImpulsepositiveDecimal

    Total normal impulse of the two bodies in one step, in kg*scene-unit/second, that an impact must exceed to break the object; absent, twice the source body's weight over one step. Only with source.

    energyFractionunitDecimal

    Fraction from 0 to 1 of the impact's relative kinetic energy that becomes the pieces' push on each other; 0.3 when absent. Only with source.

    atnonNegativeDecimal0

    Composition time in seconds at which the object breaks, moved to the first physics step boundary at or after it when the object takes part in physics. Default 0; not given with source or mode stress.

    piecesxs:positiveInteger ≤ 40968

    Number of pieces, 1 to 4096, of the seeded random partition. Not given with a partition by planes or labels. Default 8.

    seedxs:unsignedLong0

    Seed of the random partition; the same seed gives the same pieces. Not given with a partition by planes or labels. Default 0.

    interiorMaterialxs:IDREF

    Id of the material drawn on the faces that the cut exposes; the outside keeps the object's own surface. Required, except on an object of cells, where it is not allowed.

    interiorUvScalepositiveDecimal1

    Number of planar texture repeats per scene unit on the cut faces. Default 1; not allowed on an object of cells.

    partitionone of voronoi planes labels

    On an object of cells (FRX9): how the body is cut into pieces, voronoi (the engine's value: pieces seeds drawn from seed in the box of the cells, each cell to the nearest), planes (the cells are the pieces' sides of the planes in planes) or labels (the cells that have one palette index are one part). A part that is not connected by faces is split into its components, so every piece is connected. pieces and seed belong to voronoi (FRX10). The pieces have the material of their cells, and a face that a cut exposes is drawn with the palette like any other: there is no interior material (FRX8).

    planesxs:string

    With partition="planes" (FRX11): up to 63 planes, each four numbers nx ny nz offset, in object units (the object's own axes, before its scale, the origin at the corner of the cells): a cell is on the positive side of a plane if the dot product of the normal with the centre of the cell, (key + 1/2) * cellSize, is at least the offset. The normal is not unit and need not be, but it is not all zeros (a plane with no direction cuts nothing), and all the numbers are finite; it is taken to 2^-32 of its largest component.

    labelsone of material

    With partition="labels" (FRX12): material, the palette index of a cell is its label, so that a model breaks along its materials.

    modeone of impact stress

    What breaks the body: impact (the engine's value, and the one of every fracture before this attribute: at a time, or by the impact of source) or stress (FRX13 to FRX15): the body of cells is one rigid body until the load on it makes more stress in a joint between two of its pieces than strength, and the joints that are over it break together, at the start of the next step. The loads are all of them (the weight, the fields, the contacts, and the one joint of the world that may hold the body), so there is no source, no time and no push (FRX15); the body is a dynamic body of cells (FRX14). Not an XSD default, so that strength can be refused without it. The model is a rigid one: no stress wave, no energy kept in the joints, a joint that is in a cycle takes the share of its plane's cut by its area and its distance from the centre of the cut, and the stress of a body at rest on its contacts is the one of the way the solver spread the pressure over them (a block on a floor is held at the corners of its foot, and reads the bending of a beam on two supports).

    strengthpositiveDecimal

    With mode="stress" (FRX13), required: the principal tension, in pascals, at which a joint between two pieces breaks, from the normal and the bending stress of the load on the section of the joint added and the shear and the twist added (Rankine: a brittle joint). A push does not break a joint by itself.

    impulseXxs:double0

    Directed impulse along the scene X axis, in kg*scene-unit/second, given to the pieces in proportion to their mass. Not given with source or mode stress. Default 0.

    impulseYxs:double0

    Directed impulse along the scene Y axis, in kg*scene-unit/second, given to the pieces in proportion to their mass. Not given with source or mode stress. Default 0.

    impulseZxs:double0

    Directed impulse along the scene Z axis, in kg*scene-unit/second, given to the pieces in proportion to their mass. Not given with source or mode stress. Default 0.

    radialImpulsenonNegativeDecimal0

    Outward push, not negative: each piece moves away from the centre of mass of the pieces at this impulse over the object's mass, less their mean velocity, so net momentum is kept. Not given with source or mode stress.

    maxMemoryMiBxs:positiveInteger ≤ 4096256

    Memory budget in MiB for the geometry the cut of a mesh object builds, 1 to 4096; an object of cells does not use it. Default 256.

    FRX1fracture requires version="1.3" or later.test: not(/scene/@version='1.0' or /scene/@version='1.1' or /scene/@version='1.2')
    FRX2fracture requires one closed surface object3D owner and exactly one rigidBody.test: parent::object3D[not(@primitive='volume' or @primitive='plane' or @primitive='map')] and count(../fracture)=1 and count(../rigidBody)=1
    FRX3fracture interiorMaterial must reference a declared material.test: parent::object3D[@primitive='voxels'] or @interiorMaterial=/scene/materials/material/@id
    FRX4fracture numeric values must be finite.test: not(@*[name()!='interiorMaterial' and name()!='source' and name()!='partition' and name()!='planes' and name()!='labels' and name()!='mode' and not((number(substring(normalize-space(.),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(.),'+')))) - number(substring(normalize-space(.),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(.),'+')))))=0)])
    FRX5fracture source must name another object3D whose rigidBody is dynamic.test: not(@source) or (/scene//object3D[@id=current()/@source and not(@id=current()/../@id)]/rigidBody[not(@type) or @type='dynamic'])
    FRX6a fracture that comes from a source derives its time and its push, so at, radialImpulse and impulseX, impulseY and impulseZ may not be given.test: not(@source and (@at or @radialImpulse or @impulseX or @impulseY or @impulseZ))
    FRX7minImpulse and energyFraction belong to a fracture with a source.test: @source or not(@minImpulse or @energyFraction)
    FRX8a fracture of an object of cells has no interior material: its pieces have the material of their cells.test: not(parent::object3D[@primitive='voxels']) or not(@interiorMaterial or @interiorUvScale)
    FRX9partition, planes and labels belong to the fracture of an object of primitive voxels.test: parent::object3D[@primitive='voxels'] or not(@partition or @planes or @labels)
    FRX10pieces and seed belong to a voronoi partition.test: not(@partition) or @partition='voronoi' or not(@pieces or @seed)
    FRX11partition planes takes planes, from one to 63 planes of four finite numbers (nx ny nz offset, the normal not all zeros), and planes belongs to that partition.test: (@partition='planes' or not(@planes)) and (not(@partition='planes') or (@planes and count(str:tokenize(normalize-space(@planes),' '))>=4 and count(str:tokenize(normalize-space(@planes),' '))<=252 and count(str:tokenize(normalize-space(@planes),' ')) mod 4 = 0 and count(str:tokenize(normalize-space(@planes),' ')[number(substring(normalize-space(.),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(.),'+')))) - number(substring(normalize-space(.),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(.),'+')))) = 0]) = count(str:tokenize(normalize-space(@planes),' ')) and not(str:tokenize(normalize-space(@planes),' ')[position() mod 4 = 1 and number(substring(normalize-space(.),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(.),'+'))))=0 and number(substring(normalize-space(following-sibling::*[1]),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(following-sibling::*[1]),'+'))))=0 and number(substring(normalize-space(following-sibling::*[2]),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(following-sibling::*[2]),'+'))))=0])))
    FRX13fracture mode="stress" requires strength (pascals), and strength belongs to mode="stress".test: (@mode='stress') = boolean(@strength)
    FRX14fracture mode="stress" belongs to a dynamic body of cells (an object of primitive voxels with a dynamic rigidBody).test: not(@mode='stress') or (parent::object3D[@primitive='voxels'] and ../rigidBody[not(@type) or @type='dynamic'])
    FRX15a fracture by stress is fired by the load on the body and by nothing else, so source, at, minImpulse, energyFraction, radialImpulse and impulseX, impulseY and impulseZ may not be given.test: not(@mode='stress') or not(@source or @at or @minImpulse or @energyFraction or @radialImpulse or @impulseX or @impulseY or @impulseZ)
    FRX12partition labels takes labels="material", and labels belongs to that partition.test: (@partition='labels' or not(@labels)) and (not(@partition='labels') or @labels='material')
