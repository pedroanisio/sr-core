    Type of <word> in cueType

    Content

    Empty: attributes only.

    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    start requiredxs:double

    Time in seconds at which the word begins to be spoken; the caption presets use it to time the per-word effect.

    end requiredxs:double

    Time in seconds at which the word stops being spoken.

    text requiredxs:string

    The word's text; a cue without its own text is built by joining its words with spaces.

    emphasisxs:booleanfalse

    Whether the word is emphasised: under the karaoke, highlight, pop and enlarge presets it keeps the active colour for the whole page, and under the other presets it is set in bold (weight 700 or more). Default false.
