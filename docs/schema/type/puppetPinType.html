    declared here
    namexs:NMTOKEN

    Optional label for the pin; it has no effect on the deformation. Provisional (SREP 93): Human label used in diagnostics and inspection or checkpoint output, falling back to the identifier or index.

    kindone of position bend starchposition

    How the pin acts: position moves its point to the rest position plus x/y; bend also turns the surrounding area by rotation; starch moves nothing and stiffens the area around it. Default position.

    restX requiredxs:double

    Horizontal rest position of the pin, in pixels in the node's local space (origin at the top-left of its box).

    restY requiredxs:double

    Vertical rest position of the pin, in pixels in the node's local space (origin at the top-left of its box).

    xxs:double0

    Horizontal displacement of the pin from its rest position, in pixels; animate it to move the pin. Default 0. Not used by starch pins.

    yxs:double0

    Vertical displacement of the pin from its rest position, in pixels; animate it to move the pin. Default 0. Not used by starch pins.

    rotationxs:double0

    Bend pins only: the angle in degrees the area around the pin is held at. Default 0; ignored by position and starch pins.

    amountnonNegativeDecimal1

    Starch pins only: how far the stiffening reaches around the pin, as a multiple (at least 1, default 1) of one eighth of the box's longer side.
