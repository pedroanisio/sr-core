    flockType

    Type of <flock> in groupType, sequenceType, repeatType, compositionType, symbolType

    Boids (Reynolds 1987): count agents in the width x height box steer by separation, alignment and cohesion with neighbours within perception, capped at maxSpeed and maxForce, kept in the box by bounds, and pushed by the named force fields. Drawn like particles (disc, streak or sprite), oriented to their velocity.
