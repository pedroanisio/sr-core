    idxs:ID

    Name by which keys and outputs refer to the marker's time (startMarker, endMarker, marker); unnamed markers cannot be referenced.

    time requiredxs:double

    Position of the marker on the composition timeline, in seconds.

    durationnonNegativeDecimal0

    Provisional (SREP 93): Chapter duration in seconds; a positive duration ends at the earliest of start plus duration, the next chapter or output end, while zero keeps automatic chapter ends.

    kindone of cue chapter section beat comment todo ctacue

    Marker type; markers of kind chapter become chapters in an output with segments when its container holds them (mp4, mov, mkv, webm or m4a). The other kinds are annotations only.

    labelxs:string

    Display text; for a chapter marker it is the chapter title, falling back to the marker id.
