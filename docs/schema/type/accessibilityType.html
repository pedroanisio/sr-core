    accessibilityType

    Type of <accessibility> in metadataType

    flashCheck runs a photosensitive-seizure analysis (general and red flashes above 3 per second over the ITU-R BT.1702 / WCAG 2.3.1 area threshold). contrastCheck verifies burned-in text and captions against their background at @minContrast.

    Content

    Empty: attributes only.

    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    descriptionxs:string

    Synopsis of the work, written to the output file's metadata as its synopsis tag when embedMetadata is on.

    audioDescriptionxs:IDREF

    Id of the audioTrack that carries the audio-described version. An mp4, mov, mkv or webm output that takes audio muxes that track as a separate described stream with its own language; other containers warn and keep the narration in the main mix. A reference to anything but an audioTrack is error AD01.

    requireCaptionsxs:booleanfalse

    When true, delivering an output that has no caption track at all fails; false when absent.

    flashCheckone of off warn errorwarn

    Photosensitive-seizure flash analysis of rendered frames: off, warn (report) or error (fail). The check runs only when an accessibility element exists; the schema default is warn.

    contrastCheckone of off warn erroroff

    Checks burned-in text and captions against their background at minContrast: off (default), warn or error.

    minContrastpositiveDecimal4.5

    Lowest contrast ratio (such as 4.5 for 4.5:1) text may have against its background before contrastCheck reports it; 4.5 when absent.

    legibilityCheckone of off warn erroroff

    Checks caption and text display time, plus reading speed and drawn size when readingSpeed and minTextSize are set: off (default), warn or error.

    readingSpeedpositiveDecimal

    Fastest acceptable reading speed in characters per second for captions and text; a caption track's own readingSpeed overrides it and applies even when this is absent. No speed check without either.

    minDisplayTimenonNegativeDecimal0.8333

    Shortest time in seconds a caption or text should stay visible, checked when legibilityCheck is warn or error; 0.8333 when absent.

    minTextSizelengthType

    Smallest acceptable drawn size of text layers (captions are not measured), as a length resolved against the output frame; a percentage is of its height. No size check when absent.
