    burst3DType

    Type of <burst> in particles3DType

    A burst of a 3D emitter at an instant (time, count, repeat, interval), or what the impact of a crater throws out of it: crater names a crater that grows from an impact (crater@source), count is then the number of particles that stand for the ejecta, and when, where, how fast and how heavy each one is are derived (the instant, repeat and interval are not given). Each particle is born at the instant it is launched, at its launch distance from the impact point, with its own velocity and mass; the mass is kept in the particle (the render size still comes from size) and the particles together hold 80% of the crater's mass. Launch speed and mass follow the ejecta scaling of Housen and Holsapple (2011) as the engine reads it, which has not been checked against the paper; the launch angle, its spread, the launch time and the lopsidedness of an oblique impact are the engine's own. angle is the launch angle above the tangent plane of the contact and angleSpread the half-width of the uniform spread around it, in degrees; both stay within 0 to 90 together. The ejecta of regolith and ice have no table row and are an error. Positions and velocities are in scene units through physics/@pixelsPerMeter, and gravity, drag and colliders are those of the emitter. The crater's pose is that of its owner at the impact. More particles than maxParticles is an error, not a truncation.

    context particles3D/burst cinematic-particles
    P3D7a burst requires time unless it comes from a crater, and a burst from a crater derives its instants, so time, repeat and interval may not be given.test: (@crater and not(@time or @repeat or @interval)) or (not(@crater) and @time)
    P3D8a burst from a crater must name a crater that grows from an impact.test: not(@crater) or /scene//crater[@id=current()/@crater and @source]
    P3D9angle and angleSpread belong to a burst from a crater.test: @crater or not(@angle or @angleSpread)
    P3D10the launch angle of a burst from a crater and its spread must stay between 0 and 90 degrees.test: not(@crater) or ($angle - $spread >= 0 and $angle + $spread <= 90)
