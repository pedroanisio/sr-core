    heightfieldType

    Type of <heightfield> in object3DType

    The mesh of an object3D with primitive="heightfield": the parametricSurface with u = z over [-depth/2, depth/2] (zSamples), v = x over [-width/2, width/2] (xSamples) and vertices (x, -height(x, z), z), so that a flat heightfield's top face (normal -y, up) is its front face. height is an expression with the variables x and z. SREP 70.

    Content

    Empty: attributes only.

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    height requiredexpressionString

    Expression for the surface height at (x, z) in the object's local space; a positive height raises the surface (the vertex y is minus the height).

    width requiredpositiveDecimal

    Extent of the heightfield along x, centred on the origin, in local units.

    depth requiredpositiveDecimal

    Extent of the heightfield along z, centred on the origin, in local units.

    xSamplessurfaceSamplesType64

    Number of grid vertices along x, from 2 to 4096; default 64. Values outside the range are invalid.

    zSamplessurfaceSamplesType64

    Number of grid vertices along z, from 2 to 4096; default 64. Values outside the range are invalid.
