    Attributes

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    startxs:double0

    Time in seconds on the smoke volume's clock at which the source starts emitting. Default 0. Not given with crater.

    endxs:double

    Time in seconds on the smoke volume's clock at which the source stops; absent, it emits for the rest of the simulation. Not given with crater.

    densityRatenonNegativeDecimal0

    Smoke density added per second inside the source's shape while it is active. Default 0. Not given with crater.

    temperatureRatenonNegativeDecimal0

    Temperature added per second, in kelvin, inside the source's shape while it is active. Default 0. Not given with crater.

    velocityRateXxs:double0

    Velocity added per second along the volume's X axis inside the source's shape while it is active, in scene units per second squared.

    velocityRateYxs:double0

    Velocity added per second along the volume's Y axis inside the source's shape while it is active, in scene units per second squared.

    velocityRateZxs:double0

    Velocity added per second along the volume's Z axis inside the source's shape while it is active, in scene units per second squared.

    expansionxs:double0

    Target local divergence of the air inside the shape while the source is active, in 1 per second; positive makes it swell. Default 0. Not given with crater.

    shapeone of sphere box meshsphere

    Region a smoke source or impulse covers: a sphere (default), a box (width, height and depth required), or a mesh asset named by mesh. Not allowed with crater.

    radiuspositiveDecimal1

    Radius of a sphere shape, in the smoke volume's own units, before scaleX, scaleY and scaleZ are applied; 1 when absent.

    widthpositiveDecimal

    Extent of a box shape along its own x axis, before rotation and scaling, centred on the shape's position; 0 when absent.

    heightpositiveDecimal

    Extent of a box shape along its own y axis, before rotation and scaling, centred on the shape's position; 0 when absent.

    depthpositiveDecimal

    Extent of a box shape along its own z axis, before rotation and scaling, centred on the shape's position; 0 when absent.

    xxs:double0

    Position of the shape's origin (the centre of a sphere or box) along the volume's x axis, in the volume's own units; 0 when absent.

    yxs:double0

    Position of the shape's origin (the centre of a sphere or box) along the volume's y axis, in the volume's own units; 0 when absent.

    zxs:double0

    Position of the shape's origin (the centre of a sphere or box) along the volume's z axis, in the volume's own units; 0 when absent.

    rotationxs:double0

    Rotation of the shape about the volume's z axis, in degrees; 0 when absent. Applied after the x and y rotations, about the shape's own origin. Not allowed together with crater.

    rotationXxs:double0

    Rotation of the shape about the volume's x axis, in degrees; 0 when absent. Applied first, before the y and z rotations. Not allowed together with crater.

    rotationYxs:double0

    Rotation of the shape about the volume's y axis, in degrees; 0 when absent. Applied after the x rotation and before the z rotation. Not allowed together with crater.

    scaleXxs:double1

    Scale of the shape along its own x axis, applied before rotation; 1 when absent. Must not be 0.

    scaleYxs:double1

    Scale of the shape along its own y axis, applied before rotation; 1 when absent. Must not be 0.

    scaleZxs:double1

    Scale of the shape along its own z axis, applied before rotation; 1 when absent. Must not be 0.

    meshxs:IDREF

    Id of the mesh asset used as the shape; required when shape is mesh and not allowed otherwise.

    from attribute group pyroCrater
    craterxs:IDREF

    Id of a crater whose impact causes this source or impulse; the smoke then comes from the impact, so the shape, timing, density, temperature and expansion attributes are not used. Velocity attributes still apply.

    heatFractionnonNegativeDecimal ≤ 1

    Share (0 to 1) of the impact's shock energy that heats the dust; 0.1 when absent. Only allowed with crater.

    dustFractionpositiveDecimal ≤ 1

    Share (above 0, at most 1) of the crater's ejected volume that becomes dust in the smoke; 0.01 when absent. Only allowed with crater.

    specificHeatpositiveDecimal

    Specific heat of the dust in joules per kilogram kelvin, which sets how far the impact heat warms it; 1000 when absent. Only allowed with crater.

    maxTemperaturepositiveDecimal ≤ 50000

    Highest temperature rise of the dust in kelvin; 5000 when absent. Only allowed with crater.

    PYRO5a mesh pyro source must name a mesh asset; @mesh is valid only for shape=mesh.test: (@shape='mesh' and /scene/assets/mesh[@id=current()/@mesh]) or (not(@shape='mesh') and not(@mesh))
    PYRO6pyro shape and mesh selection are static; animate the source transform instead.test: not(*[@property='shape' or @property='mesh'])
    PYRO2pyro source end must be greater than start.test: not(@end) or (@start and number(substring(normalize-space(@end),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(@end),'+'))))>number(substring(normalize-space(@start),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(@start),'+'))))) or (not(@start) and number(substring(normalize-space(@end),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(@end),'+'))))>0)
    PYRO3box pyro sources require width, height and depth.test: not(@shape='box') or (@width and @height and @depth)
    PYRO4pyro source transforms must be invertible (nonzero scales).test: not(number(substring(normalize-space(@scaleX),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(@scaleX),'+'))))=0 or number(substring(normalize-space(@scaleY),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(@scaleY),'+'))))=0 or number(substring(normalize-space(@scaleZ),1+number(starts-with(normalize-space(@scaleZ),'+'))))=0)
    PYC1a pyro source or impulse from a crater derives its shape, place, timing, density, temperature and expansion, so none of them may be given.test: not(@crater and (@start or @end or @time or @densityRate or @temperatureRate or @density or @temperature or @expansion or @shape or @mesh or @radius or @width or @height or @depth or @x or @y or @z or @rotation or @rotationX or @rotationY or @scaleX or @scaleY or @scaleZ))
    PYC2heatFraction, dustFraction, specificHeat and maxTemperature belong to a pyro source or impulse from a crater.test: @crater or not(@heatFraction or @dustFraction or @specificHeat or @maxTemperature)
    PYC3a pyro impulse requires time unless it comes from a crater.test: not(self::pyroImpulse) or @time or @crater
    PYC4a pyro source or impulse from a crater must name a crater that grows from an impact.test: not(@crater) or /scene//crater[@id=current()/@crater and @source]
