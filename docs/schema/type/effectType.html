    effectType

    Type of <effect> in effectsType

    One element type for every effect; each type reads only the attributes it documents. Colour-grade family: color-grade (saturation/contrast/ brightness, as 1.0), lift-gamma-gain (lift/gamma/gain "r,g,b"), cdl (slope/offset/power "r,g,b", saturation; ASC CDL), lut (@src .cube/.clf/ .3dl, @space), curves (@curve "x,y x,y ..." per @channel), levels, white-balance (temperature/tint), exposure, hue-saturation, tonemap. shader: GLSL fragment at @src with param children as uniforms. It runs on a tile: the node's pixels, for a layer or shape without children, with a margin of 2 px plus the outward rounding of its box to whole pixels (up to 64 px past the frame); for any other node, the frame. sr_ContentRect (vec4: left, top, width, height of the node's content box in tile pixels from the tile's top-left, exact) and sr_PixelCoord (vec2: the fragment's centre relative to that corner) locate the content within it (SREP 72); the sr_ prefix is the engine's. selective-color: @hue (degrees) or @color (its hue) centres a window of width @tolerance; inside it @saturation scales and @brightness adds; @amount mixes the result with the original. @channel is not read. halftone: @size is the cell, @angle the screen's angle (default 0), @color the ink (default black) and @paint the ground between the dots (default white).

    Which effect this is (required); it selects the effect and decides which of the other attributes are read.

    enabledxs:booleantrue

    When false the effect is skipped and the picture passes through unchanged (default true).

    mixunitDecimal1

    Blend between the unaffected picture (0) and the full effect result (1), a linear mix (default 1); echo, posterize-time and pixel-motion-blur do not apply it.

    intensityxs:double1

    Strength of the effect (default 1): brightness of the added glow for glow, bloom and halation, opacity of shadows, strength of flares, leaks and sweeps; for echo, the decay between copies.

    radiuseffectRadiusType4

    Size in node-local pixels (default 4): Gaussian blur radius for blur, glow and shadows; half-length of directional blur; reach of twirl, spherize and bulge (4 or less means half the shorter side); vignette clear radius.

    thresholdxs:double0.7

    Luminance level, default 0.7: glow, bloom, halation and god rays pass highlights above it (with a soft knee near it), threshold splits black from white at it, luma-key keys around it.

    saturationxs:double1

    Colour saturation multiplier (default 1): 1 leaves colours as they are, 0 removes colour. Also read by cdl, and by selective-color inside its hue window; in noise, 0 makes the noise monochrome.

    contrastxs:double1

    color-grade only: contrast multiplier about mid-grey on display-encoded values (default 1, unchanged).

    brightnessxs:double0

    Brightness added to display-encoded values (default 0): in color-grade to the picture, in hue-saturation to the value channel, in selective-color inside the hue window.

    colorcolorType

    Colour the effect draws with; each type has its own default when absent (black for shadows, vignette and letterbox, white for glow and strokes, orange for halation and light leak). Halftone ink; selective-color hue centre.

    paintpaintType

    Second paint of the effect: the gradient for gradient-map and gradient-overlay, and for halftone the ground between the dots (default opaque white).

    offsetXeffectOffsetType8

    Horizontal offset in node-local pixels (default 8): where a drop shadow or inner shadow is displaced to, and the horizontal channel offset of rgb-split.

    offsetYeffectOffsetType8

    Vertical offset in node-local pixels (default 8): where a drop shadow or inner shadow is displaced to, and the vertical channel offset of rgb-split.

    lightsxs:IDREFS

    Ids of lights whose positions and intensities light the picture in a lighting effect (up to four are used).

    falloffone of linear quadratic smooth nonesmooth
    reliefnonNegativeDecimal0

    Depth of the surface relief (default 0): in emboss the sampling distance in pixels (at least 1), in lighting the height scale of the bump (at least 0.2).

    anglexs:double0

    Direction or rotation in degrees (default 0): of directional-blur, long-shadow and wave-warp; light direction of emboss, bevel and lighting (bevel and lighting take 0 as -45); halftone, mirror, gradient, twirl.

    amountxs:double1

    Main strength of the effect (default 1): a displacement in pixels for displacement, wave, ripple and chromatic-aberration effects; a 0-1 mix for tint, grayscale, sepia, invert and overlays; for matte-choke, positive erodes and negative dilates.

    sizenonNegativeDecimal1

    Meaning depends on the type: cell size in pixels for halftone, pixelate and mosaic; line width for stroke and outline; wavelength for wave-warp and ripple; length for long-shadow; aspect ratio for letterbox.

    frequencyxs:double1

    Rate or scale (default 1): steps per second for posterize-time, wave cycles per second (times speed) for wave-warp, spatial frequency multiplier for fractal-noise.

    speedxs:double1

    Rate (default 1) at which time-varying effects advance: it multiplies elapsed seconds for fractal-noise, heat-haze, light-leak, light-sweep, ripple, turbulent-displace and wave-warp.

    seedxs:unsignedLong

    Random seed for effects that draw noise, such as film-grain, fractal-noise, glitch, light-leak and turbulent-displace; the pattern also depends on the project seed and the effect's id.

    centerXxs:double

    Horizontal centre of the effect in node-local pixels; when absent the node's centre is used (lens-flare puts its source near the node's top-left instead). For radial, zoom, twirl, bulge, vignette and similar.

    centerYxs:double

    Vertical centre of the effect in node-local pixels; when absent the node's centre is used (lens-flare puts its source near the node's top-left instead). For radial, zoom, twirl, bulge, vignette and similar.

    samplesxs:positiveInteger16

    Number of samples (default 16): taps for directional, radial and zoom blur and god rays, copies for echo (2 to 16), samples for pixel-motion-blur; lens blur and tilt-shift derive their rings from it.

    liftxs:string

    lift-gamma-gain: black-level lift, as one number or three comma-separated r,g,b values, applied to display-encoded values (default 0 for each channel).

    gammaxs:string

    Mid-tone gamma as one number or r,g,b values (default 1; higher brightens). Read by lift-gamma-gain, and by levels (first value only).

    gainxs:string

    lift-gamma-gain: highlight multiplier, as one number or three comma-separated r,g,b values, applied to display-encoded values (default 1 for each channel).

    slopexs:string

    cdl: ASC CDL slope, a multiplier on display-encoded values given as one number or three r,g,b values (default 1).

    offsetxs:string

    cdl: ASC CDL offset added to display-encoded values, given as one number or three r,g,b values (default 0).

    powerxs:string

    cdl: ASC CDL power (exponent) applied to display-encoded values, given as one number or three r,g,b values (default 1).

    temperaturexs:double0

    white-balance: positive values warm the picture (more red, less blue), negative cool it; the scale is about -100 to 100 (default 0, neutral).

    tintxs:double0

    white-balance: shifts between green and magenta on a scale of about -100 to 100; positive values reduce green (toward magenta), 0 is neutral (default).

    exposurexs:double0

    Exposure change in stops (default 0): the picture is multiplied by 2 to the power of the value, in exposure and before the tone mapper in tonemap.

    huexs:double0

    Hue in degrees (default 0): hue-saturation rotates all hues by it; selective-color centres its hue window on it unless color is set.

    channelone of rgb red green blue alpha lumargb

    Channel an effect works on (default rgb): curves and levels adjust red, green, blue or all; curves and invert also take luma; alpha acts as rgb in curves, levels and invert; noise is monochrome unless rgb.

    curvexs:string

    curves: control points as space-separated x,y pairs (input and output in 0-1 on display-encoded values), at least two, joined by a smooth spline.

    inputBlackxs:double0

    levels: input value (0-1 on display-encoded values) mapped to black; values below it clip (default 0).

    inputWhitexs:double1

    levels: input value (0-1 on display-encoded values) mapped to white; values above it clip (default 1).

    outputBlackxs:double0

    levels: output value that black input maps to (default 0), which lifts the blacks.

    outputWhitexs:double1

    levels: output value that white input maps to (default 1), which lowers the whites.

    levelsxs:positiveInteger8

    Count of discrete steps or sides (default 8): colour levels per channel for posterize (at least 2), blade count of the lens-blur and tilt-shift aperture (below 3 is round), segments for kaleidoscope.

    keyColorcolorType

    Colour to remove for chroma-key, or whose spill is suppressed in spill-suppress (default green).

    toleranceunitDecimal0.2

    0-1 (default 0.2): chroma-key and difference-key make pixels closer than this distance to the key transparent; in selective-color, the width of the hue window as a fraction of the hue circle.

    softnessunitDecimal0.1

    0-1 (default 0.1): soft edge width: the ramp beyond the tolerance in chroma-key and difference-key, around the threshold in luma-key, the vignette falloff; matte-choke blurs its edge only from about 0.25 (at full size).

    spillunitDecimal0.5

    chroma-key and spill-suppress: how strongly the key colour's spill is removed from the remaining pixels, 0 to 1 (default 0.5).

    sourcexs:IDREF

    Second input: displacement map, gradient-map paint source, difference-key plate.

    srcxs:anyURI

    File an effect loads: the .cube, .clf or .3dl look-up table for lut, or the GLSL fragment shader for shader; both types need it (lut without it is an error, shader passes the picture through).

    spacecolorSpaceType

    Colour space of the file data: for lut, the space the table expects and returns (default the working space); for shader, the space the shader receives and returns pixels in (default sRGB).

    tonemapperone of aces agx filmic reinhard hable pq-to-sdraces

    tonemap: which curve compresses high dynamic range to display range: aces (default), agx, filmic, reinhard, hable or pq-to-sdr.

    positionone of outside inside centeroutside

    Where the line sits relative to the shape's edge: outside (default), inside or center. Read by stroke and outline.

    compositeOriginalone of behind on-top nonebehind

    drop-shadow only: behind (default) draws the shadow under the content, on-top over it, none shows the shadow alone.

    kernelone of floyd-steinberg atkinson jarvis-judice-ninke stucki burkes sierrafloyd-steinberg

    error-diffusion: dithering kernel (floyd-steinberg default, atkinson, jarvis-judice-ninke, stucki, burkes, sierra) that spreads each pixel's quantisation error to its neighbours.

    palettexs:string

    error-diffusion: space-separated #RRGGBB[AA] colours the picture is reduced to (default black and white); any AA part is ignored and the picture's own alpha is kept.

    serpentinexs:booleanfalse

    error-diffusion: when true, odd rows are scanned right to left and the kernel is mirrored (default false, every row left to right).

    directionone of horizontal verticalhorizontal

    segmented-sort: horizontal (default) sorts each row, vertical sorts each column.

    orderone of ascending descendingascending

    segmented-sort: ascending (default) or descending key order within each segment.

    sortKeyone of luma red green blueluma

    segmented-sort: the value pixels are sorted by: luma (default), red, green or blue.

    lowunitDecimal0

    segmented-sort: lower bound (0-1, default 0) of the sort key; a segment is a run of pixels whose key lies between low and high, and pixels outside stay in place.

    highunitDecimal1

    segmented-sort: upper bound (0-1, default 1) of the sort key; a segment is a run of pixels whose key lies between low and high. Must be at least low.

    stepsPerFramexs:positiveInteger ≤ 100001

    type="shader" with PERSISTENT passes: steps (evaluations of all passes) per frame. SREP 68.

    prewarmxs:nonNegativeInteger ≤ 100000000

    Steps run before the node's first active frame, with the inputs of that frame. SREP 68.
