    clayBlobType

    Type of <blob> in object3DType

    One solid of a clay object (primitive="clay"), in the object's local scene units: its signed distance field joins the others with a smooth union of width blend (or is carved out with subtract). Blobs animate like any element, so topology can change over time.

    Horizontal position of the blob's centre, in the clay object's local scene units (default 0).

    Depth position of the blob's centre, in the clay object's local scene units (default 0).

    radiuspositiveDecimal30

    Radius in local scene units (default 30): the sphere's radius, the capsule's cap and body radius, or the torus's ring radius. Not used by box.

    widthpositiveDecimal60

    Box: size along local x (default 60). Torus: diameter of the tube (default 60). Not used by sphere or capsule.

    heightpositiveDecimal60

    Box size along local y, in local scene units (default 60). Not used by the other shapes.

    depthpositiveDecimal60

    Box size along local z, in local scene units (default 60). Not used by the other shapes.

    lengthnonNegativeDecimal60

    Capsule only: distance between the two cap centres along the blob's local y axis, in local scene units (default 60).

    rotationXxs:double0

    Rotation of the blob about its x axis, in degrees (default 0); combined with rotationY and rotation.

    rotationYxs:double0

    Rotation of the blob about its y axis, in degrees (default 0); combined with rotationX and rotation.

    rotationxs:double0

    Rotation of the blob about its z axis, in degrees (default 0); combined with rotationX and rotationY.

    blendnonNegativeDecimal10

    Width of the smooth join with the blobs before it, in local scene units (default 10); 0 gives a hard union. With subtract it is the width of the carved edge.

    subtractxs:booleanfalse

    When true, carves this blob out of the blobs before it instead of adding to them (default false); on the first blob it has no effect.
