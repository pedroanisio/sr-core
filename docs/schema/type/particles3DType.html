    gasxs:IDREF

    The object3D whose native pyro volume drags the particles along. Absent (the default) is the behaviour there always was. The acceleration of a particle gains drag times the gas's velocity at its place and time, so with the emitter's drag k the particle obeys p'' = k (u - v) plus gravity and fields: it is dragged by the gas inside the volume's domain and by still air outside it, with the same coefficient (the gas fades to rest across one cell outside the domain, so there is no jump at its boundary); a drag of zero gives no coupling. The place is taken to the volume's axes by the inverse of its world matrix at that time and the velocity back by its linear part. In time the gas is the linear interpolation of the two smoke steps around the instant; the particles run before the smoke in the frame and ask it, in order, for the steps they need, so that it is simulated once for each of its steps, and a smoke that fails fails the particles with its message. The velocity fields of the steps in use are charged to the volume's maxMemoryMiB and exceeding it is an error. The particles do not push the gas, and the coupling is linear in the relative velocity: for 0.34 m rocks of 2100 kg/m3 in air at 50 m/s the quadratic drag is a rate of about 0.03 per second, so it moves dust-sized particles and not ejecta. The volume must be on the composition clock.
