    constraintType

    Type of <constraint> in physicsType

    spring/distance/rope/hinge/slider/weld/motor join bodies a and b; pin ties body a to the world point x, y (b is not allowed) and is rigid unless stiffness is given. breakForce removes the constraint when exceeded. Between 3D bodies (object3D rigidBody) the anchor is x, y, z; hinge, motor and slider turn about or slide along axisX, axisY, axisZ (scene axes; z by default for hinges and motors, x for sliders); ball is a ball-and-socket joint.

    id requiredxs:ID
    type requiredone of spring distance pin rope hinge slider weld motor ball

    Kind of joint: spring, distance, rope, hinge, slider, weld, motor, ball or pin. Pin ties body a to a world point; ball is a ball-and-socket joint between 3D bodies.

    a requiredxs:IDREF

    Id of the first body joined by the constraint (required).

    bxs:IDREF

    Id of the second body joined; required for every type except pin, and not allowed on a pin, which ties body a to the world point given by x and y.

    xxs:double

    Horizontal world position in pixels of the joint anchor at the start; used only when y is also given, otherwise the anchor is the second body's centre, or the first body's for a pin.

    yxs:double

    Vertical world position in pixels of the joint anchor at the start; used only when x is also given, otherwise the anchor is the second body's centre, or the first body's for a pin.

    zxs:double

    Depth of the anchor in pixels between 3D bodies, used when x and y are given; 0 when absent.

    axisXxs:double

    X component of the axis a 3D hinge or motor turns about, or a slider moves along; when none of the three axis components is given, hinges and motors use z and sliders use x.

    axisYxs:double

    Y component of the axis a 3D hinge or motor turns about, or a slider moves along; when none of the three axis components is given, hinges and motors use z and sliders use x.

    axisZxs:double

    Z component of the axis a 3D hinge or motor turns about, or a slider moves along; when none of the three axis components is given, hinges and motors use z and sliders use x.

    restLengthxs:double

    Rest length in pixels of a spring, distance or rope constraint; absent means the distance between the two bodies at the start.

    stiffnessxs:double

    Stiffness of a spring, distance or pin constraint; absent gives mass-scaled defaults for springs and distance joints, and a rigid pin.

    dampingxs:double

    Damping of a spring or distance constraint, or of a pin that has a stiffness; absent gives a mass-scaled default. A pin without stiffness is rigid and ignores it.

    minAnglexs:double

    Lower limit of a hinge or motor in degrees, or of travel along a slider in pixels; applies only when maxAngle is also given.

    maxAnglexs:double

    Upper limit of a hinge or motor in degrees, or of travel along a slider in pixels; applies only when minAngle is also given.

    axisAnglexs:double0

    Direction in degrees of a slider's travel axis in the 2D plane; defaults to 0.

    motorSpeedxs:double0

    Target rotation speed of a motor constraint in degrees per second; defaults to 0.
