    pyroCrater

    A source or impulse that the impact of a crater causes. crater names a crater that grows from an impact (crater@source); the smoke is derived from that impact and nothing is authored about when, where or how much: the source is a sphere of the crater's radius at the impact point, active from the impact over the crater's formation time (an impulse: at the impact), so shape, position, timing, density, temperature and expansion are not given. The dust is dustFraction of the volume thrown out of the crater, injected as a volume fraction of solids, spread over the cells the sphere covers that are not solid (so medium@extinction is extinction per unit volume fraction in these sources). The heat is heatFraction of (1/2) m U^2 sin(theta)^(3/2) for the body's mass m, speed U and angle theta from the surface; it warms the dust by heat / (dust mass x specificHeat, J/(kg K)), at most maxTemperature kelvin, and the expansion follows from the heating as an ideal gas at constant pressure. The literature gives no value for the share of the energy that goes into a plume: the defaults are the engine's, with no published value. A body that arrives slowly, in physical units, heats its dust very little. All quantities are in SI through physics/@pixelsPerMeter.
