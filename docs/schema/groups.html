    pyroCrater

    A source or impulse that the impact of a crater causes. crater names a crater that grows from an impact (crater@source); the smoke is derived from that impact and nothing is authored about when, where or how much: the source is a sphere of the crater's radius at the impact point, active from the impact over the crater's formation time (an impulse: at the impact), so shape, position, timing, density, temperature and expansion are not given. The dust is dustFraction of the volume thrown out of the crater, injected as a volume fraction of solids, spread over the cells the sphere covers that are not solid (so medium@extinction is extinction per unit volume fraction in these sources). The heat is heatFraction of (1/2) m U^2 sin(theta)^(3/2) for the body's mass m, speed U and angle theta from the surface; it warms the dust by heat / (dust mass x specificHeat, J/(kg K)), at most maxTemperature kelvin, and the expansion follows from the heating as an ideal gas at constant pressure. The literature gives no value for the share of the energy that goes into a plume: the defaults are the engine's, with no published value. A body that arrives slowly, in physical units, heats its dust very little. All quantities are in SI through physics/@pixelsPerMeter. velocityX, velocityY and velocityZ of an impulse and velocityRateX, velocityRateY and velocityRateZ of a source stay authorable with crater: the push is added to the cloud of the impact (in the volume's axes) and is not derived from it.

    AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
    declared here
    craterxs:IDREF

    Id of a crater whose impact causes this source or impulse; the smoke then comes from the impact, so the shape, timing, density, temperature and expansion attributes are not used. Velocity attributes still apply.

    heatFractionnonNegativeDecimal ≤ 1

    Share (0 to 1) of the impact's shock energy that heats the dust; 0.1 when absent. Only allowed with crater.

    dustFractionpositiveDecimal ≤ 1

    Share (above 0, at most 1) of the crater's ejected volume that becomes dust in the smoke; 0.01 when absent. Only allowed with crater.

    specificHeatpositiveDecimal

    Specific heat of the dust in joules per kilogram kelvin, which sets how far the impact heat warms it; 1000 when absent. Only allowed with crater.

    maxTemperaturepositiveDecimal ≤ 50000

    Highest temperature rise of the dust in kelvin; 5000 when absent. Only allowed with crater.
